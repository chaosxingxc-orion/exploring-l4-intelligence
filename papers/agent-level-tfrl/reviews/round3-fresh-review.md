> Multi-round rigorous review, round 1. 5 fresh hostile reviewers attacked the paper from scratch; area-chair triaged. Run wf_ad0f7863-32d, 2026-07-01.


## Chair verdict: major revision (17 critical/major + 8 minor)


All five reviewers converge on major revision; deduped, the paper has 6 critical and 11 major issues. The theory core (§5) and Lean appendix are unusually honest and verified correct, but three structural failures survive the hedging: (1) the titled "recovery" thesis is not what the theorems prove — OSA-2/3b prove additivity+factorization under ASSUMED separability, recovery rests entirely on Conjecture 1, which the paper's own speaker(~0.04, near-chance at every layer)/emotion(~0.36, flat) probes contradict for the exact paralinguistic factors the flagship targets; (2) the speech instantiation's "two-omni" novelty collapses because §8 decouples all paralinguistic retrieval to ECAPA/SER, so the omni memory does no load-bearing work on the paralinguistic-keyed benchmark, and there is no trivial ECAPA+SER+dict baseline to make OSA-2 recovery falsifiable; (3) provenance is broken — the headline +0.330 SLURP gain is baseline-contradicted by the project's own docs/theory.md (0.522→0.894/+0.372 with a 0.894→0.880 regression), no machine-readable artifact backs any table, and a dev/test selection leak may invalidate the +0.097 emotion anchor. Internal contradictions (Assumption 1 "independent of g_θ" vs Def 6 g_θ-dependent rewards), a range-not-variance OSA-1 bound that is vacuous at β→0 and =1/(8β) for [0,1] rewards, Lean quantitative "theorems" that are trivial wrappers (kl_best_of_n_le := hBeirami), single-seed/no-multiplicity statistics that fail the paper's own §9 protocol, and load-bearing author-less unrefereed preprints (skillsbench2026 +16.2pp, jitrl2026) round out the substance-fix agenda. This is a fixable proposal, not a reject: the qualitative theory is sound and the self-criticism is genuine, but the title, conclusion, novelty, and headline empirics all overclaim relative to what is established.

**[CRITICAL] NO-TRIVIAL-BASELINE** (re-run-experiment) @ §9 sub:phase2 (compounding contrast); §9 sub:phase1 'Evaluation design'

**[MAJOR] OSA1-RANGE-NOT-VARIANCE** (fix-in-lean) @ §5.2 Thm thm:hoeffding + Assumption ass:hoeffding; Abstract/§1 'realized reward spread'
  
  The OSA-1 ceiling is governed by the reward RANGE (max−min over Z), not the concentration of q0 that actually produces flat conditioning: because q0>0 strictly on finite Z, 'realized spread on supp(q0)' (abstract/intro/§5.2) equals the global range, blind to whether q0's mass sits on a flat band. The empirical mechanism is concentration (large range, small variance), where gain≈Var/(2β) ≪ spread²/(8β). The bound blows up as β→0 (the strong-selection regime the paper says 'recovers best-of-N') and collapses to the uninformative gain≤1/(8β) for R∈[0,1] (Assumption ass:verifiable).
  
  *Fix:* Add a concentration-aware bound (Bernstein/Bennett, gain ≤ Var_{q0}(R)/(2β)+higher-order), ideally formalized in Lean alongside the Hoeffding one, and present the ceiling as min(spread, spread²/(8β)) to cover small β. State that under q0>0 on finite Z 'realized spread' = global range and stop calling it 'realized'; for the generative operator (R∈[0,1]) note the bound reduces to 1/(8β) and supply the missing argument that the effective sampled-support range is small.

## Individual fresh reviews


### Theory skeptic (optimization / information theory) — attacking §5 (OSA), §6 (Convergence), and the appendix (Lean formalization + extended proofs).  (major revision)

The "qualitative core" of §5 is correct but almost entirely standard (KL-regularized control = Gibbs posterior; gain = β·KL(q0‖q*); Hoeffding/CGF bound; independence factorization), so the advertised "load-bearing theoretical contribution" is thin. More seriously, the prose systematically claims more than the theorems prove. (1) The central "agentic recovery" story is not in the math: OSA-3b (qstar_product) shows the context-isolated optimum is EXACTLY the monolithic optimum on the product space, so context isolation recovers nothing over just running best-of-N on the joint reward; all "recovery" lives in the *assumed* addition of non-degenerate per-block rewards (Conjecture 1), which their own §7 embedding data (speaker/emotion flat at every layer) actively contradicts for the flagship's target factors. OSA-2's "strictly increasing in k" is a tautology compatible with k near-zero gains summing to near-zero — i.e., quantitatively vacuous. (2) The OSA-1 bound is range-based (spread = global max−min, and since q0>0 everywhere on a finite Z, "realized spread" = full range), so it is insensitive to the *concentration* of q0 that actually produces flat conditioning, blows up as β→0 (precisely the strong-selection regime), and collapses to the uninformative gain≤1/(8β) for [0,1] rewards. (3) The "single-model vs agent" dichotomy that titles the paper is absent from the theorems — OSA-1 bounds any flat-reward space, agent or not — and is contradicted by the large single-model Op-B gains in §7. (4) The Lean "machine-checked" framing, while heavily hedged, oversells: the three quantitative "Lean theorems" are trivial wrappers (kl_best_of_n_le is literally the identity `:= hBeirami`; gain_le_of_hoeffding and regret_O_sqrt_log are one-line substitution/sqrt-monotonicity over assumed hypotheses), so no quantitative bound is actually verified in Lean, and the strict/uniqueness claims the prose relies on are not in the printed sorry-free lemmas. Recommendation: major revision.

- **[critical]** (§5.3 Theorem thm:gainprod (gain_product) + §5.4 Theorem thm:qstarprod (qstar_product); Conjecture conj:spreadfloor; contradicted by §7 Tab tab:cremad-matrix/tab:opA) OSA-2/OSA-3b prove a factorization, not a 'recovery': context isolation yields the SAME optimum as monolithic tilting on the product reward, so the theorems give no escape from the single-model ceiling — the entire 'recovery' rests on the unproven Conjecture 1, which the paper's own evidence undercuts. qstar_product (Thm thm:qstarprod / Lean qstar_product) states q*(z1,z2)=q*_1(z1)q*_2(z2), i.e. the decomposed optimum is identical to the Gibbs optimum of the joint product reward R=R1+R2 over Z1×Z2. Hence decomposition buys nothing in attainable gain relative to best-of-N on the same joint reward; it is a computational factorization, not an enlargement of optimization space. The only thing that raises gain is replacing a near-flat joint R by a sum of non-flat R_i — i.e. *adding new reward dimensions* — which is assumed (Conjecture conj:spreadfloor), not derived, and has nothing to do with 'context isolation', 'agents', or 'frozen models'. Worse, §7 (Tab tab:cremad-matrix, tab:opA) shows the per-block embedding rewards for speaker (~0.04, near-chance at every one of 37 layers) and emotion (barely moves) are themselves flat — direct evidence AGAINST the per-block spread floor for exactly the disentanglement factors the flagship targets.  
  *Fix:* Demote OSA-2 from 'recovery theorem' to what it is: 'gain is additive and the optimum factorizes under an assumed product/separable structure.' State explicitly that qstar_product implies decomposition attains the *same* gain as monolithic tilting on R=ΣR_i, so the only source of additional gain is the introduction of new non-degenerate rewards (Conjecture 1), independent of agency. - **[major]** (§5.2 Theorem thm:hoeffding + Assumption ass:hoeffding; Appendix Lemma lem:hoeffding; framing in Abstract and §1 ('realized reward spread')) The OSA-1 ceiling is governed by the reward *range* (max−min over Z), not by the concentration of q0, so it cannot quantitatively explain 'flat conditioning' — the very phenomenon it is built to characterize. The empirical mechanism in §7 is concentration (q0 piles on near-identical-reward outputs while high-reward options retain tiny probability), i.e. large range / small variance — precisely the regime where gain≈Var/(2β) ≪ spread^2/(8β) and the bound is loose by a factor ~spread^2/(4Var). ### Statistics-of-small-N hawk (attacking §7 preliminary results and §9 evaluation/plan)  (major revision)

Every preliminary number in §7 is a single-seed (seed 42) point estimate whose only uncertainty quantification is a bootstrap over test items — which captures test-set sampling variance but NOT the variance that actually matters here (re-drawing best-of-N candidates, re-running the stochastic agent, re-selecting configs from sweeps). The §9 evaluation protocol is exemplary on paper (≥5 seeds, paired-delta bootstrap, power/MDE, FWER, dev-only selection, contamination screening of every public number), but NONE of these standards is met by the actual preliminary results the paper leans on — the protocol is a promissory note, and the numbers in the contributions/abstract are presented as stronger than the evidence the paper itself describes. The most load-bearing claim of the whole proposal — that "high-spread action sets demonstrably exist" within a single frozen model (the §7 Operator-B gains of +0.330/+0.335 on SLURP/URO) — sits entirely on public benchmarks the paper concedes may be pretraining-contaminated, with the contamination screen deferred to future Phase 0. The Operator-A +0.097 emotion gain that anchors contribution C3 is, by the authors' own admission, an argmax over 74 configurations with no multiplicity correction, overlapping CIs, a single seed, and no paired test — i.e. it is consistent with pure winner's curse. The "consistent with / bounded by spread²/(8β)" framing is unfalsifiable because spread and β are never numerically evaluated. The paper is unusually candid about each caveat in isolation, but the cumulative effect is that not one number in §7 currently meets the bar §9 sets, and several are unreproducible as written (unexplained denominators, missing CIs, ambiguous dev/test selection).

- **[major]** (§7.4 (07-feasibility.tex, Tables tab:cremad-matrix, tab:opA, tab:opB) vs §9 sub:protocol point (4)) All preliminary results (Tables 1-3, §7.4) are single-seed (seed 42), and the reported bootstrap CIs are over test items only, not over the generation/sampling process. For Operator-B best-of-N (Table 3), candidate generation is stochastic; seed 42 fixes ONE realization of the N-candidate set per item, so the bootstrap CI systematically understates uncertainty — it does not capture re-drawing candidates. This directly violates the paper's own protocol (§9 sub:protocol point 4: '>=5 seeds with across-seed confidence intervals').  
  *Fix:* Re-run every preliminary number over >=5 independent generation seeds; report across-seed mean and CI in addition to the item-bootstrap CI, and a variance-components breakdown (item variance vs seed variance). Remove or down-rank any number whose across-seed CI crosses zero.
- **[critical]** (§7.4 tab:opB and 'Reading the evidence'; deferred to §9 sub:phase0 contamination paragraph) The Operator-B gains on SLURP/MInDS-14/URO (Table 3: +0.330, +0.089/+0.132, +0.335) are the single load-bearing empirical claim of the proposal — they are the sole evidence that 'high-spread action sets demonstrably exist' and thus justify the entire agent thesis.   *Fix:* Run the §9 contamination screen (train-cutoff, n-gram/transcript overlap, canary memorization) BEFORE quoting any Op-B number; or replace with a freshly collected/private spoken-intent and spoken-QA set. Until then, label the +0.330/+0.335 gains as 'wiring checks, contamination-unverified' and do not cite them as evidence of reachable spread in the abstract/intro.

- **[critical]** (§7.4 paragraph 'Operator-A readout selection...' and tab:opA (results on CREMA-D TEST)) Selection vs evaluation split for the Op-A argmax is ambiguous. §7.4 states 'CREMA-D dev600/test300' but the L16+attentive-stats configuration is described as 'the argmax over a 37-layer x 2-pooling sweep' and the +0.097 is reported on TEST. The text never says the argmax (L16, attentive-stats) was selected on dev. ### Speech/audio evaluation skeptic — attacking §7–§9 speech specifics and the proposed cross-session paralinguistic benchmark  (major revision)

The proposal is unusually self-aware: it pre-empts the obvious speech-eval objections (read-not-listen, channel-matched re-keying, SER-as-reward circularity, dual-use keys, contamination). But the hedging does not save the speech core, and in two places the hedging actively contradicts the headline contribution. (1) The central novelty — a "two-omni pairing" of a frozen vector-omni memory with a generative-omni policy — is gutted by the paper's own §7.5 finding that the omni content embedding is at chance on speaker (~0.04) and weak on emotion (~0.36). §8 therefore decouples paralinguistic retrieval onto dedicated ECAPA-TDNN/x-vector/SER heads, which means the omni embedding does NO load-bearing work for the flagship cross-session paralinguistic benchmark (whose queries are explicitly speaker/SER-keyed, with content-answerable queries removed by the listen-not-read control). The system reduces to a classic ECAPA+SER+knowledge-graph pipeline, and the paper never includes that trivial non-omni, non-agent baseline — without which the OSA-2 "agentic recovery" claim on the benchmark is unfalsifiable. (2) SID is declared a verifiable reward, but in real multi-party cross-session audio it inherits diarization error (DER); the paper acknowledges the parallel SER circularity but not the SID one. (3) The "high reward spread" evidence that motivates the whole move to the agent action space (Op-B +0.33 on SLURP/URO/MInDS) is entirely on intent/content — the "read" channel — while the paralinguistic "listen" channel shows only a fragile, single-seed, winner's-cursed +0.097; the evidence is from the wrong modality for a paralinguistic-memory thesis. Add acted-CREMA-D + high-IAA SER subset selection biasing the benchmark toward easy categorical affect, a gold-transcript (not ASR-transcript) listen-vs-read control, underpowered speaker numbers used for a load-bearing design decision, and an unspecified DER/SER-error disqualification threshold, and the speech instantiation is considerably weaker than the theory section. Major revision.

- **[critical]** (§7.3 (sec:feas:novelty) vs §8 subsec:memory 'Decoupled retrieval keys' and §7.5 Table tab:cremad-matrix; §9 sub:phase1 'Minimal spec' (ii)) The omni embedding does no load-bearing paralinguistic work, contradicting the 'two-omni pairing' novelty. §7.3 (Novelty Delta) and §1 sell the unfilled slot as a 'frozen omni memory (Operator-A) + frozen omni policy (Operator-B)'. The flagship benchmark (§9, Phase 1) is by construction speaker-ID-keyed and SER-keyed, with content-answerable queries removed by the listen-not-read control. Therefore, on the benchmark that defines the contribution, the omni 'vector-omni memory' is never queried — the paralinguistic memory is carried entirely by off-the-shelf, non-omni speaker/SER models.  
  *Fix:* Either (a) demonstrate empirically that the omni embedding contributes paralinguistic retrieval signal beyond what ECAPA/SER already provide (e.g., joint retrieval ablation showing omni adds AUC on speaker/affect linking), and reframe the novelty around that, or (b) drop the 'two-omni memory' framing for paralinguistics and honestly position the system as an omni-policy + classic-speech-encoder memory, which is a much narrower claim. As written the novelty and the design are mutually inconsistent.

The plan's only baseline (§9 Phase 2) is 'best-of-N with empty non-persistent memory', which isolates test-time compute but not the question of whether ANY agentic/omni machinery beats a 50-line speaker-verification + dictionary script.  
  *Fix:* Add the ECAPA(verification) + SER(classifier) + speaker-keyed dictionary pipeline as the PRIMARY baseline the agent must beat, with paired-bootstrap CIs. Pre-register that if the agent does not significantly exceed it, the agentic-recovery claim is not supported. - **[critical]** (§8 subsec:arch 'Which rewards are verifiable'; §9 sub:phase1 'Minimal spec' (iii) and 'Listen-vs-read control' (frozen diarization); contrast with §8 subsec:memory SER-surrogate paragraph) SID is declared a 'verifiable' reward but is not verifiable in real multi-party cross-session audio. §8 'Which rewards are verifiable' and §9 Phase-1 (iii) treat speaker-ID accuracy as a clean verifiable reward 'on corpora that carry ground-truth speaker labels'. Table tab:cremad-matrix reports speaker ~0.04 at n=300 on a 91-way task (chance 0.011); the paper itself calls this 'under-powered' and 'near chance rather than exactly chance'. - **[minor]** (§7.5 all three tables; §9 sub:protocol items (4) and (5)) All preliminary numbers are single-seed (42) and single argmax configs, violating the paper's own evaluation protocol. §9 sub:protocol mandates >=5 seeds with across-seed CIs, FWER correction across the sweep, and paired-delta tests.   *Fix:* Re-run the preliminary tables under the §9 protocol (>=5 seeds, across-seed CIs, FWER correction, paired-delta bootstrap on the +0.097 emotion delta) before using them to motivate the design, or downgrade them explicitly to 'illustrative, sub-protocol' and do not draw the high-vs-flat-spread contrast from them.
- **[minor]** (§9 sub:phase1 'Evaluation design' (>=2 backbones)) Two-backbone 'generalization' uses two models from the same family. §9 'Evaluation design' runs every metric across Qwen3-Omni and Qwen2.5-Omni 'to separate benchmark signal from backbone idiosyncrasy'. These share lineage, tokenizer, and likely overlapping pretraining/contamination and the same read-not-listen inductive bias.  
  *Fix:* Include at least one architecturally independent omni/speech-LLM (e.g., SALMONN or a non-Qwen omni) in the backbone sweep, and report contamination screens per backbone since shared-family contamination would not be caught by cross-Qwen comparison.
- **[minor]** (§9 sub:phase1 'Construction and its realism risks' option (b); §9 sub:milestones M2) No named real repeated-caller corpus; the only artifact-free construction path is left as an unresourced assumption. §9 'Construction and its realism risks' offers (a) re-recording personas or (b) 'source a real repeated-caller corpus', but names no such corpus and addresses none of the consent/PII/ethics/availability obstacles of recurring-speaker audio across genuinely distinct sessions.  
  *Fix:* Name a candidate repeated-caller / longitudinal-speaker corpus with licensing and consent status, or scope the benchmark to the re-recording construction with an explicit data-collection protocol (speakers, sessions, IRB/consent, channel diversity) and cost/timeline, so feasibility is established rather than assumed.

### Novelty & reward-hacking red-teamer  (major revision)

This is an unusually self-aware proposal: the authors pre-empt many attacks by hedging OSA-2 as a conjecture, flagging the open "moat," and labeling surrogate rewards Goodhart-prone. But the hedging masks three load-bearing failures that the disclaimers do not actually repair. (1) The OSA theory does NOT support the agent pivot it is used to motivate: OSA-2's "recovery" is the near-tautological fact that log-partition functions of independent blocks add, which sums gain over MORE tasks but does nothing for the per-block flatness that OSA-1 diagnoses — and the paper's own preliminary data (speaker near-chance at every layer; emotion weak/fragile) is direct evidence AGAINST the spread-floor Conjecture 1 for exactly the paralinguistic factors the flagship targets. (2) The "verifiable-reward gate" — billed as the Goodhart antidote — is genuinely verifiable only on content/intent (WER, exact-match), i.e. precisely the factors that already work and were never the obstacle; for the novel cross-session paralinguistic use case no ground-truth exists at deployment, so the load-bearing reward there collapses to the self-consistency surrogate the authors themselves call Goodhart-prone. The gate is robust where it is unneeded and absent where the contribution lives. (3) Assumption 1 (reward "independent of g_θ") is internally contradicted by two of the four "verifiable" rewards it lists (probe accuracy, retrieval hit@k), which are functions of the frozen model's own representations — making the Operator-A reward channel circular and not Goodhart-immune. Secondary issues: title and conclusion overclaim relative to the theory section; the empirical motivation bait-and-switches flat factors (speaker/emotion) for high-spread factors (intent/QA); and the load-bearing acceptance-gate design rests primarily on a single unverified preprint. The honesty is real but does not rescue the core thesis; the novelty net of disclaimers is a domain transfer plus a benchmark proposal.

- **[critical]** (Sec. 5.3 (Theorem thm:gainprod, Conjecture conj:spreadfloor); Sec. 7.4 (Tables tab:cremad-matrix, tab:opA)) OSA-2 'recovery' is a category error and the paper's own data contradicts its enabling conjecture. Theorem 4 (gain_product, Sec. 5.3) proves only that for an ALREADY-product base with an ALREADY-separable, ALREADY-non-degenerate reward, total gain = sum of per-block gains and grows with the number of blocks k. This is the trivial multiplicativity of the partition function over independent factors. Crucially it sums gain over MORE sub-tasks; it does nothing to raise the per-block ceiling spread_i^2/(8β). The OSA-1 obstacle is per-factor flatness (e.g. speaker readout), and context isolation cannot manufacture spread a frozen embedding does not encode. The whole agent pivot therefore rests entirely on Conjecture 1 (spreadfloor, Sec. 5.3), and the paper's OWN preliminary results are evidence AGAINST it for the flagship factors: Table 1/Sec. 7.4 reports speaker accuracy near chance at EVERY one of 37 layers (best 0.043 vs chance 0.011) and emotion only weakly informative (~0.36, fragile +0.097). These are exactly the paralinguistic factors the cross-session memory benchmark (C5) targets.  
  *Fix:* State explicitly that OSA-2 sums gain across decomposed sub-tasks and does NOT raise per-factor headroom, so it cannot escape OSA-1 flatness on any individual factor. Confront Conjecture 1 against the speaker/emotion probe data directly: concede the near-chance speaker readout is prima facie evidence the spread floor fails for paralinguistics, and restrict the recovery claim to factors where the frozen model demonstrably retains spread (content/intent), or reframe the flagship around those. Drop 'recovers' framing where the per-block spread is unestablished.
- **[critical]** (Sec. 8.1 ('Which rewards are verifiable'); Sec. 8.2 ('Composite keys and their reward status'); Sec. 2.4 (Assumption ass:verifiable)) The verifiable-reward gate is structurally absent in the regime that defines the contribution, where it degenerates to a self-consistency surrogate the authors call Goodhart-prone. Sec. 8.1 concedes genuinely verifiable rewards are WER/CER, exact-match intent, and speaker-ID 'only on corpora that carry ground-truth speaker labels.' For a self-evolving agent accruing cross-session paralinguistic memory over real user interactions, no such labels exist at deployment, so the load-bearing admission reward for paralinguistic writes is the key-agreement surrogate, which Sec. 8.2 admits 'measures self-consistency of two model passes, not consistency with ground truth' and whose optimization 'can reinforce the extractor's bias rather than the truth.' Meanwhile the genuinely verifiable channel is robust precisely on content/intent — the factors that already work and never needed the agent.  
  *Fix:* Quantify what fraction of flagship (paralinguistic, cross-session) write/skill decisions can actually be gated on a verifiable channel at deployment — if near zero, say so and retitle the gate as a self-consistency gate for that regime. Add a concrete decoupling protocol (separate routing estimator from reward estimator, periodic calibration against held-out labeled probes) and an experiment measuring surrogate-vs-truth drift over sessions, rather than deferring it to 'future work.' Do not claim Goodhart resistance for the paralinguistic loop.
- **[critical]** (Sec. 2.4 (Definition def:verifiable items iii/iv vs Assumption ass:verifiable); Sec. 7.4 (Operator-A probe-accuracy gains)) Assumption 1 (verifiable reward 'independent of g_θ') is internally contradicted by the rewards used for the flagship Operator-A channel. Definition 6 lists as canonical verifiable rewards '(iii) retrieval hit@k for embedding queries' and '(iv) probe accuracy for a fixed linear probe' — both computed ON the frozen model's own embeddings, hence functions of g_θ. Assumption 1 then asserts R is 'independent of g_θ.' Operator-A readout selection (Table tab:opA) is optimized using exactly these g_θ-dependent rewards.  
  *Fix:* Reconcile Definition 6 and Assumption 1: either restrict 'verifiable' to label-derived rewards genuinely independent of g_θ (WER, exact-match against y*), explicitly moving probe-accuracy and retrieval-hit into a separate 'model-dependent verifiable' class with its own caveats, or weaken Assumption 1 and re-derive which guarantees survive when R depends on g_θ. Add nested cross-validation / held-out probe training so the probe reward is not evaluated on the representations it was fit to.

The title asserts TFRL '...Recovers in Context-Isolated Agent Systems' as a declarative fact; the Conclusion (Sec. 10) states 'OSA-2 shows that, under context isolation, an operator-A search distribution is adequate for the multi-key agentic reward' and 'OSA-3 (with OSA-3b) establishes ... the gate's behavior as a control law.' Both directly contradict Sec. 5.3–5.4, which bill OSA-2 as a conditional result plus an open Conjecture 1 ('not something the present theory establishes') and the trust-region/control-law reading as 'an analogy/conjecture, not a theorem.'

In the Conclusion, replace 'OSA-2 shows ... is adequate' with 'OSA-2 proves additivity under assumed separability and CONJECTURES (Conj. 1) that isolation produces it,' and replace 'establishes the gate's behavior as a control law' with 'proves a static deficit and factorization; the control-law/convergence reading remains an open conjecture.' Mirror the Sec. 5 hedges verbatim.
