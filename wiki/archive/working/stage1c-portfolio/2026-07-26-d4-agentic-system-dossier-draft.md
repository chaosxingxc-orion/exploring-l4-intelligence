### 2602.14224 — The Interspeech 2026 Audio Reasoning Challenge: Evaluating Reasoning Process Quality for Audio Reasoning Models and Agents

**方法面（method surface）**：Benchmark/challenge design paper, not a system. (1) Task: F(A,Q) -> (Y_CoT, Y_Answer) over MMAR (1000 real-world audio-QA MCQ instances spanning sound/music/speech and mixes; signal/perception/semantic/cultural layers) (Sec 3.1). (2) Metric = MMAR-Rubrics, an instance-level rubric protocol: Gemini-2.5-Pro generates k=5 instance-specific checkable atomic criteria from the human-annotated gold CoT; GPT-4o rates each criterion binary True/False with a textual justification; E(Y_CoT) = fraction satisfied. Crucially ANSWER-GATED: S = E(Y_CoT) if answer correct else 0 (Eq.2, Sec 3.2). Validated against a system-level 5-point holistic rubric: inter-rater Krippendorff alpha 0.7921 vs 0.7459, intra-rater 0.9216 vs 0.9081 (5 runs GPT-4o at T=1.0), human alignment on 50 preference pairs 17 agree / 2 reversals / 31 ties vs 15/8/27 (Table 1). (3) Two tracks (Sec 3.3): Single Model Track = one end-to-end forward, NO external tools/APIs/search/controllers; Agent Track = may orchestrate multiple open-source models and tools, explicitly inviting plan-execute-reflect, structured memory for intermediate artifacts, and self-verification/cross-checking. (4) Results (Sec 4, Tables 2-4): 156 registrations / 18 countries; final stage 14 single-model + 16 agent teams on the full 1000. Agent top = 69.83 Rubrics / 76.90 Acc; single-model top = 65.29 / 74.00; agent rank2 = 66.23 / 77.40 (highest accuracy anywhere); agent rank3 = 66.09 / 75.10 (multi-agent debate under a central Controller with 'Peer Opinions' critique rounds). Agent-track floor is far higher (rank16 = 23.91) than single-model floor (rank14 = 19.03). DECISION RIGHTS the challenge frames as in-scope for agents: sub-task decomposition, active evidence seeking, self-correction. SELECTION SIGNAL: none at system level - the rubric is an OFFLINE metric requiring gold CoT and gold answer; it is not and cannot be a controller signal. Explicitly NO reward model anywhere in the evaluation or in any top agent system; the only 'reward' in the paper is training-time GRPO reward used by the 1st single-model team (CoT semantic similarity), i.e. gradient-based and out of our contract. Notable: the 2nd single-model team was TRAINING-FREE but WHITE-BOX - scaling attention weights over the audio token span of Qwen3-Omni-Thinking to fight 'skimming' (62.55 / 71.00), no parameter updates.

**自述与显见局限**：Stated/evident: (a) automatic CoT evaluation is conceded to be an open problem and even the improved protocol has only alpha~0.79 inter-rater, i.e. ~20% disagreement remains (Sec 3.2, Table 1); (b) the metric needs human-annotated gold CoT per instance, so it is not portable to new data or usable online; (c) answer-gating discards all information about correct-reasoning/wrong-answer cases and makes abstention and partial credit unmeasurable (Eq.2); (d) rubric criteria were revealed only AFTER the challenge (per 2603.17822 Sec 5.3), so leaderboard ranks conflate architecture with accidental rubric alignment; (e) the leaderboard has NO cost/latency/call-count axis - a 1-call model and an 8-10-minute 40-tool pipeline are ranked on the same line; (f) no direct-readout baseline row is reported in the official leaderboard tables at all (baselines only appear in participant papers); (g) single-turn 4-way MCQA only - no multi-step, no open-generation, no interactive speech tasks; (h) tool-track descriptions are secondhand and inconsistent with the primary reports (overview says the champion integrated 'over 40 specialized audio tools'; the champion's own paper says 25 enabled / 21 observed); (i) Qwen3-Omni family is the backbone of every top entry in both tracks, so results are single-family-conditioned.

**改进空间（方向级台阶）**：Direction-level steps left open ON TOP of this: (1) a budget-normalized axis for the same benchmark - report Rubrics/Acc at matched LALM-call counts and wall-clock, which nobody in either track did; (2) an abstention/selective-prediction variant of the metric (risk-coverage over the 1000) so 'know when you cannot hear it' becomes scoreable, currently structurally impossible under Eq.2; (3) an online-legal proxy for reasoning quality that uses only deployment-available signals (evidence density, corroboration count, inter-source agreement) and is calibrated AGAINST the offline rubric once - this is the clean way to use the rubric without leaking it into the loop; (4) extension past MCQ to open-ended audio QA / instruction following, where selection over candidates stops degenerating into vote counting; (5) cross-instance state: every ARC system is stateless per sample, so a memory that carries tool-usefulness or category-difficulty priors across the 1000 instances is unoccupied within ARC and the audio-agent trio; the omni closed-loop group has one occupied form (2512.16978's per-video one-time index / many-query raw evidence store) -- what remains unoccupied is promotion of VERIFIED claims plus per-category reliability priors across items; 【复核更正 wf_5f1f5801】 (6) a stopping-rule track - the challenge grants agents the right to iterate but never scores when they should stop.

**可借鉴**：Baseline numbers with provenance: agent SOTA 69.83 Rubrics / 76.90 Acc (rank1), 66.23 / 77.40 (rank2), 66.09 / 75.10 (rank3); single-model SOTA 65.29 / 74.00; best training-free single-model 62.55 / 71.00 (white-box attention scaling, useful contrast to a black-box wrapper); full 16-row and 14-row leaderboards (Table 4) give a distribution, not just a top line, so our lift can be positioned as a percentile. Implied direct-readout anchor: 664/1000 correct-answer paths from Qwen3-Omni-Thinking used for the reliability study (Sec 3.2 bullet 1) => ~66.4% direct accuracy. Protocol assets: MMAR-Rubrics k=5 criteria-generation + GPT-4o binary-rating-with-justification recipe, the answer-gated scoring equation, the reliability harness itself (3-rater inter / 5-run intra Krippendorff alpha + 50-pair human preference study) - that harness is directly reusable to prove any metric WE introduce is stable. Track rules are a ready-made ablation contract: 'Single Model Track' == our direct-readout condition, 'Agent Track' == our wrapper condition, with the no-external-tools clause spelled out. Released: MMAR-Rubrics data + eval scripts.

**资产完备度**：Open resources: MMAR-Rubrics data and evaluation scripts at github.com/ddlBoJack/MMAR; challenge site audio-reasoning-challenge.github.io. Underlying MMAR benchmark = NeurIPS 2025 (arXiv 2505.13032), 1000 instances. Judge stack is proprietary-API dependent: Gemini-2.5-Pro for criteria generation, GPT-4o as rater (Gemini-2.5-Flash and GPT-5-mini used only for the reliability study) - reproducing scores needs paid API access and the note that judge variability alone moves scores ~1.1 pt (69.8 leaderboard vs 68.71 single-run replication, per 2603.17822 fn.1). Licenses not stated in the paper. Pin: arXiv 2602.14224v1, cs.SD, 15 Feb 2026. No participant code is released by the organizers; team identities in the overview are anonymized (Team A-E) and must be cross-linked via the participant papers (rank1 agent = TalTech 2603.17822; rank2 agent = VISA 2606.07264).

**域界定**：Audio-omni, speech included as one of MMAR's modalities (speech-only questions are the largest slice; TalTech reports speech-only 85.7% vs music 58.7%). Transfers to speech-omni: the two-track direct-vs-wrapper contract, the answer-gated process metric, the metric-reliability harness, and the whole leaderboard as baseline distribution. Does NOT transfer: single-turn 4-way MCQA framing (no multi-turn, no generation, no duplex/spoken output), gold-CoT dependence, and the reliance on Qwen3-Omni-family cores. The 2nd single-model entry's attention manipulation is training-free but requires internals, so it is outside our black-box contract and belongs in the 'what we deliberately refuse' column.

**备注**：Field-level counterexample to the prior 'agentic systems lose to direct-readout' fact: on MMAR the agent track beats the single-model track on BOTH axes, and the gap is larger on reasoning quality than on accuracy (Sec 4.1). So the OmniGAIA finding is task-conditioned, not a law - but note the ARC gain is bought with an unaccounted budget (see TalTech's 8-10 min/sample). Also note the overview's own claim that the gap is 'more pronounced in reasoning quality' is consistent with an alternative reading it does not test: agents write denser prose, and a rubric that counts satisfied atomic criteria rewards density (TalTech admits exactly this in its Sec 5.3).

### 2603.17822 — Multi-Source Evidence Fusion for Audio Question Answering (TalTech, Aivo Olev & Tanel Alumae) - 1st place, Interspeech 2026 ARC Agent Track

**方法面（method surface）**：Training-free black-box pipeline over frozen components; no gradients anywhere. Flow (Sec 3, Fig 1): Audio+Question+Choices -> two open-weight LALMs (Step-Audio-R1, Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Thinking) each queried 4x (full audio + 3 equal-duration segments) and PROMPTED TO REPORT OBSERVATIONS, NOT TO ANSWER -> unified evidence analysis (merge across segments, corroboration bonus when segment and full-audio agree, audio-content-type classification, then a reasoning-LLM call labels each observation corroborated (conf 0.80-0.95) / source-specific (0.50-0.70) / disagreement) -> Step 1 evidence gathering, agent iteratively selects from 12 whole-audio tools (23 if music), up to 3 rounds -> three-stage contradiction detection -> Step 2 targeted validation with 5 (8 for music) segment-level tools on specific time ranges, up to 2 rounds -> two sequential LLM calls: answer selection, then reasoning generation. TOOL BANK: 25 enabled, 21 observed, in four hand-set reliability tiers (Table 1): Analytic (beat/energy/spectral; cap 0.90, weight 1.0), Probabilistic (Whisper ASR, diarization, separation, instruments; 0.75 / 0.75), Heuristic (chord, environment, scene; 0.60 / 0.50), LALMs (StepAudioR1, Qwen3-Omni; 0.70 / 0.40 - LALMs are ranked LAST). EXACT SELECTION/ARBITRATION SIGNAL: a scalar confidence per evidence item = tier base confidence x 1.5 corroboration multiplier x 1.3 direct-answer bonus, hard-capped at 0.70 for LALM evidence, reduced by a domain-appropriateness factor for out-of-domain tool use; confidence and relevance tracked separately and the argumentation stage weights evidence by their PRODUCT. The final decision is NOT an argmax over that scalar - it is an LLM-as-arbiter (Kimi-K2-Thinking, T=0.6) reading the weighted, source-labelled evidence and picking the best-supported option. Explicitly stated: 'All caps, weights, and multipliers were set empirically during challenge development rather than learned from data' (Sec 3) - CONFIRMED no reward model, no learned scorer, no value function. DECISION RIGHTS exercised: select (LLM arbiter), repair/re-query (contradiction-triggered verification hypotheses with specific tool calls and time ranges), route (music vs non-music tool sets), stop (hand-coded: trigger verification only on disagreement or insufficient confidence, hard caps <=3 and <=2 rounds). NOT exercised: abstain (never), per-instance budget allocation (uniform), cross-instance memory (none). PROVENANCE GUARDING is a first-class mechanism: the reasoning agent never sees the LALMs' answer predictions (anti-anchoring/anti-sycophancy, citing Turpin and Sharma); a non-dismissal policy keeps single-source LALM claims 'speculative' rather than 'hallucinated' unless actively contradicted by reproducible tool evidence; four named logical pitfalls are checked (absence-of-detection as proof of absence, dismissing single-source claims, diarization over-segmentation, non-overlapping transcription disagreement); a seven-section reasoning template (what is heard / evidence synthesis / conflict resolution / reliability assessment / tool cross-references / per-choice evaluation / conclusion) plus a completeness check; and selection is separated from elaboration so the model cannot commit to a narrative before weighing evidence. RESULTS: 69.83 Rubrics (1st) / 76.9% Acc (2nd) on MMAR-1000.

**自述与显见局限**：Stated: (a) 'the reliability weights and confidence caps were tuned for this specific challenge' (Sec 1) - no held-out validation of any scalar; (b) end-to-end latency 8-10 minutes per sample, 'limiting real-time applicability', and they concede 'much of this cost is avoidable: a non-reasoning LLM could likely handle most pipeline stages with minor accuracy loss, and many questions can be answered from speech LLM observations without invoking multiple tool verification loops' (Sec 4.2) - i.e. the authors themselves diagnose the missing allocation policy; (c) per-tool usefulness scores 'should be interpreted as indicative, as judge ratings were not validated against human annotations' (Sec 5.1); (d) the leaderboard 69.8 vs single-run 68.71 gap is attributed to LLM-judge variability (fn.1); (e) rubric criteria were revealed only after the challenge, so 'these architectural choices were not optimized for the rubrics but happen to align with them' (Sec 5.3) - an honest admission that the win may be partly metric-shaped; (f) multiple-choice only, 'future work will investigate scaling beyond multiple-choice settings' (Sec 6). Evident but unstated: (g) the anti-anchoring design (hiding LALM answers) is justified with 'our ablation suggests the effect is small in this setting (Section 5.2)' but Table 6 contains ONLY the two single-source rows - there is no answers-visible ablation row anywhere, so the load-bearing provenance-guard decision is UNVERIFIED; (h) the only ablation is an argumentation-level replay with all upstream artifacts frozen, so tool gathering, contradiction detection, the tier scalars, the segment decomposition, and the seven-section template are each never ablated - the +3.2/+4.3 pp result isolates dual-LALM fusion and nothing else; (i) 4 of 25 tools never fired during the entire evaluation; (j) Table 2 cites Qwen3-Omni-Thinking at 66.4% Acc against VISA's 69.9% for the same-named model, an unreconciled baseline discrepancy.

**改进空间（方向级台阶）**：Direction-level steps left open ON TOP of this: (1) BUDGET/ALLOCATION is the biggest measured opening and the paper hands us the statistics: LALM agreement predicts accuracy with a 36.5 pp spread (unanimous 94.5% n=128, majority 83.2% n=565, conflicting 58.0% n=307, Table 3) and confidence is monotonically calibrated (91.1% at >=0.80 n=237, 74.4% at 0.60-0.79 n=722, 39.4% at 0.40-0.59 n=33) - so a policy that terminates on unanimity and reallocates the saved tool rounds to the conflicting 307 is both obviously available and never attempted; a reward/advantage-driven controller is the natural way to set that policy instead of hand caps. (2) TOOL SELECTION is grossly mis-allocated and quantified per tool (Table 5): energy dynamics 59.3% irrelevant over 784 invocations, spectral features 67.8% over 736, speaker count 90.3% over 394, event sequence 89.0% over 264, temporal segments 90.4% over 166, tempo tracking 95.5%, rhythm analysis 100.0% irrelevant - a gradient-free policy that learns which tools pay off per question type has a large, already-measured headroom, and the paper's own LLM-judge usefulness scores are a ready-made offline signal to fit it. (3) CALIBRATE INSTEAD OF HAND-SET: replace the tier caps/multipliers with values fit on a held-out split (still training-free w.r.t. the frozen core - we fit the arbiter, not the model), and report the delta against their hand-set configuration as a clean lift. ### 2605.28480 — Audio-Mind: An Auditable Agentic Framework for Audio Understanding (arXiv:2605.28480v1, eess.AS, 27 May 2026)

**方法面（method surface）**：MECHANISM: a stateful graph over a FROZEN audio LALM frontend + a FROZEN text-LLM planner, with an explicit shared evidence state (audio artifact list with stable ids audio_0/derived-with-provenance, evidence log, tool-call history, planner trace) (Fig.1, S4.1). Question lifecycle: (P1) planner writes a question-oriented perception prompt that restates the question, names listening targets and REQUIRES the frontend to report uncertainty; (P2) frontend LALM returns structured evidence = general description + focus points + preliminary answer + uncertainty/verification needs; (P3) planner turns the caption into a lightweight plan, explicitly treating the caption as evidence, not ground truth, and marking which claims look answerable by direct perception; (P4) k-round loop in which the planner emits EXACTLY ONE action type per round: {parallel independent tool calls | targeted frontend re-listen on selected audio ids | ANSWER | GIVE UP}, each with a mandatory short rationale fed back in later rounds; (P5a) neutral evidence summary that preserves contradictions and does not adjudicate sources, (P5b) FINAL ANSWER IS GENERATED BY THE LALM FRONTEND from the ORIGINAL audio + summary (not by the planner), (P5c) a format-only checker that validates structure, never content, looping back to 5b on failure (S4.2-4.4).
DECISION RIGHTS: select/allocate/stop/abstain all sit with the TEXT planner; the audio-grounded ANSWER-generation right is deliberately returned to the frozen LALM; format validation is a separate agent so formatting failures are not conflated with evidence failures.
KEEP-THE-INCUMBENT GATE, EXACTLY: there is NO reward model, NO candidate scoring, NO comparison between the direct answer and the agent answer. The gate is (a) a prompted evidence-gap judgment by the planner over the frontend's own self-reported uncertainty, plus (b) an architectural guarantee that the final answer is always re-grounded on the raw audio by the frontend. Empirically the gate is conditional, not always-on: 31.2% of questions exit at ZERO tool calls, 31.1% exit in a single planner round, mean 1.68 tool calls and 2.68 planner rounds per question, 93.3% exit within five rounds, cap = 15 planner steps, temperature 0.05 (S5.3, App.B, App.D). Targeted re-listening is rare by design: 50/999 questions (5.0%), 64 re-listens total (App.B.3). 99.9% of runs exit with ANSWER; exactly one GIVE UP, caused by an API safety rejection — so the abstain right is nominally present but never exercised.
SIGNALS USED: only question text, frontend caption + its self-declared uncertainty/confidence, tool outputs, planner trace, tool inventory metadata. Explicitly no ground truth, no baseline outputs, no logprobs, no hidden states (S5.3).
TOOLS AS BOUNDED EVIDENCE INTERFACES (S3): two roles — PERCEPTION tools (extract observations: ASR, diarization, speaker verification, pitch/tempo/key/chord, spectral stats, plot inspection) vs TRANSFORMATION tools (trim, denoise, filter, resample, harmonic-percussive separation, channel convert) whose output is NOT evidence but a better input for later perception or re-listening. Each tool carries a planner-facing 'boundary' saying what claim class it may support (a transcript verifies lexical content but not speaker identity; tempo/chord estimates are measurements, not music-theoretic judgments). 38 planner-visible tools in 6 categories: audio derivation 8, temporal segmentation 5, metadata validation 2, speech/speaker 9, acoustic-music 12, signal visualization 2 (Table 1, App.E Table 8).

### 2511.02834 — Agent-Omni: Test-Time Multimodal Reasoning via Model Coordination for Understanding Anything (arXiv:2511.02834v2, cs.AI, 5 Nov 2025)

**方法面（method surface）**：MECHANISM: a master agent (Claude 3.7 Sonnet, text-only — it cannot hear) running a 4-stage loop over a pool of FROZEN modality-specific foundation models, with no fine-tuning or retraining anywhere (S2). Stages: PERCEPTION — master summarises each provided modality into a JSON {modality list, per-modality semantic summary}; REASONING — master derives a user_intent and writes modality-addressed sub-questions into agent_questions{image_model:[q1,q2,...], audio_model:[...]}, with the prompt explicitly encouraging 'more than three' diverse questions per agent per round, and requiring the master to inject ALL context because 'downstream agents have access only to the user's input in their specific modality... they do not have access to the user's original query' (App.B, Fig.4); EXECUTION — a dispatcher invokes the named models and pairs each sub-question with its answer; DECISION — master emits {final_answer, is_final, suggestions}; if is_final is false the suggestions are fed back into a new reasoning round (the 'master loop'), capped at L=3 by default.
DECISION RIGHTS: decomposition, delegation, fusion, self-assessment and STOPPING all sit with the master. Stopping is a prompted boolean completeness flag (is_final) with no evidence grounding, no scoring, no reward, no verification against the raw signal. Model-to-modality assignment is STATIC and hand-picked (Table 3: text=DeepSeek R1, image=video=Claude 3.7 Sonnet, audio=Qwen2.5-Omni) — the master routes to a modality slot, never selects among candidate models, and never resamples an agent. Crucially, and in contrast to Audio-Mind, the FINAL ANSWER IS WRITTEN BY THE TEXT MASTER, never re-grounded on the raw audio by the audio model: the audio path is a one-shot text bottleneck.
SIGNALS USED: only the text of agent answers and the master's own completeness judgment. No confidence, no logprobs, no agreement scores, no candidate set.
EFFECT SIZES vs DIRECT READOUT — AUDIO ROWS (Table 6, the in-scope ones): MMAU 73.20 vs Qwen2.5-Omni 70.90 (+2.3), vs Phi4-Multimodal-Instruct 59.70, vs Qwen2-Audio-7B 54.70; MELD-Emotion 51.97 vs 38.35 (+13.6, the largest audio gain); VoxCeleb-Gender 98.60 vs 97.85 (+0.75, near ceiling). **自述与显见局限**：STATED (Limitations): reliance on external foundation models makes results sensitive to API changes; errors/biases from individual models PROPAGATE through coordination; the iterative master loop adds latency and compute; evaluation is on curated benchmarks only, so generalisation to noisy, adversarial or safety-critical settings is unverified; text output only. Ethics adds that coordination may AMPLIFY component biases.
EVIDENT: (1) The master is text-only and never hears the audio; every audio claim survives only as master-written text, which is precisely the 'orchestration bottleneck' mechanism Audio-Mind (2605.28480 S5.2) identifies as the cause of agentic under-performance — and Agent-Omni does not measure it. (2) No non-regression check: the framework has no mechanism to notice that it lost 3.6 points to direct readout on OmniInstruct, and the paper does not flag those losses in prose (the narrative claims 'best accuracy on nearly all datasets'). (3) is_final is an unverified self-report; there is no calibration evidence that it correlates with correctness. (4) No seeds, no CIs, no repeated runs; several 'gains' (VoxCeleb +0.75, MMLU-Social -0.09) are inside any plausible noise band, and iteration-wise curves are non-monotone in three benchmarks. (5) The audio agent is called once per sub-question — no resampling, no self-consistency, no candidate scoring — so the audio side is a strict single-trajectory pipeline. (6) No memory across queries; 'suggestions' persist only inside one query's loop. (7) The pool assignment is fixed by an offline ablation on the SAME test benchmarks the system is then evaluated on (S3.9 + App.A), i.e. the pool is selected on test data. (8) Cost is reported as latency only, on a mixed API/local deployment, which is not comparable across rows.

**改进空间（方向级台阶）**：(1) THE MISSING NON-REGRESSION GATE. Agent-Omni has no keep-the-incumbent mechanism at all; the OmniInstruct -3.6, MathVision -1.2/-5.6 and MMLU -1.5 rows are exactly what an answer-level selection right between {direct readout, orchestrated answer} would remove. Adding a training-free selector over that binary is a concrete step ON TOP of this architecture.
(2) RE-GROUND THE FINAL ANSWER ON RAW AUDIO. Returning the answer-writing right to the frozen omni core (Audio-Mind's design) is a testable modification to Agent-Omni's decision stage that its own architecture forbids today; it isolates the text-bottleneck cost.
(3) DYNAMIC AGENT SELECTION OVER THE AUDIO POOL. Table 14 shows a 54.5-point swing on VoxCeleb-Gender and a 4.2-point swing on MMAU purely from which audio model is called, with different winners per task. A training-free router/bandit that picks or ensembles audio agents per QUESTION using only observable signals (inter-agent agreement, answer stability under paraphrase) is unoccupied — the paper picks one arm offline on test data.
(4) REWARD-BASED STOPPING. is_final is a prompted flag; the loop already exits at iteration 1 for 60-94% of queries and contributes +0 to +3 points beyond that. Replacing it with an advantage estimate (does another round change the answer? does agreement rise?) both cuts the 18-83x latency and removes the non-monotone regressions (VoxCeleb 98.74 -> 98.60, VSI 39.55 -> 39.50).

### 2605.08762 — Omni-DeepSearch: A Benchmark for Audio-Driven Omni-Modal Deep Search (arXiv:2605.08762v1, cs.SD, 9 May 2026)

**方法面（method surface）**：NOT A SYSTEM — a benchmark plus a reference tool-augmented inference pipeline; it specifies, and then measures, the system behaviours an audio-initiated agent must have.
TASK CONTRACT (S3.1): audio is the ONLY initial modality (chosen deliberately because 'models may over-rely on visual information when audio and visual signals are presented together'); given 1-4 clips and a question the system must (i) infer the audio-grounded entity e0 (or, for multi-audio, a shared mediator entity em that all clips constrain), (ii) formulate search queries, (iii) invoke external text/image/video search, (iv) do multi-hop reasoning over retrieved evidence, (v) do cross-modal verification, (vi) emit a short objective string answer, with UNKNOWN available as an explicit abstain token (App.D). Four design principles: mandatory audio dependence, hard-to-find/easy-to-verify, omni-modal retrieval, answer uniqueness.
REFERENCE PIPELINE (S4.1, tool schemas in App.D): three tools — text_search, image_search_by_text_query, video_search — each accepting EXACTLY ONE query per call; the prompt forces every step to be a search ('You MUST NOT answer based on your internal knowledge... Do not generate the answer in the first shot'), forces one minimal sub-question per step, and forces an explicit answer to the previous sub-question from the local retrieved context before moving on. Video tasks use a two-stage verify-then-densify policy: retrieve candidate video, extract ~16 frames for rapid verification, re-query or pick a new candidate on mismatch, then extract ~64 frames for fine-grained temporal reasoning. Budgets are fixed retry caps, written (X,Y) = max searches for text/image tasks and for video tasks.
DECISION RIGHTS IN THE REFERENCE PIPELINE: the frozen model holds query formulation, tool choice, retry/abandon, and answer emission; there is no external controller, no reward, no scoring of candidates, and the only stopping rule is budget exhaustion or self-declared completion.
BENCHMARK CONSTRUCTION (S3.3-3.4): 640 samples, 15 fine-grained categories = 3 single-audio retrieval targets x 4 audio content types (speech / music / animal / ambient) + multi-audio text search split by clip count. Questions are Wikipedia knowledge-graph paths of length k>=5 (k>=3 for image-text, where the terminal node must be verifiable only through image evidence) rooted at the audio-associated entity; one non-root node is bound to a RECENT NEWS EVENT to defeat parametric-knowledge shortcuts. Filtering: Gemini-3-Pro audio QC (single dominant source, unambiguous category) then four GPT-5 stages — joint audio+question reasoning filter, question-only reasoning filter, first-hop entity leakage filter, visual-modality-necessity filter — plus a manual check for alternative reasonable answers. Metric: accuracy with three independent LLM judges (GPT-5.4, Gemini-3-Pro, Claude-Sonnet-4.6) by majority vote.

**改进空间（方向级台阶）**：(1) A STOPPING / EVIDENCE-CONSOLIDATION RULE IS THE NAMED, UNFILLED GAP. A.6 is a fully worked demonstration that the correct answer was in hand and was then lost to extra search. This is a training-free, black-box-legal, directly measurable step on the paper's own artifact.
(2) PER-CATEGORY BUDGET ALLOCATION. Table 3 shows the optimal budget is category-dependent (image/speech want more, video/ambient want less), yet the pipeline spends a uniform cap. Allocating a fixed TOTAL budget across the 640-item portfolio by predicted marginal return is unoccupied and measurable on released data.
(3) MULTI-AUDIO AS A CONSTRAINT-SATISFACTION PROBLEM. The dominance-bias failure means the system treats n clips as one strong plus n-1 weak cues. A controller that keeps a candidate mediator set and scores it by AGREEMENT ACROSS ALL CLIPS (a label-free reward) is a clean step; MULTI is the second-hardest cell (40.63 for the best model, 0-9.4 for open models).
(4) ENTITY-HYPOTHESIS SEARCH RATHER THAN ENTITY COMMITMENT. Table 4's 33.76 entity-only vs 43.44 end-to-end shows retrieval feedback already repairs grounding implicitly for strong models and NOT AT ALL for weak ones (12.50 vs 11.72). Explicit multi-hypothesis entity maintenance with retrieval-based re-ranking is where the weak-model gap lives — and our frozen local core sits squarely in the weak-model regime.
(5) TOOL-CALL VALIDITY REPAIR. Empty and malformed tool calls are a documented failure class for weaker models; a repair/retry layer with schema validation is trivially training-free and would move open-model scores off the floor before any reasoning improvement is attempted.
(6) VERIFICATION AS A SEPARATE RIGHT. Image tasks fail by silently falling back to text; a controller that refuses to accept a text-derived answer for a visual-necessity item (the benchmark labels these) enforces provenance — this is exactly a provenance-guarding decision right.
(7) A NO-TOOL DIRECT-READOUT ROW should be added by us as a baseline discipline, even though the construction pipeline is designed to make it near-zero.

**备注**：The closest training-free precedent to our loop, and the paper that most usefully falsifies the naive version of our thesis from inside: with a weaker brain the same scaffold drops to 68.7 on Daily-Omni, BELOW the 72.08 direct readout of the frozen Qwen3-Omni-30B. OPEN COMPOSITION. Across all three, NO scalar reward or advantage exists at inference. Verification is either free-text self-assessment (2512.23646 Reflect), a binary continuation gate (2512.16978 verify_claim), or a prompt threshold (2606.19341 "confidence >= 0.9" rule, App. B.5). Consequently every one of our charter's decision rights is unoccupied at the level of a signal-driven controller: SELECT (no inference-time candidate generation or ranking anywhere - 2606.19341 uses best-of-N only offline for SFT data synthesis), REPAIR (2512.23646 App. D.3 documents 5-7-step redundant Clip-QA loops with no detection or correction), STOP (all three use a hard cap plus model self-declaration; no calibration data is reported for any stopping rule), ABSTAIN (absent in all three), ALLOCATE (flat per-item caps; 2512.23646 uses only 6.7-9.7 of 30 permitted steps on average, 2606.19341 saturates at ~11.7 turns even with K=52, so unused budget is never redistributed to hard items). Cross-instance memory exists only as a raw per-video evidence index (2512.16978); no system promotes VERIFIED claims across queries, and 2512.16978's own data gives ~18 questions per video, making that a cheap, directly testable gap. Finally, no paper puts the FROZEN OMNI CORE in the orchestrator seat: 2606.19341 trains it, 2512.16978 uses a 35B text LLM, 2512.23646 uses OpenAI o3 plus Gemini-2.5-Flash. The composition "frozen omni core drives its own loop, steered by an external gradient-free reward, over a speech-task portfolio, with budget/stopping and cross-instance memory" is confirmed unoccupied by this trio.

GATES AND RISKS (each grounded in an in-trio number). G1 - Direct readout is a live threat, not a formality: LongShOTAgent 66.64 vs Qwen3-Omni-30B-T 64.05 is only +2.59 for a 35B orchestrator plus a 5 FPS/188 h index; the GPT-4o-brained OmniAgent-ZJU (2512.23646) scores 68.7 on Daily-Omni versus 72.08 for the direct-readout Qwen3-Omni-30B, i.e. the wrapper LOSES outright when the brain is weakened. 【复核更正 wf_5f1f5801】 Any gain we report must be paired with the same frozen core's direct readout and a compute/cost column. G2 - The metric protocol can flip the verdict: on WorldSense MCQ the frozen omni models beat LongShOTAgent (54.0 / 52.65 vs 46.56) and lose it open-ended (-12.3 / -19.1 vs -3.2); on Video-MME monolithic models shed 13.6-20.5 points once options are stripped. We must pre-register BOTH protocols so we neither hide wrapper gains behind MCQ nor manufacture them by choosing the flattering protocol. G3 - Information boundary: 2512.16978's rubric verifier is gold-grounded by construction ("comparing the candidate answer against the human-validated reference answer rather than relying on the verifier's own world knowledge", intro/§3.4). It is an excellent read-out oracle for evaluation and an illegal controller signal; any in-loop scorer must be re-derived from deployment-available information only. G4 - Core-swap fragility dominates method effects: orchestrator deltas span +12.95 to +38.52 (2512.16978 Table 3) and brain swaps span 82.7/75.9/68.7 (2512.23646 Fig. 5), while the tool-model swap for event localization alone costs 8.5 points. Conclusions established on a frontier brain do not transfer to our llama.cpp Qwen3-Omni-30B; the frozen-core feasibility run is a gate, not an ablation. G5 - The trained mirror-image sets an interpretable bar and, encouragingly, most of its lift is architectural rather than gradient-borne: LVBench 43.0 -> 48.7 with Agentic SFT, -> 49.8 vanilla GRPO, -> 50.5 TAURA, so RL contributes +1.8 of +7.5; but Standard (non-agentic) SFT REGRESSES to 41.6, i.e. the loop is what pays. The open question our program can answer is how much of the loop's share survives with zero gradients - and 2606.19341 never ran that arm, while asserting (§3.2, unmeasured) that base models cannot sustain long-horizon agentic reasoning. G6 - Reporting hygiene: all three are single-run with no seeds or variance (2512.16978 App. G says so explicitly); two of three report no per-item cost for the agent; only 2512.23646 publishes tokens/latency/dollars. We should ship cost and variance by default, since our margin over direct readout will plausibly be single-digit.
