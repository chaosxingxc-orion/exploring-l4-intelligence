## B0 axis statement (constructor)

This lane occupies B0 axis (c) — sample complexity of realizing q* — and cannot reduce to the killed object because it takes the killed theorem as a premise rather than an opponent: under separable reward and product base (qstar_product's exact hypotheses), the isolated optimum EQUALS the monolithic optimum, so the two selection arms compared here share, by the killed theorem itself, one and the same value ceiling V* = sum_k sup R_k, and both arms converge to it a.s. as N goes to infinity. The claim is exclusively about the finite-N rate of that convergence: the Gibbs identity gain = beta*KL(q0||q*) <= spread^2/(8*beta) is a distributional statement about limiting tilted values and is algebraically silent on order statistics of N i.i.d. draws — no rearrangement of exp(a+b)=exp(a)exp(b) produces a statement containing (1-(1-p)^N). Nor is this covert headroom-via-isolation on the output side: for an autoregressive ASR decoder every word sequence has nonzero probability, so the composed transcript's strict support membership is unchanged (ruling out axis-a smuggling); only its realization PROBABILITY under whole-utterance sampling is exponentially small, which is precisely the sample-cost object axis (c) names.
