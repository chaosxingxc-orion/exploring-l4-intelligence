## Refuter (prosecution; defends the 7/02 null)

### R1 tautology check

The strongest T-part is an accounting identity wearing a theorem's clothes, and its headline ('moves the oracle ceiling ITSELF') is formally false for the actual operator. (1) Zero support does not exist for a softmax autoregressive LM: q0 assigns positive probability to every token string, so supp(q0) NEVER misses argmax R — the hypothesis 'when supp(q0) misses argmax R' is unsatisfiable in exactly the setting the paper targets. This is deep-review P3 verbatim turned around: 'Finite Fintype Z + q0>0 ⇒ spread = global range ⇒ the certified bound is gain ≤ 1/(8β) … vacuous exactly for the concentrated, autoregressive omni models the paper targets.' With q0>0 everywhere the fixed-q0 spread bound is already the vacuous global-range bound, and nothing can 'strictly exceed' a vacuous bound — the T-part's exceedance claim cannot even be stated against the bound as certified. (2) The honest restatement — lexicon injection raises an exponentially small probability to a usable one — is a SAMPLE-COMPLEXITY claim, i.e., it collapses into lane M4's own pre-registered honest content ('decomposition changes realization complexity (hit cost …), not headroom' — prereg §5 M4). The N=∞ oracle ceiling never moves; only the finite-N realized ceiling does. M3's differentiation from the killed box therefore rests on an idealized zero-support fiction. (3) The finitary decomposition itself: total gain = (E_{q0(c)}[R] − E_{q0}[R]) + tilt-gain(q0(c)). This is the chain rule of expectations — the additive sibling of exp(a+b)=exp(a)·exp(b). It attributes all contested gain to the conditioning term, about which it says nothing quantitative: no floor (P5 pattern: 'strict positivity has no floor so the k-growth story is vacuous'), and c is exogenous — WHO supplies the lexicon is outside the theorem (P7 pattern: an exogenous knob). Conceded: it does not literally reduce to the product tautology, and it matches the letter of P4's named overturn ('a family q0(c) with a theorem cleanly separating reward-tilt gain from conditioning gain'); but P4's overturn ALSO requires showing the gain is attributable to reward structure after controlling for model class, and whether a frozen model USES injected text is pure ICL ability — a model-class effect, P4's own second blade: "'flat conditioning' is really the model IGNORING the instruction = a model-class effect — contradicting the slogan 'the lever is reward structure, not model class.'"
