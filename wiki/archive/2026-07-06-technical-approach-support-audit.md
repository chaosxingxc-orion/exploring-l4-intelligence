## Verification corrections applied (downgrades and fixes)

- **P3 — "31–51% retention" restated.** 2603.13686's 31–51% is a **cross-model** worst case (native voice agents vs GPT-5 text @ 85%) on hard multi-turn tool-calling, **not a within-model retention rate**. The within-model text tax is ~**3–11 pt** (2603.24596; 2509.23755), and 2605.15104 finds only 1.8–4.8 pt with "neither architecture dominates." Do not cite 31–51% as a universal penalty. *(over-claim, ≈1 order of magnitude)*
- **P3 — VoiceBench (2410.17196) numbers corrected.** The prior "cascade 74.50 vs best-E2E 64.02" and "text→speech gap 5.77 pt" were wrong; the paper's figures are **cascade Naive-4o 89.49 vs best open-source E2E VITA 75.43 (~14 pt)** and a **text→speech gap of 4.37 pt**. The directional leg (cascade beats every E2E by >10 pt) and the verbatim LLaMA-Omni ">11 pt text drop" **hold**.
- **P3 — Cascade-Equivalence (2602.17598) is architecture-dependent.** Ultravox ≈ its cascade, **but Qwen2-Audio genuinely diverges** — and Qwen is our own model family (Qwen3-Omni). So "the omni is just an implicit cascade" is **not established for our model** and may itself be residual-omni-value evidence.
- **P3 — in-house +0.283 (SQuAD-zh) invalidated as counter-evidence.** It compares omni(audio) vs omni's **own lossy transcript** on a **text-sufficient** extractive task — precisely the regime Cascade-Equivalence predicts no audio advantage, and a weak baseline (our documented information-boundary over-reach). It must be re-run on **transcript-invariant minimal pairs** against a **strong enriched cascade** (Whisper-large-v3 + SER + prosody + diarization + matched text LLM), paired-bootstrap CIs, MI(audio;label | enriched-text)>0.
- **P4(b) — audio-native RAG upgraded to regime-conditional.** Not "latency-only": **SpeechRAG (2412.16500) beats the ASR→text cascade on accuracy under high WER / noise**; WavRAG's "only matches" (2502.14727) is the clean/low-WER regime. Net: **proven-under-noise, open under a strictly training-free frozen setup** (SpeechRAG trains a speech adapter).
- **P4(c) — speaker-keyed memory: eval surface is active, ceiling-crossing result is not.** Add 2510.19358, 2606.22868, 2602.01313, 2606.18134 as verifiable surfaces. But the specific result — **voiceprint-keyed memory beating text-memory on verifiable recall, training-free** — **remains unoccupied**; EgoMem (2509.11914) is fine-tuned and recognition-metric-only.
- **P4(d) — acceptance-gate negative scoped to "open."** 2606.14476's 97.6–99.2% blind deference is **specific to GNN-tools for node classification**; generalizing to "a frozen controller structurally cannot gate audio elements" over-reaches. Treat frozen-controller acceptance-gate feasibility for audio as an **open empirical question**, not a settled negative.
- **P4(a)/omni-as-reward — bounded, not clean route-1.** When the "complementary verifier" is the same frozen omni in a different context, its gain is capped at achievable δ_corr with a shared-blind-spot floor (2312.09244). Frame it as a **measured-δ_corr decorrelation lever**; reserve the strong new-info claim for a genuinely independent (different-pretraining/architecture) verifier or an executable ground-truth check.
- **P6 — HedgeTune wording (2506.19248):** recovers the optimal **inference-time hedging parameter**, not literally "the optimal number of samples n." Substance (single interior optimum; mitigates reward-hacking at the threshold) holds and supports the N\* framing.

## Residual uncertainty (explicitly marked)

1. **The two surviving omni contribution surfaces are propped on weak baselines.** Perception>transcript and speaker-keyed memory are currently supported mainly against a **bare** transcript cascade (CP-Bench, WS-2) or the omni's **own** transcript (the invalidated +0.283). Re-baselined against a strong **enriched matched-backbone** cascade — the honest deployable baseline — both may shrink toward **contested / absence-of-evidence**, exactly where Cascade-Equivalence and the WS-2 companion place them. **Unresolved; the single highest-exposure item.**
2. **The speaker-key may be commodity.** A diarization / speaker-verification front-end (a new-info element a *text* agent also gets) can recover "who spoke"; the omni's unique share is only what that front-end cannot recover — **currently unmeasured**.
3. **Acceptance-gate feasibility for a frozen controller** (our actual research object) is **untested in the audio/omni domain** — neither the settled-negative WS-4 first implied nor a demonstrated positive.
4. **P5 on instruction-tuned omni** — whether the AAD/ACD audio foothold survives strong-baseline, pre-registered controls (vs 2606.12160 / 2504.10020) is **unverified**.

## Verdict

**The technical approach is adequately supported at the paradigm level but exposed precisely where our differentiated claim lives.** Four of eight pillars are strong and largely uncontested: training-free reward-guided optimization is a mainstream, competitive paradigm (P1), its oracle@N bound is essentially provable (P2), the over-optimization/convergence apparatus is canonical and several results sit in our exact BoN regime (P6), and training-free element-use/routing policy optimization is a dense, actively-surveyed subfield (P7) — with the honest twist that P7's ubiquity makes the *act* of policy optimization commodity, so novelty must come from elsewhere. Dataset-level isolation (P8) is bedrock methodology, caveated only by the "necessary-but-not-sufficient for open-web agents" refinement. The exposure is concentrated in the two pillars that carry the omni-specific thesis: **P3 leg-3 (omni-as-sensor value) and P4(b)/(c) (audio-native RAG and audio/speaker-keyed memory)** — the very "perception>transcript, speaker-keyed memory" roles we call non-commodity. The literature there is genuinely split (Cascade-Equivalence 2602.17598 — though it *diverges for our Qwen family*), the RAG win is proven only under noise and not yet under a frozen training-free setup (2412.16500), the memory ceiling-crossing result is unoccupied (2509.11914 is fine-tuned), and our one in-house counter-datum was invalidated as a weak-baseline artifact. Compounding this, **P4(d) — the acceptance-gate that is our research object — is an open question, not a settled result in either direction**, and the omni-as-reward bridge must be framed as a bounded-δ_corr decorrelation lever, not a clean ceiling-crosser. Net: the program is well-grounded to *pose* its decisive question, but its positive claim will stand or fall on a Stage-2 experiment that re-baselines perception and memory against a **strong enriched matched-backbone cascade** (never the omni's own transcript), isolates transcript-invariant items, and tests the frozen-controller acceptance-gate empirically rather than assuming it.
