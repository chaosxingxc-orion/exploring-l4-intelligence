# D3 — Agentic-Benchmark Isolation & oracle@N Audit

**Stage 1 (problem-definition). Hypothesis-grade.** This deliverable audits candidate benchmarks for the decisive system-level test: can a **training-free element-use policy** over a *frozen* omni/speech system beat the **bare model's own oracle@N** on a *verifiable* task? It grades each bench on (i) **isolation feasibility** — can deployable information be held apart from oracle/reference (answer-key) information so a retrieval/memory/tool/verifier element cannot leak the answer, and (ii) **oracle@N measurability** — can we sample N rollouts of one frozen model and score the best against a verifiable check. It closes with the recommendation and the empty-cell verdict. Corrections flagged by adversarial verification are folded in and marked.

Every number/citation below traces to an arXiv ID carried in the WS-3 findings or the verification pass. Grades: **HIGH / MED / LOW** isolation; **yes / partial** oracle@N.

## Oracle@N reading

The oracle@N-vs-selection yardstick is realized *cleanly and directly* only in **text**: Agentic-Systems-as-Boosting (2605.14163) runs training-free critic/comparator selection over one weak model's proposals and reports single 67.0% → selection 76.4% vs **oracle best-of-8 = 79.0%** on SWE-bench Verified — it recovers most of, but never exceeds, the oracle gap, and the ceiling moves only via its execution/test verifier (a decorrelated new-info element). Best-of-Majority (2510.03199) and Radial-Consensus (2604.12196) give the formal pass@k/coverage ceiling. This is exactly consistent with the InfoBoundary result and is the framing our on-modality harness must inherit.

**InfoBoundary(A) is a framing bound, not a contribution.** That "a selector cannot emit an unsampled candidate, so success ≤ pass@N" is essentially the definition of coverage (Verifier-3, LOW). Under the theory-track discipline it cannot carry a novel result; the non-tautological question is *how high the bare omni's oracle@N already sits and whether cheap selection leaves headroom* — which is under-quantified and is the thing to **measure**.

Among on-modality benches, the three **live-simulator** suites (τ-Voice, EVA-Bench, and the text τ²-Bench template) support genuine oracle@N because the closed-loop sim lets one frozen model roll out N times against a deterministic DB-state check. The **static** suites (VoiceAgentBench, SpokenWOZ) are oracle@N-*partial*: their verifiable subset is samplable offline, but interactive whole-task completion cannot be faithfully resampled. The **understanding** suites (AIR-Bench, AudioBench) are samplable per-item yet have no agentic task to run oracle@N *against*.

## Human topline finding

**Absence-of-evidence across all seven benches** — none reports a human-vs-model capability topline. Two partial anchors, neither a capability ceiling: EVA-Bench's linguist inter-annotator **κ = 0.777–0.845** validates its *judges*; SpokenWOZ, being human-human, makes the operator's completion a *de-facto* reference. This is a gap in the field, not a defect we can fix by citation.

## Empty-cell verdict

**Absence-of-evidence — a genuine open cell, not confirmed-empty.** No paper assembles the full conjunction {training-free · element-use POLICY (tool-select / memory-admit / retrieval-gate / verifier-select / decoding-choice) · on an OMNI/SPEECH system · on a VERIFIABLE task · measured against the BARE model's own oracle@N/pass@k}. Every sub-component exists in isolation and the near-neighbors are close, but the decisive joint — the oracle@N-vs-policy comparison **on an omni/speech system** — is unoccupied.

Two corrections to the original WS-3 wording (per Verifier-2):

- **The benchmark surface is NOT empty.** The prior "no speech/audio-native agentic tool-use benchmark" claim is **retracted (HIGH-severity self-contradiction)**: VoiceAgentBench (2510.07978), Full-Duplex-Bench-v3 (2604.04847), τ-Voice (2603.13686), EVA-Bench (2605.13841) and MIST (2605.06897) all exist. The empty cell is the **METHOD**, not the task surface: the benches *measure models*; none *optimizes a training-free element-use policy* or *sets a bare-model oracle@N baseline*. (Absence-of-refutation, kept: I found no dedicated *survey* specifically of speech/audio agentic tool-use.)
- **On-modality training-free agentic element-use exists but always misses the yardstick.** AudioGenie-Reasoner (2509.16971, training-free multi-agent tool-route + search-for-missing-info) compares to Qwen2.5-Omni/GPT-4o, never oracle@N; Agent-Omni (2511.02834, test-time model coordination; MMAU 73.2%) beats foundation + DSPy-CoT, not oracle@N and pulls in *multiple decorrelated models*; OmniAgent (2512.23646) uses a *ground-truth skill-chain* oracle, not the bare model's oracle@N; EChO-Agent (2606.15141) reports no oracle@N. Long-video omni-agent (2512.16978) and the trained sibling AuTAgent also miss or are RL-trained. Training-free element-use *policies* exist in text (TARG 2511.09803 retrieval-gating; Eagle 2409.15518 router; AutoTool 2511.14650; Red-Bandit 2510.07239) but never against oracle@N. **Decoding-choice** is the one element genuinely realized training-free on-modality (Audio-Aware Decoding 2506.07233; AVCD 2505.20862; Temporal Contrastive Decoding 2604.15383) — but these are *fixed decoding laws* (licensed lever 3), not a learned policy, and are measured vs greedy, not oracle@N. **Boundary case:** classic ASR N-best rescoring (ProGRes 2409.00217; BERT rerankers 2104.04950) is training-free speech selection measured against oracle-WER — but the reranker is usually an *external* LM (new-info element), it is single-pass not agentic, and it demonstrably never beats oracle.

**Standing caveat:** "element-use policy" and "oracle@N" are our internal framings; a paper could implement this under "test-time compute allocation," "self-refine coverage," or "tool budget" and be missed by element-oriented querying — hence **absence-of-evidence, not confirmed-empty**.

## Recommendation — which bench(es) support the problem definition

**No off-the-shelf benchmark isolates the omni's two surviving non-commodity contribution surfaces** (perception>transcript; voiceprint/speaker-keyed memory). The original WS-3 recommendation to make **τ-Voice / EVA-Bench the decisive "beat bare-model oracle@N" harness is DOWNGRADED (Verifier-3, HIGH-relevance).** Those are retail/airline/telecom tool-calling suites — commodity planning/orchestration where a text LLM already does the job and where the audio channel is a *documented liability* (From-Text-to-Voice 2605.15104: audio introduces argument-value errors, net-negative for the omni). A win there would not isolate perception/speaker-memory; a loss would not refute it. The benchmark axis is mismatched to the hypothesis.

**Recommended two-part strategy:**

1. **Use τ²-Bench / τ-Voice / EVA-Bench as the verifiable-machinery TEMPLATE and as COMMODITY-ORCHESTRATION CONTROLS.** Their deterministic DB-state / SHA-256 completion checks + closed-loop simulator (→ faithful N rollouts → oracle@N) are exactly the harness scaffold to reuse. Run the bare omni's oracle@N and the element-use policy here to *quantify* the commodity ceiling and to show the policy does **not** need to beat it — not as the omni-contribution test. Prefer `tau2-bench-verified`; score EVA on the deterministic completion component only.

2. **BUILD the decisive isolation harness around the two surviving surfaces, each with an enriched three-arm baseline:**
   - **(a) Transcript-invariant perception minimal-pairs** — items where the gold label *flips with acoustics while the words are held constant*, forcing MI(audio; label | enriched-text) > 0. Three arms: bare-omni **oracle@N** vs an **enriched matched-backbone cascade** (Whisper-large-v3 + SER + prosody + diarization tags → the *same* text LLM, the honest deployable baseline per TextPro-SLM 2605.05927) vs the element-use policy. Report the omni-minus-*enriched*-cascade residual with paired-bootstrap CIs — **never** omni-minus-bare-cascade or omni-minus-its-own-transcript (both are the documented weak-baseline / lossy-own-ASR artifact; the in-house +0.283 SQuAD-zh number does *not* survive as counter-evidence because SQuAD-zh is text-sufficient).
   - **(b) Voiceprint-keyed cross-session recall** — three arms: end-to-end omni memory vs **ASR + speaker-verification/diarization front-end (SpeakerLM 2508.06372) + text-RAG** vs the policy. The "who-spoke" key is recoverable by a *commodity* front-end, so the omni's unique share is only what that front-end cannot recover — currently unmeasured; this three-arm test is what would settle it.

3. **Perception probes (not decisive):** VoiceAgentBench (2510.07978) for a multilingual + safety perception probe; AIR-Bench (2402.07729) and AudioBench (2406.16020) as single-turn perception probes for the omni>transcript / paralinguistic edge.

**Why the perception surface is still worth isolating (Verifier-1 caveat, favorable):** cascade-equivalence is **architecture-dependent** (2602.17598) — Ultravox is statistically indistinguishable from its matched cascade, but **Qwen2-Audio genuinely diverges**. Since the project's line is Qwen3-Omni, "the omni is just an implicit cascade" is *not* established for our model and may itself be residual-omni-value evidence — which is precisely what harness (a) would test.

## Residual uncertainty (explicit)

- **Empty-cell = absence-of-evidence, not confirmed-empty.** Element-oriented querying can miss a paper filed under alternative terminology; a direct hit could surface.
- **No verified human topline exists** on any of the seven benches — the human-vs-model ceiling for these agentic-speech tasks is genuinely unknown (κ anchors validate judges, not capability).
- **The two surviving surfaces are themselves contested.** Under a *strong enriched* matched-backbone baseline both perception>transcript and voiceprint-keyed memory shrink toward contested / absence-of-evidence (Verifier-3); the perception residual and the omni's unique speaker-memory share are **unmeasured**, and the closest speaker-memory result (EgoMem 2509.11914) is fine-tuned + recognition-only, not a verifiable-recall beat over text memory. The recommended harnesses are designed to *measure* these residuals, not to assume them.
- **Omni-as-reward is a decorrelation lever, not a clean route-(1) crossing.** When the "complementary verifier" is the same frozen omni in another context it shares weights/blind-spots; its gain is capped by achievable error-decorrelation δ_corr with a residual shared-blind-spot floor (Eisenstein herding 2312.09244). Reserve the strong new-info-element claim for a genuinely independent verifier or an executable ground-truth check.
- **Frozen-controller acceptance-gate feasibility is OPEN, not a settled negative.** The tool-over-deference result (2606.14476) is scoped to the GNN-tool / node-classification domain and should not be generalized to "a frozen controller structurally cannot gate audio elements."
- **Load-bearing numbers to re-verify at Stage 2 before they carry weight:** MMAU-Pro's text-only shortcut cap (16–30%) and audio-beats-cascade direction (2508.13992); HearSay's 92.89% gender figure (2601.03783, and note it is a *privacy-leak* benchmark whose queries a refusal-trained deployable model may decline); CP-Bench's full cascade range including the 64.43% arm (2509.16589), which is on closed GPT-4o, not the open frozen omni.
- **Terminology correction:** the "matched-backbone" cascade protocol belongs to Cascade Equivalence (2602.17598), **not** MMAU-Pro; MMAU-Pro's cascade feeds captions/transcripts to an *unmatched* larger text LLM.
