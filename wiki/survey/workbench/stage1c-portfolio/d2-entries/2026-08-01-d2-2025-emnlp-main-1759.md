## 2. 方法全景

### 2.1 一句话骨架

摘要逐字（第 34683 页）：

> "We present **iKnow-audio**, a framework that integrates knowledge graphs with audio-language models to provide robust semantic grounding. iKnow-audio builds on the **Audio-centric Knowledge Graph (AKG)**, which encodes ontological relations comprising semantic, causal, and taxonomic connections reflective of everyday sound scenes and events. By **training knowlege graph embedding models on the AKG** and **refining CLAP predictions through this structured knowledge**, iKnow-audio improves disambiguation of acoustically similar sounds and reduces reliance on prompt engineering."（`knowlege` 为原文拼写，照录）

引言给出的两件套构成（第 34684 页）：

> "iKnow-audio is built on two key components: (i) the **Audio-centric Knowledge Graph (AKG)**, a general-purpose, text-based KG that encodes rich relational information about sounds, and (ii) **CLAP-KG**, a pipeline that refines CLAP predictions using embeddings derived from our proposed AKG."

四项贡献逐字（第 34684 页）：> "(1) iKnow-audio: a novel framework that integrates knowledge graphs with audio-language models for contextual and relational audio understanding. (2) AKG: Audio-centric Knowledge Graph… (3) CLAP-KG: a pipeline that leverages AKG embeddings to refine CLAP predictions. (4) **Systematic zero-shot evaluation on six benchmark datasets**, showing consistent improvements over baseline CLAP."

### 2.2 AKG 的结构（**关系类型逐字核验——任务下达的第一个承重点**）

**任务下达口径为「relation types: semantic/causal/taxonomic」。逐字核验结论：该三元组是【摘要的概括措辞】，不是 AKG 的关系模式；模式本身是【九类】，且其中【没有任何一类字面名为 "semantic"】。二者必须分开引用。**

**（a）摘要层（第 34683 页）逐字**：

> "the Audio-centric Knowledge Graph (AKG), which encodes ontological relations comprising **semantic, causal, and taxonomic** connections reflective of everyday sound scenes and events."

**（b）方法层（§4，第 34687 页）逐字**：

> "The schema comprises **nine high-level relation categories**, each reflecting distinct aspects of auditory context."
> "The full relation schema is detailed in Appendix A.1."

**（c）模式层（Appendix A.1 + Table 3，第 34694–34695 页）——九类全表，逐字照录**：

| # | 类别（Table 3 的 Category 列 / A.1 正文名） | Example Relations（Table 3 逐字） | Purpose（Table 3 逐字） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Co-occurrence & Temporal（A.1：Co-occurrence and Temporal） | `co-occurs with`, `precedes`, `follows`, `overlaps with` | "Capture temporal ordering and co-occurrence of sound events." |
| 2 | **Causal & Functional** | `produces`, `caused by`, `triggers`, `indicates`, `responds to`, `affects` | "Encode causality, function, and event-response dynamics." |
| 3 | **Taxonomic & Hierarchical** | `is a type of`, `has subtype`, `is instance of`, `belongs to class`, `is variant of` | "Structure sound events via type, class, and instance hierarchies." |
| 4 | Environmental（A.1：Spatio-Environmental Relations） | `occurs in`, `can be heard in`, `localized in`, `originates from`, `associated with environment` | "Anchor sound events in physical, spatial, and environmental contexts." |
| 5 | Source & Agent | `emitted by`, `performed by`, `generated by`, `is sound of`, `produced during` | "Link sounds to their generating sources." |
| 6 | Perceptual & Qualitative | `has loudness`, `has pitch`, `has duration`, `has timbre`, `perceived as`, `emotionally associated with` | "Model perceptual properties and subjective qualities of sound." |
| 7 | Cross-modality（A.1：Modality-Crossing relations） | `described by`, `associated with event`, `linked to visual`, `transcribed as` | "Establishes connections to textual or visual modalities." |
| 8 | Intentionality | `invites action`, `used for`, `requires attention`, `warns about` | "Represent expectations, actions, or alerts invoked by sound." |
| 9 | Compositionality（A.1：Scene Composition and Event Structure） | `part of scene`, `scene contains`, `event_composed_of`, `temporal component of`, `entails event` | "Capture hierarchical and compositional structure of scene and events." |

**核验裁定（承重，引用纪律）**：
1. 「**causal**」与「**taxonomic**」在模式层**各有一个同名类**（第 2 类 Causal & Functional、第 3 类 Taxonomic & Hierarchical），**可以逐字对应**。
2. 「**semantic**」在模式层**没有对应类**——Table 3 的九个 Category 名里无此词。摘要的 "semantic" 是对其余七类（时序/环境/来源/感知/跨模态/意图/组合）的**概括词**，Table 3 题注也把整表称作 "Each category defines **semantic** relations"。
3. → **R2 引用时不得写作「AKG 含 semantic / causal / taxonomic 三类关系」**；正确写法为「AKG 的模式为**九类高层关系类别**（其中含 Causal & Functional 与 Taxonomic & Hierarchical 两类），摘要将其概括为 semantic / causal / taxonomic 三个面」。
4. **A.1 正文名与 Table 3 的 Category 名在三处不一致**（Environmental vs Spatio-Environmental、Cross-modality vs Modality-Crossing、Compositionality vs Scene Composition and Event Structure）——**本件以 Table 3 为准并同时记下 A.1 的措辞**，见 §8 内部不一致 I-1。

**（d）实际关系实例数与模式的落差**：Table 4（第 34696 页）逐字给出 `Relations` 列 = **Clean 47 / Noisy 47 / Test 46**。**即模式层是九个类别，但落地的关系类型实际为 47 种**（九类各含若干具体关系，A.1 列举的合计 41 个关系名 + Table 4 前 20 名中出现而 A.1 未列的 `has children` / `has sibling` / `has parent` 等）。→ **引用「九类」时须同时给出「47 种具体关系」，否则量级会被读错一个数量级。**

**（e）三元组形式与实例**：§3.1 逐字——> "Each factual statement is encoded as a triple `(h, r, t) ∈ E × R × E`… For example, the triple **(dish clinking, occurs in, kitchen)** captures a spatial context in which the sound typically appears."
Table 5（第 34698 页）给出 48 条样例，**分两段**：第 1–30 条 "generated by LLM"（如 `vehicle engine — caused by — combustion`、`crying — emotionally associated with — sadness`、`flute — has pitch — high`），第 31–48 条 "derived by SALT"（如 `pigeon dove — belongs to class — bird`、`piano — has subtype — grand piano`）。

### 2.3 AKG 的构造管线与规模

**来源基座逐字（§4，第 34687 页）**：

> "We derive this graph from standardized sound event labels aggregated across **over 27 publicly available datasets**, as cataloged in the **Standardized Audio event Label Taxonomy (SALT)** (Stamatiadis et al., 2024). Our AKG includes entities such as sound-producing sources (e.g., dog, engine), sound events (e.g., barking, idling), and higher-level categorical labels (e.g., domestic animal, vehicle)."

**与通用 KG 的自我定位逐字（§4）**：

> "Unlike general-purpose KGs such as **DBpedia**, **ConceptNet**, and **Wikidata**, which offer limited coverage of everyday sounds and lack fine-grained audio semantics and perceptual grounding, our knowledge graph is tailored for auditory scenes…"

**两路构造 + 两阶段过滤逐字（§4，第 34687 页）**：

> "The AKG triples are generated through **two complementary approaches**: (1) exploiting the **hierarchical structure of the SALT taxonomy** (Figure 3), and (2) **prompting a Large Language Model (LLM)** (Figure 4), both applied to SALT labels. For the LLM-based method, we use **Mistral-7B-Instruct**. The outputs of both methods are merged into an initial **raw AKG containing 51,254 triples**. The subset of LLM-generated triples is then refined through a **two-stage filtering pipeline: an LLM-based plausibility check followed by manual validation**. This process yields a curated set of **20,387 unique, high-quality triples**, which we refer to as the **pruned AKG**. The triples derived directly from the SALT taxonomy **remain unchanged** throughout this process."

**Table 4 上半段逐字（第 34696 页）**：

| Subset | Triples | Relations | Heads | Tails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clean | 18,348 | 47 | 857 | 4,282 |
| Noisy | 49,215 | 47 | 860 | 11,063 |
| Test | 2,039 | 46 | 673 | 1,068 |

**本件复算（论文未给这两条算式）**：
- 18,348 + 2,039 = **20,387** = 正文的 pruned AKG 规模 ✓
- 49,215 + 2,039 = **51,254** = 正文的 raw AKG 规模 ✓
→ 即 Table 4 的 `Clean` / `Noisy` 是**扣除同一个 2,039 条测试划分之后**的 pruned / raw 训练集，两套变体**共用同一测试划分**。**这条对应关系全文未明写，但两条加法逐位吻合，可作为 Table 4 与正文自洽性的交叉验证。**（本件推算，标为推断。）

**LLM 提示词全文可得**：Figure 8（生成）与 Figure 9（校验）在第 34697 页给出完整模板，均可从文本层逐字抽取。生成模板的两步结构逐字：> "Step 1: Reason about the plausibility of generating real-world, common-sense triples… If the relation type is not applicable or would lead to speculative, forced, or non-sensical triples, conclude that no valid triples can be generated. 2. **知识内容的最终来源是 Mistral-7B 的常识生成 + LLM 自校验 + 人工筛**，加上 SALT 分类法的既有层级。**无任何外部事实源（维基/网页/文献/数据库）入图。**
3. **人工介入的比例、规则、抽检率未报**（"manual validation" / "manual post-processing" 两处均无量化）——与 ConEC 的「半自动实体纠错未报错误率」是同型的可审计性缺口。

### 2.4 与 CLAP 的集成（CLAP-KG 三阶段）

**基线定义（§3.2，第 34686 页）**：> "CLAP formulates classification as a **nearest-neighbor retrieval task**, where the predicted label ĉ ∈ C is obtained by maximizing cosine similarity: `ĉ = arg max_{c∈C} sim(φ_A(a), φ_T(c))`"（Eq. 2）；top-k 结果记为 `C_k`。

**三阶段（Figure 2 题注逐字，第 34685 页）**：

> "(a) CLAP initially **misranks** the correct label (e.g., baby) due to acoustic ambiguity with other labels. (b) We **query the AKG using top-k predictions** to retrieve related concepts via relevant relations (e.g., has parent). (c) **Enriched prompts** are compared with the audio embedding, and similarity scores are **aggregated to re-rank predictions**…"

**阶段 b — 链接预测（§3.3，第 34686 页）**：

> "Given a predicted class label ĉ ∈ C_k, we use φ_KG to infer the most semantically plausible tail entities t ∈ E connected to ĉ via **a curated subset of informative relations R_q ⊂ R**."
> "`T_c = {(ĉ, r, t) ∈ T | r ∈ R_q}`, where **R_q ⊂ R is a curated set of relations used for semantic enrichment** (e.g., produces)."
> "We select the **top-m most plausible tails**: `T_c^top = {t*_1, …, t*_m}`"

**阶段 b′ — 提示词拼接（§3.3）**：> "To generate enriched prompts, we **concatenate** each class label ĉ with its associated tail entities t*_i… `p_{ĉ,t*_i} = concat(ĉ, t*_i)`"（Figure 2 的实例：`baby + human sounds`、`cat + domestic animals`、`goat + farm animals`）。

**阶段 c — 打分与聚合（§3.3，Eq. 3–5）**：

> `s(p) = sim(φ_A(a), φ_T(p))`（Eq. 3）
> `s̃(ĉ) = log( exp(s(ĉ)) + Σ_{p∈P_ĉ} exp(s(p)) )`（Eq. 4，**log-sum-exp 融合**）
> `c* = arg max_{ĉ∈C_k} s̃(ĉ)`（Eq. 5）

**Eq. 4 的作用逐字（§3.3，第 34686 页；这是全篇唯一一处「无条件保留原始预测」的机制说明）**：

> "This operation **softly pools evidence across the original and contextualized prompts**, allowing the model to benefit from both raw CLAP predictions and knowledge-enriched signals. **Aggregation in Equation 4 is crucial in striking this balance: without it, performance may degrade due to overreliance on contextual prompts, which risks introducing noise or ambiguity.**"

**Algorithm 1（第 34700 页）给出的完整循环结构**：对每个 `c ∈ C_k`、每个 `r ∈ R_q`，取 `TopM(φ_KG(c, r, ·), m)` → 拼接 → 全部编码 → 逐类聚合 → argmax。**即增强提示词的总数 = k × |R_q| × m，是一个由三个固定超参决定的定值，与样本无关。**

**接口需求（承重）**：全流程只用到 CLAP 的 `φ_A`（音频编码器）与 `φ_T`（文本编码器）的**输出向量**与它们之间的余弦相似度。**未访问 logits、beam、隐状态、注意力**。**但也不是纯文本 API-only**——需要读到 embedding 并自行做相似度与 log-sum-exp 融合，属 **embedding-API 级**。

### 2.5 训练态分层：**什么被训练、什么被冻结**（承重）

| 组件 | 状态 | 逐字支撑（页） |
|---|---|---|
| **CLAP**（`φ_A` / `φ_T`） | **冻结，零权重更新** | "We leverage CLAP (Elizalde et al., 2023), a **pretrained** model that embeds audio and text into a shared representation space."（34686）；§6.3 逐字："Note that in our pipeline, **the KG directly operates on CLAP predictions without further training**."（34691）；结论逐字："The proposed method is **post-hoc and lightweight**, akin to Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), **requiring neither fine-tuning nor prompt engineering** when applied to audio-language models."（34691） |

## 6. DFS 四问

### 6.1 方法核心

把**零样本音频分类**从「标签 ↔ 音频的单点余弦匹配」改成「**在一张音频域知识图上做一跳尾实体预测，用预测出的邻居把标签扩写成若干条上下文化提示词，再把这些提示词与原标签的相似度软融合后重排 top-k**」。三件套：(i) **AKG**——从 SALT 分类法（跨 27+ 公开数据集的标准声音事件标签）与 Mistral-7B 生成的常识三元组合成，经 LLM 可信度校验 + 人工筛，得 20,387 条剪枝三元组 / 47 种关系 / 九类模式；(ii) **KGE（RotatE，PyKEEN 训练）**——把符号图变成可做链接预测的稠密打分器 `φ_KG(h,r,t) = −‖h∘r−t‖₂`；(iii) **CLAP-KG**——冻结 CLAP 上的后处理重排，核心算子是 Eq. 4 的 log-sum-exp 融合。作者自述的定位句（结论，34691）：> "The proposed method is **post-hoc and lightweight, akin to Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)**, requiring neither fine-tuning nor prompt engineering when applied to audio-language models."

### 6.2 自述局限（Limitations and Future Work 全节逐字，第 34692 页）

> "(1) **Shallow and Heuristic Reasoning**: Our approach currently performs only **single-hop reasoning (tail prediction)** over the knowledge graph (AKG) and enriches prompts using **simple string concatenation**. This limits the depth and expressiveness of semantic inference. Future work could explore multi-hop reasoning as relations in the KG space can be chained."
>
> "(2) **Noise and Incompleteness in the AKG**: The AKG was automatically constructed and cleaned, yet it **may still contain noisy, generic, or missing triples**. Additionally, **link prediction from the KGE model can be unreliable for rare or ambiguous events**, potentially introducing irrelevant or spurious concepts into the reasoning process."
>
> "(3) **Limited Evaluation Scope**: We have not evaluated the method on **music** datasets, although the AKG encodes music-related knowledge (through music-related labels from SALT). Extending evaluation to musical audio and broader domains would help assess the generality of the approach."
>
> "(4) **Design and Efficiency Constraints**: The use of **top-k selection** for both CLAP and KG predictions **may not capture the most informative evidence and could be biased toward frequent entities**. Moreover, **inference-time reasoning introduces additional computational overhead (through a beam search)**. Future work may explore alternative sampling strategies and efficiency optimizations."

**自述局限之外、本件逐表核对新增的六条（论文未列）**：
- (i) **重排天花板**：`c* = arg max_{ĉ∈C_k}` 把可达空间钉死在 CLAP 的 top-k 内，**正确标签不在 top-k 即不可挽回**；论文从未把它写成上限。
- (ii) **Hit@5 的零 delta 是恒等式而非结果**（§4.2 读数 1）；论文给的是语义解释，未给结构解释。
- (iii) **`+KG-agg` 在多格上净负于不注入**（§4.2 读数 4）——论文只说它"underperforms on FSD50K and AudioSet"，实际 Hit@5 行**六个载体中五个净负**（US8K/TUT/FSD50K/AudioSet/DCASE），跌幅最大 −11.7 pp（DCASE 91.2→79.5）。
- (iv) **`k`、`m`、`R_q` 三个决定管线全部行为的超参，全文未报取值**；KGE 网格搜索的**选中超参亦未报**；CLAP **检查点版本未报**。→ 已发表数字**不可复算**（与 ConEC 的 λ 未报值同型）。
- (v) **人工筛的比例、规则、抽检率未报**，故"20,387 unique, high-quality triples"中"high-quality"一词**无可审计的定义**。
- (vi) **AKG 的构造语料与评测载体在标签空间上同源**：AKG 的实体来自 SALT，而 SALT 是"aggregated across over 27 publicly available datasets"的标准标签集；六个评测载体的类名也用 SALT 标签作提示词（§5.2）。→ **知识与评测标签空间不是独立的两个来源**；论文未讨论这一同源性对增益归因的影响。（本件观察，标为新发现。）

### 6.3 改进空间

按与 R2 的相关度排序，逐条给出论文中的可证伪抓手：

1. **关系选择从「离线策展」升为「按样本选择」是论文自己指出的空格**：`R_q` 全程固定，而 §6.2 明说 "The most impactful relations, however, **vary by dataset** and are often content-specific"，且 §6.2 把退化类的候选归因之一写成 "**querying the KGE model with suboptimal relations**"。→ **「哪些关系该用」在本篇是一个已被作者点名、但未做任何选择机制的面。**
2. **准入通道在数据模型里已存在但未接通**：`φ_KG` 对每个候选尾实体都产出一个可比的 plausibility 分数，管线却只用它排序取 top-m，**从不设阈值、不拒绝、不弃权**。把该分数接入准入/加权，是一处论文自身已备好、实验未触及的改动面。
3. **`+KG-agg` 净负格是现成的门控实验位**：同一载体上已有 no-injection 参照臂（CLAP 列）与无条件注入臂（`+KG-agg` 列），**缺的正是「按样本决定注不注入」的第三臂**。
4. **多跳推理**：Limitations (1) 自列。
5. **提示词构造**：Limitations (1) 自认为 "simple string concatenation"；把尾实体组织成句子/结构化描述而非拼接，是与 ConEC 的「词袋不是语料约束」同型的改动面。
6. **可复算性补齐**：报出 `k` / `m` / `R_q` / 选中的 KGE 超参 / CLAP 检查点版本，是把本篇数字变成可校准锚的最低门槛。

### 6.4 R2 可借鉴部分

1. **Eq. 4 的 log-sum-exp 软融合，及其消融（`+KG-agg` 列）**是本篇最干净的一处设计：它把「注入的证据」与「原始模型信念」放在同一分数尺度上做**无条件软池化**，且论文用一条完整的消融列证明了**去掉它就会跌破不注入**。**这是一个「注入必须与原信号融合、不能替换」的可移植算子 + 可移植消融设计**，R2 的注入臂可直接采用这一对（融合臂 / 纯注入臂）结构。
2. **「三列并置」的表结构（baseline / 去关键组件 / 完整）**在六个载体上同时给出，使「增益来自注入」与「增益来自融合算子」可被分离。R2 的臂结构可采用同一分离原则。
3. **策展 KG 的两路构造 + 两阶段过滤协议**（分类法层级路 + LLM 生成路；LLM 可信度校验 + 人工筛）是一份可复用的知识层构造规格，且**两个提示词模板随文全文给出**（Figure 8/9）——这在读集内是少见的完整度。
4. **raw vs pruned 双变体对照**（Table 1）给出了一个可移植的读数：**知识层的清洗质量对下游链接预测的影响，可以在不动下游模型的情况下单独量化**（RotatE MRR 49.5 → 56.1）。R2 若自建知识层，可复用这一「知识质量单独消融」的位置。
5. **逐类双向报告**（改善 5 类 / 退化 5 类，并配 UMAP 定性诊断）是一种成熟的「整体增益是净值」的呈现纪律，避免只报聚合增益。

### 8.3 可信度小结（不作方向性裁定）

- **可直接引用的强项**：`Table 1` 的 KGE 选型与 raw→pruned 提升（RotatE MRR 49.5→56.1）；`Table 2` 的六载体 Hit@1 / MRR delta（TUT2017 +10.1 / DCASE17-T4 +8.2 为最大两格）；`Table 2` 的 `+KG-agg` 消融方向（去融合即多格净负）；`Table 3` 的九类关系模式全表；Limitations 四条自述。这五组均有表体或正文直接支撑、无跨表冲突。
