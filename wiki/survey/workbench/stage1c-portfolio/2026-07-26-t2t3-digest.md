## Task family 1 — Closed-option audio/omni reasoning (MMAU / MMAR / MMSU / SAKURA / AQUA-Bench / Audio-Hallucination / MMAU-Pro)

Baseline ladder (frozen core → prompted wrapper → learned/RL gate), MMAU Test-mini, Qwen2-Audio-7B (AuTAgent Table 2):
Vanilla 50.00 → Random-select 49.10 → All-Tools 40.90 (**below vanilla**) → Tools-w/-Desc (prompted) 51.20 → AuTAgent (RL-trained gate) 54.20. Single-tool oracle = 66.1 (not a strict upper bound — excludes no-tool action).

MMAR (ARC leaderboard, 1000 items): direct readout Qwen3-Omni-Thinking 58.41 Rubrics/69.90 Acc (VISA-self-tested) — two circulating numbers for the 30B Thinking model (66.4 TalTech-cited vs 69.9 VISA-self-tested), plus 68.1 for the distinct Flash-Thinking API variant, non-portable, must re-run locally. Agent 1st (TalTech) 69.83/76.90; Agent 2nd (VISA) 66.23/77.40.

AudioProcessBench decision-utility (chain-level aggregation over MMAU-Pro+MMAR+MMSU traces): generator mean accuracy 46.64%, random-aggregator 44.1, best critic (Gemini-3-Flash) chain Avg 60.5 — but step-verification spread (51.5pts) compresses 5x into chain-selection spread (9.5pts); **no oracle/headroom row published** in this benchmark (flagged gap).

Uncertainty/abstention signals (MMAU/MMAR/MMSU/SAKURA/AQUA-Bench/Audio-Hallucination, 2604.25591): discrete semantic entropy and semantic entropy (both black-box-legal in counts-only form) are the consistently strongest AUROC/AURAC signals; P(True) self-verification is competitive but collapses to chance (0.52) or below (0.44) on weaker cores/harder benchmarks.

## Task family 2 — Audio-visual multi-hop / omni topology (Music-AVQA / AVHBench / OmniBench / DailyOmni / WorldSense / MOV-Bench / OmniVideoBench / MSU-Bench / LongShOTBench)

Chain of Modality (2604.14520) fixed-topology table is the standard ablation control: CoM's learned router **loses** to the single best fixed topology on 3/6 of the 6 audio-visual cells in 2604.14520 Table 6 (Music-AVQA A-V, AVHBench A-Hal, DailyOmni). WorldSense is protocol-conditioned: MCQ favors direct readout (54.0/52.65 vs agent 46.56, LongShOTAgent); 2512.16978's actual open-ended (options-stripped) protocol gives agent 43.4 > Qwen3-Omni-Instruct 41.7 > Qwen3-Omni-Thinking 33.6 (deltas -3.2/-12.3/-19.1); the 61.2-vs-54.0 figure is a *different paper* (2512.23646, standard MCQ protocol, frontier o3 brain, no open-ended arm), not an open-ended reversal of the same system — always run both protocols and keep the two papers separate.

LongShOTBench (274 videos, ~188h, open-ended weighted rubric): LongShOTAgent 66.64 vs Qwen3-Omni-30B-Thinking direct 64.05 (**+2.59, thin margin**, uninstrumented for cost). Options-stripped protocol shows MCQ inflates omni-model scores by 13-20pp relative to open-ended.

AOP-Agent / MOV-Bench (519 items): on the MOV-long subset the reflector alone carries +10.6 of the +15.35 total gain; the all-item gain is +9.83 (do not pair the subset-specific component delta with the all-item total). Two independently re-implemented prior agentic frameworks (OmniAgent, ActiveVideoPerception) land **14.5 and 17.5 points BELOW** direct inference on the same backbone.

## Task family 3 — ASR / speech core loops (GigaSpeech / WenetSpeech / ASRU2019 / AISHELL-NER / CS-Dialogue)

S²ER (semantic-equivalence judge) is the field's strongest evidence that token-level metrics under-report wrapper gains: CS-Dialogue S²ER 19.73→4.16 (4.7x) while MER moves only 14.44→13.28 (~8% relative). **Not comparable across papers** — same group reports Loop-0 GigaSpeech S²ER as 14.12 (conference) vs 21.47 (journal) on identical data; the metric is judge-protocol-dependent.

Every reported Interactive/Agentic-ASR trajectory is **gold-conditioned** (stop decision owned by a judge that consumes Y_GT, correction utterances authored from gold) — treat as an oracle ceiling, never a deployable number.

## Task family 4 — Speech generation self-verification (TTS BoN / catastrophic-failure detection)

CFR_N (catastrophic-failure-rate, explicit dropout/collapse oracle) on hard prompts: 0.269 (N=1) → 0.000 (N≥4); LibriSpeech test-clean: 0.058 → 0.000 (N=2). Authors **demote the BoN column to an oracle upper bound** because the selector (WER-vs-reference) is the same function as the reported metric — the missing row across the whole D5 corpus is a reference-free deployable selector.

## Task family 5 — Tool/skill selection (Dynamic-SUPERB / AudioRAG-500 / AVQA)

Boundary ladder on Dynamic-SUPERB and MMAU: prompted gating over no-tool = +2.3 to +5.5; learned (RL) gating over prompted = +3.0 to +4.3 (AuTAgent, same tool library and frozen core — costs 8×A100, 200 GRPO steps, ~2,025 gold-labelled items). Indiscriminate supply is actively **harmful**: All-Tools sits 5.7–9.1 points below no-tool.

## Task family 6 — Reward-model / preference benchmarks (VL-RewardBench / MM-RewardBench / Audio-HH-RLHF / Omni-RewardBench-TA2T / Clotho-AQA)

Omni-RRM is the closest published degenerate single-step case with audio: BoN@N=10 on AVQA audio-only MC, 77.7→80.3 (+2.6) over Self-consistency@N, at 10x generation cost + 9 rationale-generating RM passes. Audio-HH-RLHF is explicitly flagged by its own authors as TTS-wrapped **text** preference, not real acoustic evidence.

## Task family 7 — Multilingual open-output speech portfolio (MCIF / KIT reranking)

The single most load-bearing headroom table in the whole corpus: oracle vs greedy over 17 candidates × 4 tasks — ASR −32.10 WER, SQA +14.42, SSUM +3.85, ST +6.11 (mean +14.12) — against a **best realized policy of +3.71 (26% recovery)**, with 8 of 10 tested strategies net WORSE than plain greedy. MBR (zero model calls) is the cheapest black-box-legal signal and the only strategy with 0% spurious-candidate selection.

## Task family 8 — Text-domain construct/mechanism donors (WebArena / Jericho / MATH500 / GSM8K / HumanEval / GPQA-Diamond / HLE-Verified / LiveCodeBench / BabyVision / FinTabNet / OmniDocBench)

Non-audio. Kept in T2 only as method/protocol donors (JitRL, ETS, ATLAS mechanisms; 2607.13347's recovery-metric and harm-accounting instrument kit). FinTabNet/OmniDocBench deliver the sharpest possible negative result: on FinTabNet, **every judge policy has negative recovery** and the do-nothing baseline beats all of them.

## Task family 9 — LALM/ALM-as-judge reliability (AudioJudge / SpeakerSleuth / ParaPairAudioBench / TRACE / S2S-Arena / MTalk-Bench / full-duplex judge)

- AudioJudge: multi-aspect ensemble up to 0.912 Spearman with human system-ranking preference (SpeakBench, Gemini-2.5-Flash); ChatbotArena-Spoken (a separate, lexical-content benchmark) 0.902 zero-shot -> 0.931 with ICL (GPT-4o-Audio); human inter-annotator accuracy itself only 69.5–83.0%.
- SpeakerSleuth: 12 LALMs tested; models **prioritize textual coherence over acoustic evidence** — performance collapses (fails to detect obvious gender switches) when interlocutor turns are given as text.
- ParaPairAudioBench: LALM judges lag human by 32pp average; severe calibration failure on Tie/abstain cases (best model Tie-accuracy 18-19% on Style/Emphasis vs human 85.3/76.0, rising to 35-43% on Age/Gender where human Tie-accuracy itself falls to 38.7 on Age); position bias up to 29.4pp.
- TRACE: cheap textual-blueprint LLM judge beats both ALM judges and transcript-only LLM judges on agreement, at lower cost (training-free, reference-free at inference).
- MTalk-Bench: LLM-as-judge reliable on nonverbal (paralinguistic) evaluation **only when text annotations are also provided**.
- Full-duplex production judge (Gemini family, Salesforce, internal/not released): the most rigorous multi-rater validation in the corpus — per-dimension substitutability, not global; model swaps must re-validate calibration separately from rank-correlation.

3. **S²ER Loop-0**: 14.12 vs 21.47 (same group, same data, different paper version) — judge-protocol-dependent, not a model property.
4. Verification-lane discipline: our local llama.cpp Qwen3-Omni-30B lane is explicitly a **weak-core** verification lane. ATLAS's own cross-benchmark evidence on weak-vs-strong-core asymmetry is direction-unstable (2/3 benchmarks favor weak-core gain, HLE-Verified reverses) — no lift measured on the verification lane may be extrapolated as a strong-core prediction.
