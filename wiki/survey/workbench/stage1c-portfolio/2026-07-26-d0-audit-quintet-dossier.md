## arXiv:2509.16971 — AudioGenie-Reasoner: A Training-Free Multi-Agent Framework for Coarse-to-Fine Audio Deep Reasoning

**方法面**：Claim A [SUPPORTED] — training-free (no core-weight update) audio reasoning agent/system doing evidence acquisition and iterative coarse-to-fine evidence refinement with a sufficiency-based stop route. Evidence (verbatim): "the first unified training-free multi-agent system that coordinates perception and reasoning over an evolving chain of textual evidence" ... "a novel proactive iterative document refinement loop, featuring tool-augmented routes and specialized agents, to continuously search for missing information and augment the evidence chain in a coarse-to-fine manner until sufficient question-related information is gathered"; "The agent returns a status flag $s \in\{\text{Sufficient}, \text{Insufficient}\}$"; "Once the iterative refinement loop concludes (i.e., when $s=\text{Sufficient}$) or the maximum number of iterations is reached"; "The plan outlines one of three tool-based actions: audio question-answering, guided re-captioning, or automatic speech recognition."; "The max number of iterations is set to three." (Abstract; Sec. "Our Method" §Proactive Iterative Document Refinement Loop; Sec. Experimental Setup/Implementation Details.)

Claim C [PARTIAL] — signal-bearing components inventory. Evidence: only two exist. (1) A stop/continue evaluator: "assesses if the current document contains sufficient evidence to confidently answer the question... returns a status flag $s\in\{$Sufficient, Insufficient$\}$" — routes evidence acquisition, not answer choice. (2) An emitted-but-unused confidence: "$\left(A^{*}, S_{c}, R\right)=\mathcal{F}_{\text{answer}}(D_{f},Q,L)$ where $A^{*}$, $S_{c}$, and $R$ represent the final selected answer, the associated confidence score, and a detailed textual rationale" — $S_c$ is never consumed by any threshold, re-selection, or abstain rule and is never evaluated anywhere in the paper. No candidate selection / reranking / best-of-N over complete answers, no repair/revision of a produced answer (refinement targets the evidence document $D_i$, not the answer), no abstention. (Sec. "Our Method" Eqs. 2 and 5; Sec. Experimental Setup §Datasets.)

**自述与显见局限**：Claim B [PARTIAL] — occupies the broad training-free audio-agent surface. Evidence: "To the best of our knowledge, this is the first exploration of MAS in audio deep reasoning." — self-claimed scope is audio deep reasoning (closed-form MCQ), not audio agents broadly; the system is one ALLM + one proprietary LLM + three fixed tool routes, single trajectory, max 3 iterations, evaluated only on MMAU-mini and MMAR. (Introduction, contribution (1); Sec. Experimental Setup.)

Claim C sub-claim [PARTIAL] — is it a SPEECH agent / nearest direct speech-agent prior. Evidence: "MMAU-mini consists of 1,000 closed-form questions covering three audio types: sound, music, and speech." Speech is one dimension among sound/music/speech and their mixtures; no speech-specific task, metric, or ablation exists, and after the initial captioning step the agents operate on text ("transforms the audio reasoning task into a complex text understanding problem"). (Sec. Experimental Setup §Datasets; Sec. Our Method §Paradigm Shift.)

Claim D [SUPPORTED] — authors' own stated limitations/future work. Evidence: "Future work will focus on enhancing signal-level reasoning by developing more specialized evidence generators for low-level acoustic cues." (No Limitations section; two richer future-work blocks are present in the LaTeX but commented out, including "explore learning-based policies for agents to further optimize the iterative process".) (Sec. "Conclusion and Discussion".)

Notes (evident, not self-owned): the stop rule over-refines — "With four rounds, performance drops on both datasets, likely because extra rounds introduce noise and irrelevant cues" (best round is 2 on MMAU-mini, 3 on MMAR, i.e. dataset-dependent and effectively chosen in hindsight). "Training-free" means no weight update, but the reasoning core is GPT-4o-2024-08-06 (proprietary API), and the only agent-LLM ablation swaps it for GPT-3.5-turbo — never for a local/open LLM, so no frozen-open-model ceiling is established. GPT-4o is also used to post-process raw outputs before scoring, for AGR and for baselines. Headline framing is "SOTA over existing open-source models": on MMAR AGR scores 58.85 avg, below Gemini-2.0-Flash (64.86) and Gemini-2.5-Flash (63.43/67.07); and AGR's MMAR number is computed on 905 samples while table-mates are quoted numbers presumably on the full set, so the comparison is not strictly like-for-like.

**改进空间**：No Limitations section exists. The only live future-work sentence is: "Future work will focus on enhancing signal-level reasoning by developing more specialized evidence generators for low-level acoustic cues." (Conclusion and Discussion). Two further future-work blocks exist in the LaTeX source but are COMMENTED OUT and therefore not in the published paper: (a) "We see room to strengthen signal-level cases such as counting and temporal structure, fine acoustic differences, and audio quality... semantic tasks benefit from transcription, while the signal side lacks equally mature evidence generators"; (b) "Future work will explore learning-based policies for agents to further optimize the iterative process, potentially improving both efficiency and accuracy." Additional un-owned weakness the paper reports but does not name as a limitation: the stop rule over-refines — "With four rounds, performance drops on both datasets, likely because extra rounds introduce noise and irrelevant cues" (best round is 2 on MMAU-mini, 3 on MMAR, i.e. dataset-dependent and effectively chosen in hindsight). Code is promised, not confirmed: "The code will be available at https://github.com/ryysayhi/AudioGenie-Reasoner."

**可借鉴/完备度**：Mechanism components explicitly described in the audit evidence: sufficiency-based binary stop/continue evaluator (status flag Sufficient/Insufficient) gating the refinement loop, not the answer; three fixed tool-based actions (audio question-answering, guided re-captioning, automatic speech recognition/Whisper) as the acquisition action space; max-iteration cap of 3; an emitted-but-unconsumed confidence score $S_c$ alongside the final answer and rationale — flagged in the audit as a signal that exists but is never thresholded, used for re-selection, or evaluated. Reproducibility: code "will be available" at the stated GitHub URL, not confirmed live in this audit. Source of record used for this audit: the arXiv LaTeX `.eprint` (PDF rendering unavailable in that environment); both files were fetched the same day per the audit note.

**域界定**：Audio (not speech-only). Real audio experiments on two audio-reasoning MCQ benchmarks: MMAU-mini (1,000 closed-form questions over sound/music/speech) and MMAR (authors could only collect 905 of 1,000 samples). Speech is one of three audio types plus mixtures; there is no speech-specific task (no ASR/WER, no S2TT, no emotion regression, no open-ended generation). Internally the pipeline converts audio to text once and then reasons in text space, so the LLM agents themselves are text-only; audio is re-touched only through tool calls (AQA / re-captioning / Whisper ASR).

## arXiv:2510.02995 — AudioToolAgent: An Agentic Framework for Audio-Language Models

And: "When the agent queries multiple tools and receives conflicting outputs, it triggers targeted re-queries... Claim B part 3 [SUPPORTED] — reported evaluation. Evidence: "Experiments with MMAU, MMAR, and MMAU-Pro show state-of-the-art accuracy: up to 77.50% in MMAU, 77.00% in MMAR, and 61.90% in MMAU-Pro. ## arXiv:2603.09714 — MUGEN: Evaluating and Improving Multi-audio Understanding of Large Audio-Language Models

**方法面**：Claim A [SUPPORTED] — recorded title match. Evidence: "\title{MUGEN: Evaluating and Improving Multi-audio Understanding of Large Audio-Language Models}" — exact match, with the acronym expanded in text as "MUGEN (Multi-audio Grounding and Understanding Benchmark)". (Title, camera-ready; Introduction.)

Claim B [SUPPORTED] — fixed-K audio-permutation majority/consensus aggregation that changes final answers; self-consistency consensus, not an external evaluator or scalar reward. Evidence: "Before each inference, we randomly permute the order of audio candidates to reduce positional sensitivity... The model generates responses under these permutations, which are mapped back to the original indexing system and aggregated via majority voting." "Both methods generate 10 responses for majority voting. In SC, all responses are sampled under the original audio order, whereas APSC generates one response for each of the 10 permutations, maintaining the same number of generations and comparable computational cost." Answers change: APSC+CoT reaches 31.31 (+2.62) / 74.40 (+6.74) / 75.26 (+5.66). Aggregation is unweighted majority vote over the model's own samples — no reward model, no verifier, no external scoring signal. (Sec. 5.1 Methodology, Improvement Strategies; Sec. 5.2 Results; Table 3, tab:improve_table_diff.)

**自述与显见局限**：Claim C [PARTIAL] — occupies the "fixed-K consensus" surface for audio LLMs. Evidence: occupancy is real but narrow — K is fixed at 10 with no sweep, the aggregation is plain unweighted majority voting, and the study covers only "Qwen2.5-Omni and Gemini-3-pro (both thinking levels), the strongest models in Table~\ref{tab:model_performance}" on a single self-authored benchmark of 5/6-way audio-as-option MCQ. Plain SC actually hurts the open model (28.69 -> 28.00, -0.69), so what is demonstrated is permutation-debiasing under a fixed budget, not consensus selection in general. Cost is acknowledged, not optimised: "producing multiple generations per instance incurs considerable computational overhead." (Sec. 4.2 / Sec. 5.1-5.2; Table 3.)

Claim D [PARTIAL] — stated limitations/future work. Evidence: no Limitations or Future Work section exists; only in-line acknowledgements: "producing multiple generations per instance incurs considerable computational overhead"; "CoT yields minimal or even negative impacts"; "targeted training strategies or architectural adjustments may be required to strengthen multi-audio understanding"; "even the strongest model remains far from perfect." (Sec. 4.1 Main Results; Sec. 5.2 Results; Sec. 6 Conclusion.)

Notes (evident, not self-owned): two abstracts are present in the source; the first is commented out and quotes 6.74% for APSC alone — the live abstract correctly attributes 6.28% to APSC and 6.74% to APSC+CoT. The open-source model gain is tiny and near chance — Qwen2.5-Omni goes 28.69 -> 30.69 (APSC) -> 31.31 (APSC+CoT) against 20% chance on 5 options; plain SC is NEGATIVE for it (-0.69), so the benefit is specifically the permutation (a positional-bias debias), not consensus per se. All large gains (+5.37 to +6.74) are on the proprietary Gemini-3-pro. A Claude Haiku 4.5 LLM-as-a-judge is used, but only for answer extraction/grading (99% human agreement on 400 items) — it is NOT in the APSC decision path and must not be read as an external evaluator driving selection.

**改进空间**：There is no dedicated Limitations or Future Work section (Interspeech format). Owned costs/limits: "while this method achieves significant improvements, producing multiple generations per instance incurs considerable computational overhead"; "CoT yields minimal or even negative impacts... the primary challenge lies in acoustic perception and cross-audio comparison, rather than a deficiency in logical reasoning"; "targeted training strategies or architectural adjustments may be required to strengthen multi-audio understanding"; "even the strongest model remains far from perfect, indicating that multi-audio understanding remains an open challenge". Un-owned but visible gaps: no sweep over K (K is fixed at 10), no accuracy-vs-cost curve, no weighted or reward-based aggregation, no abstention, and the improvement study covers only two model families (Qwen2.5-Omni and Gemini-3-pro at two thinking levels), not the seven benchmarked LALMs.

**可借鉴/完备度**：Benchmark and code at https://github.com/danielqwer/MUGEN (camera-ready Interspeech class). 35 audio-grounding tasks / 1,750 instances / 7 dimensions design with an audio-as-option format (five candidates per item, six with a reference audio); cascaded ASR+LLM baseline (Whisper-large-v3 + Gemini-3-pro) used as a semantic-shortcut control. Audio-permutation self-consistency (APSC) protocol: fixed K=10, permute audio-candidate order before each inference, map answers back to original indexing, majority-vote — reported as debiasing positional sensitivity, distinct from plain self-consistency (SC), which is measured as a separate arm and found negative for the open model. Answer extraction/grading via Claude Haiku 4.5 LLM-as-a-judge with 99% human agreement reported on 400 items (grading only, not in the decision path).

**域界定**：Fully speech/audio. 35 audio-grounding tasks, 1,750 instances, 7 dimensions across speech, general audio and music, with an audio-as-option design: five audio candidates per item (six when a reference audio is present), so all options are audio signals, not text. Includes a cascaded ASR+LLM baseline (Whisper-large-v3 + Gemini-3-pro) as a semantic-shortcut control. Real speech corpora plus TTS-synthesised items. All tasks are multiple-choice; accuracy is the sole metric.

## 2607.11433 — Omni-Decision: A Progressive Evidence-State Agent System for Omni-Modal QA

But the measured runtime shows the opposite exit: "Stuck (\texttt{unresolved\_block}): the critic identifies an evidence gap, but the agent cannot close it ... and eventually exits with a forced answer or budget termination" (152/857 cases, 21.7% accuracy). ## 2606.15141 — EChO-Agent: Evidence Chain Orchestration Agent for Audio Reasoning

**方法面**：Earlier-project-record claim [PARTIAL] — "frozen Qwen3-Omni organized as Tool -> Evidence -> Reason -> Verify audio reasoning workflow". Evidence: the workflow name is exact — "a four-stage pipeline: \textbf{Tool $\rightarrow$ Evidence $\rightarrow$ Reason $\rightarrow$ Verify}" — but the system is a TWO-model hybrid, not an organized frozen Qwen3-Omni: "we employ \textbf{DeepSeek-V3} as the evidence constructor" and "a LLM-based orchestrator agent $\mathcal{M}_{\text{L}}$ ... dispatches tool calls ... and manages verification", while Qwen-3-Omni-Instruct is only the reasoning stage $\mathcal{M}_{\text{A}}$. The paper never uses the words "frozen" or "training-free". (Sec. 1; Sec. 3.1 Task Formulation; Sec. 3.3 LLM-Based Evidence Integration; Sec. 3.4.)

Claim A — verification GATES actions [CONTRADICTED]. Evidence: verification gates only the final output, never tool actions: the tool stage is fixed in advance — "We adopt a \emph{question-type-conditioned static dispatch} strategy: the orchestrator analyzes $q$ and selects a predefined tool combination based on the question, eliminating variance from stochastic tool selection." The verifier's three checks are format compliance ("repaired via rule-based post-processing"), reasoning-answer consistency, and dual-pass arbitration — all post-hoc on candidate answers.
