### 2603.05231 — Boosting ASR Robustness via Test-Time Reinforcement Learning with Audio-Text Semantic Rewards (ASR-TRA)

**方法面（method surface）**：Per-utterance test-time adaptation (TTA) of Whisper, framed via a Structural Causal Model A,P->Y->R (Fig. 3). Algorithm 1 is exact: (1) greedy decode y0 at temperature 0 and score r0=CLAP(y0,s); (2) prepend a learnable soft prompt p in R^{L x d} (L=4, d=384 => 1,536 params) to the decoder input embeddings before <bos>, framed as do(p) causal intervention (Eq. 3); (3) sample n=4 candidates with temperature t_i ~ U(0.4,0.6) (Eq. 4); (4) score each with CLAP audio-text cosine similarity (Eq. 5); (5) advantage adv_i = r_i - mean_j({r_j}, j=0..n) (Eq. 7); (6) REINFORCE loss L = -(1/N) sum grad log P_theta(y_i|s,p) * (r_i - rbar) (Eq. 8); (7) UPDATE BOTH the Whisper weights theta (lr 1e-6..1e-5) and the prompt p (100x larger lr); (8) re-decode the adapted output; (9) RESTORE theta and p to their original state so updates never accumulate across samples (Alg. 1 lines 12-14, 'Optimization via Reinforcement Learning' final paragraph). Signals used: external CLAP audio-text similarity (primary) plus optional DeepSeek-V3 text scoring (ablation Table 3). Decision rights actually exercised: NONE of select/repair/stop/abstain/allocate - the n=4 candidates are consumed only as gradient samples and the final answer is a fresh re-decode, not an argmax over candidates; candidate count, temperature range, and update-step count are fixed constants. Core is NOT frozen (temporarily unfrozen, then restored); gradients, sequence log-probs, and weight access are all required. Tasks/benchmarks: ASR only - Whisper-Tiny (39M) and Whisper-Base (74M) on LibriSpeech test-other + MS-SNSD 8 noise types at 10 dB SNR, and L2-Arctic accented English (six L1s). Metrics: WER and per-utterance latency (s).

**自述与显见局限**：Stated (Discussion, 'Two limitations remain'): (i) reward-model constraints - LLM-based reward dominates inference latency in the hybrid setting (7-9x overhead per Table 3), and CLAP supports mainly English audio-text similarity, limiting multilingual use; (ii) the framework handles single-utterance adaptation only, with streaming/conversational extension and 'implicit few-shot learning where temporally accumulated feedback across utterances provides supervision' named as future work. Reward fidelity: the authors report CLAP score vs ground-truth WER Spearman rho = -0.431 (Ablation, 'Reward Modeling') and present it as evidence of the reward's reliability ('supporting its reliability as a semantic reward'); our own judgment that |rho|=0.43 is weak for a controller, and the absence of any reward-hacking analysis, is OUR assessment, not a self-reported limitation. 【复核更正 wf_5f1f5801】 Evident but unstated: (a) no ablation of gradient-step-vs-selection - the paper never tests picking the argmax-CLAP candidate from its own n=4 pool, so the necessity of the gradient update is unmeasured; (b) per-condition regressions with no guard - Korean L2-Arctic 13.42 baseline -> 13.76 ours (worse than no adaptation), and SUTA beats it on NB (33.75 vs 35.81) while SGEM beats it on SD (19.50 vs 22.34) and Hindi/Korean; (c) tiny-model regime only, no LLM-scale or omni core; (d) single run, no CIs or seeds, single RTX 6000 Ada; (e) no reward-hacking analysis despite rho=-0.431; (f) LoRA reported as giving negligible speedup because decoding+reward dominate runtime, so the cost model is never turned into a budget policy.

**改进空间（方向级台阶）**：(1) The exact gradient-free counterpart is a single missing table row: replace the REINFORCE step with reward-ranked selection over the same n=4 pool plus wrapper-state search (prompt text, temperature schedule, retrieval context) - their own Table 3 'prompt-only + CLAP' row (42.75 Tiny / 37.45 Base) is nearly that variant minus selection, and the released code makes the comparison cheap. (2) Budget-aware allocation: n=4 and t~U(0.4,0.6) are constants; per-utterance allocation keyed on the reward margin (their adv_i = r_i - rbar is already computed and thrown away after the gradient step) is untouched. (3) Abstain / no-regression guard: keep the y0 direct readout unless the reward margin over it exceeds a threshold - this directly targets the Korean/NB/SD regressions and is the mechanism that converts a sometimes-worse wrapper into a never-worse one. (4) Cross-utterance memory: the authors themselves name persistent, context-aware accumulation across utterances as open; nothing in the method carries state (theta and p are explicitly restored). (5) Reward provenance guarding: one CLAP scalar at rho=-0.431 is a hackable proxy; committees (CLAP + LM + ASR-agreement + round-trip TTS verification) with disagreement-triggered escalation are open. (6) Scale/contract transfer: whether an external audio-text reward still steers behavior when the core is a genuinely frozen omni LLM (no weights, no log-probs) is untested here and is exactly the substitution our program needs.

**可借鉴**：Code: https://github.com/fangcq/ASR-TRA (license not stated in the paper). Benchmark recipes with numbers to beat: LibriSpeech test-other x MS-SNSD 8 noise types (AC/AA/BA/CM/MU/NB/SD/TP) at 10 dB SNR - Whisper-Tiny baseline mean WER 32.71, SUTA 32.27 (latency 1.690 s), SGEM 30.22 (0.775 s), ASR-TRA 28.64 (0.720 s) (Table 1); L2-Arctic six L1s - baseline 32.11, SUTA 32.59, SGEM 31.40, ASR-TRA 28.21, with Arabic 32.74->22.92 and Vietnamese 61.21->53.84 (Table 2); ablation grid on Gaussian-noise LibriSpeech test-other (Table 3): no-adapt 45.06 Tiny / 40.25 Base; finetune+CLAP 40.49/35.97; prompt+CLAP 42.75/37.45; finetune+prompt+CLAP 36.65/33.42 at 0.489/0.580 s; +LLM 36.35/30.24 at 4.191/4.355 s; CLAP+LLM 35.49/30.28 at 4.365/4.367 s. Diagnostics worth adopting wholesale: the 'blind confidence' probe - top-100 highest-confidence noisy test-other samples where baseline WER is 83.61, SUTA degrades to 122.37, SGEM 63.00, ASR-TRA 45.17 (a clean stress test for any confidence-independent controller); and the reward-fidelity audit metric (Spearman rho between reward and the true metric). Also reusable: per-utterance latency (s/utt) as the budget unit, SUTA/SGEM as gradient-based TTA baselines, the group-relative baseline rbar as a selection/stopping statistic rather than a gradient coefficient, and the y0 greedy anchor kept inside the reward pool as a built-in fallback.

**资产完备度**：Code released at github.com/fangcq/ASR-TRA; no license, no checkpoint, and no data release stated in the text. All assets are public and free: Whisper-Tiny/Base, CLAP (Elizalde et al. 2023), LibriSpeech test-other, MS-SNSD, L2-Arctic (L2-ARCTIC requires a usage agreement). Numbers are single-run means with no CIs or seed counts, so treat every delta as un-intervalled. Pin: arXiv:2603.05231v1 (5 Mar 2026, cs.SD, AAAI-2026 copyright block); pin the ASR-TRA repo by commit SHA; pin Whisper and CLAP checkpoint revisions; DeepSeek-V3 is used as an LLM reward via API, so that row is not reproducible bit-for-bit.

**域界定**：Speech-only ASR over a small encoder-decoder (Whisper 39M/74M); no omni, no LLM-scale core, no speech generation. Transfers to speech-omni: the external ground-truth-free reward source (CLAP audio-text alignment), the noise/accent distribution-shift benchmark suite, per-utterance latency accounting, the blind-confidence subset diagnostic, and the reward-fidelity Spearman audit. Does NOT transfer under our black-box contract: prompt-embedding intervention plus REINFORCE (requires weights, gradients, and sequence log-probs), any conclusion drawn from the 39M/74M regime, and WER-only reporting (token-level metric, known to under-report wrapper gains).

**备注**：This is the nearest speech-domain occupant of the reward-as-signal idea but is disqualified as a comparable under the frozen/black-box contract: it updates theta and p by policy gradient every utterance. It is best cited as the gradient-based prior-art anchor to match or beat without gradients on its own benchmark. Note also that it exercises zero explicit decision rights - it is reward-driven learning, not reward-driven control.

### 2602.00846 — Omni-RRM: Advancing Omni Reward Modeling via Automatic Rubric-Grounded Preference Synthesis

**方法面（method surface）**：Builds a generative, rubric-grounded omni-modal reward model (Qwen2.5-Omni-3B/7B backbone) and then uses it as an inference-time selector over a frozen generator. Reward definition (Sec. 3): given multimodal context x and a response pair (yA,yB) the RM emits a structured record r = (sA, sB in [0,10], verdict l in {A,B,E}, five criterion-level comparative justifications j1:5, plus a redundant verdict copy for robust parsing); the five criteria are fluency/coherence, relevance, accuracy/completeness, reasoning quality, safety/ethical alignment, with modality-aware instantiation - for audio, 'acoustic event recognition, spoken content, and consistency between audio cues and textual responses'. Reward provenance: fully synthetic, no human labels - Omni-Preference (41.0k: image 17.0k RLAIF-V, video 12.2k, audio 11.8k Clotho-AQA) built by capability-gap pairing (strong vs weak generator, e.g. R1-AQA-7B / Qwen2.5-Omni-7B vs Qwen2-Audio-7B / Qwen2.5-Omni-3B; Table 1), then annotated by two heterogeneous teachers (GPT-4o-mini + Gemini-2.0-Flash) with a strict consensus filter (retain only identical non-tie verdicts numerically consistent with score ranking and justification); hard pairs defined by reconciled margin Delta = |sA-sB| < 2. Training: LoRA SFT (Eq. 2) then GRPO with bounded composite reward R = w_fmt R_fmt + w_pref R_pref + w_rub R_rub (Eq. 3) using group-normalized advantages, clipped PG, and a KL penalty to the SFT reference. Decision rights at inference (Sec. 4.4): SELECT ONLY. Best-of-N re-ranking over a fixed base generator Qwen2.5-Omni-7B via a single-elimination tournament of pairwise RM comparisons in a fixed bracket order, N-1 comparisons per query, N swept over {2,4,6,8,10}; the paper states this 'isolates the effect of inference-time alignment from any changes to the generator's underlying parameters'. No repair, no stopping rule, no abstention, no adaptive budget, no cross-query memory, single step. Gradient use: on the reward model only; the generator core is frozen. Tasks: preference accuracy on VL-RewardBench, MM-RewardBench, ShareGPT-Video, Audio-HH-RLHF, Omni-RewardBench-TA2T; BoN on MMMU, Video-MME, and AVQA (audio-only, multiple-choice).

**自述与显见局限**：Stated: Sec. 4.1 flags the audio benchmark caveat explicitly - Audio-HH-RLHF is built 'by applying TTS to prompts from HH-RLHF, while retaining the original human-written chosen/rejected responses and preference labels ... though it does not cover real-world acoustic noise or speaker variability'; i.e. the controlled audio channel is text preference wearing an audio costume, mitigated only by adding the public TA2T set. Sec. 4.4 concedes 'Omni-RRM introduces extra RM decoding latency due to structured rationales' and defers the latency analysis to Supplementary C.3-C.4 (absent from the main text). Sec. 2 states plainly 'our novelty does not lie in omni-modality'. Evident but unstated: there is no Limitations section; the BoN figure (Fig. 2) carries no CIs, no seeds, and single runs; the tournament bracket order is fixed and its order-sensitivity is untested; there is no cost-matched comparison (BoN@10 = 10 generations + 9 rationale-generating RM passes vs simply giving the frozen generator the same compute); the frozen generator's own oracle ceiling (pass@N) is never reported, so the fraction of available headroom captured by +2.6 is unknown; AVQA is used in an audio-only multiple-choice setting, so 'audio BoN' is discrete-option reranking rather than generative speech control; audio training data is Clotho-AQA only (audio captioning/QA), a narrow acoustic distribution; audio preference accuracy is only ~17 pp above chance.

**改进空间（方向级台阶）**：(1) Decision rights beyond select: the RM already emits five criterion-level judgments, which is a diagnostic signal currently collapsed into one verdict - routing 'which criterion failed' to a targeted repair or re-ask action is entirely open on top of this work. (2) Budget and stopping: N is a swept constant with cost N generations + (N-1) rationale-heavy RM passes; the paper's own hard-pair margin notion (Delta < 2) is exactly the confidence statistic needed for a stop/escalate rule and sits idle at inference. (3) Allocation: fixed-bracket single elimination spends comparisons on hopeless candidates; margin-aware bracketing or successive halving under a comparison budget is unoccupied. (4) Multi-step: no path from the reward back into observation building, tool/retrieval use, or a second generation attempt. (5) Genuine speech: real acoustic noise, speaker variability, spoken dialogue, and speech-output tasks are explicitly uncovered by their own admission - the speech-omni instantiation of this control plane is open. (6) Headroom accounting: adding a direct-readout / wrapper / oracle-ceiling three-column report would convert +1.7..+2.6 into a fraction-of-headroom claim, which is what the effect-over-novelty posture requires. (7) Cross-instance memory: nothing persists between queries.

**可借鉴**：Released resources - dataset, training and inference code, and model checkpoints at https://tmfk418.github.io/Omni-RRM (abstract). Directly usable as an off-the-shelf external judge over a frozen Qwen2.5-Omni with audio support, with its calibration known (audio 66.8 / 65.0). The BoN harness is exactly the baseline table we need: fixed base generator Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, N in {2,4,6,8,10}, single-elimination tournament with N-1 pairwise comparisons, and Self-consistency@N (multiple-choice majority vote) as the must-beat cheap baseline. Quoted effect sizes: preference accuracy (Table 2) Qwen2.5-Omni-7B backbone 57.8 / 57.5 / 66.3 / 62.4 / 56.9, Overall 60.2 -> Omni-RRM-7B (sft) 62.6 -> (sft+rl) 67.1 / 72.9 / 80.2 / 66.8 / 65.0, Overall 70.4 (+17.0% relative), versus Gemini-2.5-Pro 70.6, Gemini-2.0-Flash 66.2, GPT-4o-mini 58.3, Omni-RewardModel-BT 60.9; ablations (Table 3, four-benchmark Overall, 7B) w/o rationale 64.4, w/o rubric RL 68.1, full 71.8; modality mixture on Audio-HH (3B) omni 65.1 vs audio-only 63.8; difficulty stratification gains +12.0 pp Hard vs +6.6 pp Easy. BoN gains at N=10 over Self-consistency@N: MMMU 54.5 -> 56.4 (+1.9), Video-MME 52.4 -> 54.1 (+1.7), AVQA audio-only 77.7 -> 80.3 (+2.6). Prompt-level borrowables needing no training: the five-criterion rubric schema, the justify-before-verdict ordering, the redundant-verdict-copy parsing trick, the dual-teacher consensus filter as a provenance guard for any labels we synthesize, and the Delta = |sA-sB| margin as a difficulty/confidence statistic.

**资产完备度**：Project page promises dataset + training/inference code + checkpoints (https://tmfk418.github.io/Omni-RRM); no license stated in the paper text. Benchmarks are public but heterogeneous in access: VL-RewardBench, MM-RewardBench, ShareGPT-Video DPO, Omni-RewardBench-TA2T, MMMU, Video-MME, AVQA, Clotho-AQA; Audio-HH-RLHF is their own TTS-over-HH-RLHF construction (recipe described, TTS engine unspecified in the main text). Key numbers rely on proprietary teachers (GPT-4o-mini, Gemini-2.0-Flash) and so are not exactly reproducible. Pin: arXiv:2602.00846v2 (7 Jul 2026, cs.CL); pin the Omni-RRM checkpoint revision, the Qwen2.5-Omni-7B generator revision, and the benchmark split definitions (TA2T is reported with ties included in the main table, without-tie variants only in Supplementary C.6).

**域界定**：Omni RM (text/image/video/audio) with a frozen omni generator; the audio surface is Clotho-AQA training, Audio-HH-RLHF (TTS-synthesized text preference), TA2T, and AVQA in an audio-only multiple-choice setting. **备注**：This is the closest published instance of our degenerate case on a frozen omni core including audio: reward model selects, generator untouched. ### 2606.18323 — Reliable Neural-Codec Text-to-Speech by ASR Self-Verification and Distillation: Near-Zero Catastrophic Failures Across Models and Codecs

**方法面（method surface）**：Two lanes. Lane A (test time, Sec. 4): best-of-N ASR self-verification for autoregressive neural-codec TTS - sample N candidates per prompt, transcribe each with Whisper, and return the lowest-WER non-dropout candidate. The oracle is explicit (Eq. 1): phi(g) = 1[n(g) < tau_tok OR |h(g)| <= tau_w OR WER(h(g),x) > theta] with tau_tok = 25 speech tokens, tau_w = 1 word, theta = 0.5, combining a dropout arm and a collapse/wrong-content arm; the aggregate CFR_N = (1/|P|) sum_p prod_{k=1..N} phi(g_pk) (Eq. 2) counts a prompt as failed only when ALL N candidates fail, so CFR_N is a pass@N ceiling and is non-increasing in N. ### 2601.18510 — Just-In-Time Reinforcement Learning: Continual Learning in LLM Agents Without Gradient Updates (JitRL)

**方法面（method surface）**：Test-time policy optimization over a frozen LLM agent, no gradients anywhere. Three stages (§4, Alg. 1 p.26). (1) MEMORY: after each episode an LLM Evaluator maps the trajectory to step-wise rewards r_t on a certainty-graded -3..+3 scale (prompt verbatim, App. G.1.2), aggregated to discounted returns G_t = Sum_u gamma^{u-t} r_u (Eq. 3); each transition is stored as a triplet (abstracted state s_t, normalized action a_t, G_t). State abstraction is hand-designed per environment (App. F): WebArena = regularized URL with content IDs replaced by placeholders + local action history; Jericho = noun/verb structured summaries plus hierarchical [SUMMARY]/[PROGRESS]/[LOCATION] context. (2) RETRIEVAL-BASED VALUE ESTIMATION: top-k=10 neighbors by Jaccard token similarity (Eq. 24, two-stage URL-filter-then-Jaccard on WebArena; dual vector index 0.75 state + 0.25 history on Jericho); V-hat(s) = mean G over N(s) (Eq. 4); Q-hat(s,a) = mean G over the action-matched subset (Eq. 5); for unseen actions, optimism-under-uncertainty Q = V-hat + alpha/|N(s)| with probability lambda, else Q = 0 (Eq. 6). Advantage A-hat = Q-hat - V-hat (Eq. 7), normalized by max_a |A| (Eq. 25). (3) POLICY UPDATE: additive logit shift z'(s,a) = z(s,a) + beta*A-tilde(s,a), proved to be the EXACT closed-form solution of max_pi' E[A] - (1/beta) KL(pi'||pi_theta) (Theorem 4.1), with consistency of V-hat/Q-hat/A-hat (Thm 4.2) and of the induced policy (Thm 4.3). An augmented candidate set C = C_LLM union {retrieved actions} injects historically effective actions the model did not propose this step, with base logit initialized to 0 (Eq. 23). Two logit sources are specified for black-box deployment (§5.1 Implementation Details): token-level log-probabilities of an index token ('1','2','3'), or VERBALIZED confidence 0-100 transformed to logits for models exposing no logprobs. DECISION RIGHTS: select-among-candidate-actions per step (sampling from the reweighted softmax) plus an exploration-allocation knob via lambda/alpha. No stop, no abstain, no budget decision - step limit (10 WebArena / 60 Jericho) and episode count (50) are fixed constants (Tables 10-11). Core frozen; extra calls = candidate generation (|C|=3) + one evaluator pass per episode. TASKS: WebArena (5 sites, Avg + Final success over L=5 sequential episodes) and Jericho (Library, Zork1, Zork3, game score over 50 episodes). RESULTS: WebArena micro-average Final 51.35 vs Static 36.30, best training-free baseline EvoTest 42.49 (Table 1); WebArena-Lite Final 60.00 vs WebRL 46.06 / SFT 23.00 (Table 2, cross-backbone); same-backbone Llama-3.1-8B on-the-fly JitRL 32.97 vs WebRL 27.27 (Table 12); Jericho Zork1 Final 69 vs EvoTest 54, GRPO 10 (Table 3); cost $290 vs WebRL ~$9,900 (Table 9, App. R: 154 h on 16xH200). ### 2601.21484 — ETS: Energy-Guided Test-Time Scaling for Training-Free RL Alignment

**方法面（method surface）**：Training-free sampling from the KL-regularized RL-optimal policy, no gradients. THEORY (§3-4.1): the RLHF optimum is p*(x0|y) proportional to p_ref(x0|y)*exp(r(y,x0)/lambda) (Prop. 1). Prop. 2 decomposes the corresponding BACKWARD transition kernel for a unified masked-language-modeling view that subsumes autoregressive models and diffusion LMs: p(x_s|x_t,y) proportional to p_ref(x_s|x_t,y) * E_{p_ref(x0|y,x_s)}[exp(r(y,x0)/lambda)]. The second factor - the ENERGY - is the expected exponentiated future reward of a partially completed generation, i.e. a value function computed by rollout rather than by a trained critic. ALGORITHM 1: at I guidance steps spaced every B tokens (B = 64/128/256/512), sample M candidate segment-continuations from the frozen reference model; for each candidate run K independent completions to the end; estimate its energy by Monte Carlo (Eq. 8); self-normalize the M energies into weights w_m; resample one candidate multinomially; continue decoding. lambda fixed at 0.1. Explicit degenerate case (§4.2): with I=1 and lambda -> 0 the normalized weights collapse to an argmax and ETS becomes Best-of-N. REWARD (§4.4, App. B.3/B.4): verifier-free self-consistency - sample K completions, extract each discrete final answer, majority-vote, assign reward 1 to candidates matching the consensus and 0 otherwise (Eq. 67); a global answer cache A_cache accumulates extracted answers across the whole generation to raise the effective vote count. Thm. 2 gives exponential decay of the voting error P(a-hat != a*) <= (|A|-1)exp(-N(p-q_max)^2/2) UNDER the assumption p > q_max. App. B.4/D.2 compares four training-free reward candidates (mean token logprob, negative entropy, self-certainty, self-consistency); Fig. 9 shows only self-consistency separates correct from incorrect responses - the other three overlap. GUARANTEES: Prop. 3 bounds total-variation deviation by O~(I/sqrt(M) + I*epsilon); Thm. 1 under importance sampling by O~(I/sqrt(M) + I/sqrt(K)). ACCELERATION (§5): ETS-IS replaces reference-model rollouts with a scale-aligned small proposal model (Qwen3-1.7B for Qwen3-8B, shared tokenizer) and corrects by importance weights l(x0) = p_ref/p_small - which requires token-level logprobs from BOTH models; for diffusion LMs, Fast-dLLM (block KV cache + confidence-aware parallel decoding) serves as the proposal, again IS-corrected. DECISION RIGHTS: select-among-partial-generations at a FIXED schedule. No stop, no abstain, no repair; M, K, I, B and temperature are designer-set per benchmark and per model (Tables 2-4). RESULTS (Table 1, pass@1, best accuracy within constrained latency): Qwen3-8B average Base 60.57 -> Beam 63.25 -> Best-of-N 66.31 -> ETS 69.34 (7.35x latency) -> ETS-IS 66.45 (5.26x), vs GRPO post-training 65.98; Qwen3-1.7B 43.98 -> ETS 54.76 vs GRPO 48.08; LLaDA-8B-Instruct 46.71 -> ETS 51.01 (30.6x) vs VRPO 46.40. Ablations (§6.3): scaling M helps more than scaling K, K=3 is the sweet spot, I=4 or 8 recommended; baseline TTS methods DEGRADE with more samples due to verification noise while ETS does not (Fig. 5). Oracle gap (Table 5, GSM8K Qwen3-8B): ground-truth reward gives pass@N 98.10 while self-consistency gives 94.09 (Best-of-N) / 94.22 (ETS).

**自述与显见局限**：STATED: no dedicated Limitations section. In-text admissions: (a) Remark 2 (§4.3) - the TV bound grows linearly in I only because the guidance error epsilon is ASSUMED identical at every guidance step; in practice epsilon varies across x_t, so the actual error profile (and hence the optimal I) is unknown and a tighter bound is left as future work. (b) §5.2 - EAGLE-3 speculative decoding is incompatible with their batching (tree attention / vLLM integration) and quantization yields no operator-level gain at their scale, so acceleration is limited to the small-proposal route. (c) App. C.1 - the Power Sampling baseline requires a hand-crafted task-specific verifier and therefore could not be run on GSM8K at all; ETS's escape from that constraint is the stated contrast. (d) App. D.3 - temperature must be tuned per dataset and per model, and a single temperature is not optimal across hyperparameter scales. EVIDENT (not stated): (1) The self-consistency reward requires an EXTRACTABLE DISCRETE final answer (Extract + mode, Eq. 67); it is undefined for free-form outputs, which is most of the speech-omni portfolio (captioning, dialogue response, translation, summarization). **改进空间（方向级台阶）**：Direction-level steps that remain open ON TOP: (1) EVIDENCE-TRIGGERED GUIDANCE SCHEDULE - guidance points sit on a uniform token grid every B tokens; the paper itself observes (Remark 2) that the guidance error varies across states, which is precisely an argument for placing guidance where uncertainty or branching is high rather than on a clock. ### 2606.01667 — ATLAS: Agentic Test-time Learning-to-Allocate Scaling

**方法面（method surface）**：An LLM ORCHESTRATOR owns the test-time control loop end-to-end; nothing is trained and no reward model, scoring function or learned controller sits in the loop (§3.1, Alg. 1). Each turn emits a structured trace Thought_t (assessment of the candidate pool) / Action_t in {explore[args], stop[final_answer]} / Observation_t = candidate c_t = (answer, reasoning, approach, confidence). The single tool `explore` dispatches a FRESH INDEPENDENT solver on the ORIGINAL problem - the solver never sees prior candidates, which makes candidates conditionally independent given the problem and lets the orchestrator assess each one's marginal contribution in isolation. The orchestrator never solves the problem itself (enforced by prompt Principle 1). On stop it synthesizes the final answer DIRECTLY from the full Thought/Action/Observation history ('stateful evidence management'); replacing this with a separate integrator call costs more and does not improve accuracy on 3 of 4 benchmarks (Table 4b). ACTION-SPACE VARIANTS (§3.2, Table 1): ATLAS explore[] (only decision = stop timing); ATLAS-MM explore[model_name] over a {Haiku, Sonnet, Opus} pool (decision = solver capability, targeting capability ceilings); ATLAS-MI explore[instruction=string] (decision = solver focus, targeting focus failures). Budgets T=8 (ATLAS) and T=20 split Haiku 8 / Sonnet 8 / Opus 4 (MM), extended thinking 32k tokens/call (App. B). BUDGET/STOPPING DISPOSITION (§3.3): 'explore effort' is a hyperparameter realized purely as one of three short prompt sentences injected into the system prompt (App. H.1, verbatim: Low = two agreeing candidates suffice; Medium = prefers one cross-method explore, self-reported confidence is unreliable; High = three independent agreeing methods or an explicit cross-model cross-check). Justification is framed via rational metareasoning / value-of-computation (Hay et al.). Prompt Principle 5 grants an explicit ABSTAIN right: when candidates provide no useful information the orchestrator MUST submit an empty answer rather than spend budget or attempt the problem itself. DECISION RIGHTS EXERCISED: stop, budget-allocate (how many explores, which solver tier, what instruction), abstain, and select/synthesize. Repair is deliberately absent - the loop can only re-roll a fresh solver, never revise an existing candidate. RESULTS (Table 3, Claude Sonnet 4.6 backbone, all baselines matched on backbone and answer format): Pass@1 48.00 / 77.14 / 83.33 / 19.59 -> ATLAS 56.00 (HLE-Verified) / 82.29 (LiveCodeBench) / 85.86 (GPQA-Diamond) / 23.71 (BabyVision); ATLAS-MM 60.00 / 85.63 / 88.38 / 23.97. Orchestrator tier matters (Table 4a GPQA: Haiku 82.83 < Sonnet 85.86 < Opus 86.87); disabling extended thinking costs ~1 point at identical explore count (Table 4c). Cross-family (Table 5): open-weights orchestrators over a Sonnet explorer give positive gain everywhere (Qwen3.6-35B +8.00 HLE, +1.15 LCB; Gemma-4-26B +3.00 / +2.86 / +2.32); full-stack Qwen (its own weaker Pass@1) gains +5.0 HLE, +12.0 LiveCodeBench, +6.2 BabyVision. (a) SELECT-over-actions: JitRL converts retrieval-estimated advantage into an additive logit shift z' = z + beta*A, proved to be the exact closed-form solution of the KL-constrained policy-optimization objective (Thm 4.1), with retrieval standing in for a value network (Eq. 4-6) and an LLM evaluator standing in for a reward model (App. G.1.2). (b) SELECT-over-partial-generations: ETS derives the RL-optimal backward kernel as p_ref x energy (Prop. 2) and resamples M candidate continuations at I fixed checkpoints weighted by Monte-Carlo expected future reward, with a TV-distance bound O~(I/sqrt(M)+I*eps) (Prop. 3) and the explicit statement that I=1, lambda->0 recovers Best-of-N (§4.2) - i.e. our program's 'best-of-N is the degenerate case' framing is already formalized in the literature and should be cited, not re-derived. (c) STOP / ALLOCATE / ABSTAIN: ATLAS gives the control loop to the model itself through a single explore action plus stop, with an abstain principle and solver-tier allocation, and demonstrates the stop is not schedule-driven (88.9% exact-stop, 7.1x enrichment over a uniform-stop null, Fig. 7a). Occupation is therefore dense on select and now real on stop/allocate/abstain.

OPEN COMPOSITION. Three gaps hold at direction level across the trio and are consistent with the 2026-07-26 emptiness finding rather than derived from it. (1) MEMORY x BUDGET/STOPPING: JitRL has cross-episode and cross-task memory (~47% of retrieved context is cross-task, Table 6) but a hard-coded step/episode budget and no stop; ATLAS has runtime stopping and allocation but is memoryless BY CONSTRUCTION (each explore sees only the problem, nothing persists across questions); ETS has neither. No paper here lets accumulated cross-instance evidence set the budget. (2) REWARD-ADMISSIBILITY GATE: all three let a signal steer actions without ever asking whether the signal is trustworthy this time - JitRL's evaluator is ungated (its own stated limitation #2), ETS's consensus reward has a validity condition (Thm 2 requires p > q_max) that is proved and then never checked at runtime, ATLAS's bar is three prompt sentences. A gate that decides WHEN reward may steer is unoccupied by this trio and is the sharpest form of the charter's second question. (3) REPAIR: none of the three repairs. JitRL reweights, ETS resamples, ATLAS re-rolls fresh independent solvers and structurally forbids candidates from building on each other. Reward-driven repair on an existing candidate is the least occupied decision right in this set.

GATES AND RISKS THAT MUST HOLD BEFORE A REWARD SIGNAL MAY STEER. (i) ACCESS GATE: JitRL's headline mechanism needs logits over a candidate index token; its black-box-compatible verbalized-confidence path is announced and never measured (no table), and beta - the sole knob converting advantage into logit displacement - is never reported and never ablated. Adopting the mechanism under our contract silently changes it, so the verbalized path must be measured first. ETS-IS needs logprobs from two models with a shared tokenizer and is simply inadmissible; ETS proper needs mid-sequence resumption, which for us must be re-derived at tool-call/turn boundaries rather than token positions. (ii) VALIDITY GATE: every selection signal in the trio is a readout of the same frozen core (LLM self-evaluation / self-consistency / orchestrator judgment), so its ceiling is the core's own consensus - and consensus inverts exactly where a speech core is systematically biased (accent, code-switch, noise, overlap). Thm 2's p > q_max should be operationalized as a deployed admissibility check (vote margin, answer-set entropy), not left as an assumption. (iii) OBSERVATION GATE: all three keep the state textual. JitRL's stated limitation #3 names non-text state as its failure mode; ATLAS's orchestrator sees only (answer, reasoning, approach, confidence) summaries and posts its smallest margin over majority voting on its only non-text benchmark. For speech this means the controller cannot distinguish independent agreement from shared acoustic misperception - the very case ATLAS's own Principle 3 warns about. The waveform observation/state-abstraction contract is therefore where the text-domain evidence stops and is load-bearing design, not a port detail. (iv) CONTROL GATE for our own reporting: ATLAS shows the controls a stopping claim needs (matched backbone, matched answer format, LLM-Selection at fixed N to isolate stopping from aggregation, seed stability n=5, per-question cost with exclusions stated); JitRL shows the failure mode to avoid (headline Table 2 mixes backbones, and the matched-backbone appendix shrinks the margin). (v) BUDGET-NOISE RISK: ETS §6.3 records baseline test-time-scaling accuracy DEGRADING with more samples due to verification noise, so more compute is not monotone and our budget dimension must be evaluated on a cost-accuracy frontier, not at a single N.

DIRECT-READOUT BASELINE IMPLICATIONS. All three report against the same frozen model's own unassisted output (JitRL Static, ETS Base, ATLAS Pass@1) - the headroom-accounting convention the program requires is already standard here and should be adopted verbatim.
