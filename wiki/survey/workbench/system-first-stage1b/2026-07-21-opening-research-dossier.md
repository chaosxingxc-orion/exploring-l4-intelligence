## Opening task decomposition

The first execution batch has four coupled but separately auditable products:

1. frozen arXiv query receipts and raw Atom hashes;
2. T1 proceedings-route receipts;
3. a breadth-first canonical work ledger with explicit exclusions and unresolved identities; and
4. a depth-first queue for full-text method-path coding and citation closure.

The first full-text priority group is not a presumed novelty set. It is an adversarial proximity set:
Omni-Decision and AOP-Agent are direct neighbors; AudioToolAgent, Native Active Perception, Thinking
While Listening, Agent-Omni, EChO-Agent and MM-ReAct exercise audio/omni observation and action paths;
ReAct, Reflexion and LATS anchor general sequential-control lineage; Light-Omni, LatentOmni,
OmniSelect and OmniDrop test trained, peripheral, or white-box boundaries.

## Coding questions for every priority path

- What is the deployed method path and core topology, and which components are trained or updated?
- What information is visible at inference time; is any answer-bearing new information injected?
- What state persists, and across which utterance, turn, trajectory, or session boundary?
- Which signal instance exists, who produces it, and which later decision right does it causally alter?
- What actions are available: observe, retrieve, call a tool, branch, repair, select, stop, or synthesize?
- How are candidate supply, verifier discrimination, equal-K baselines, budgets, stopping and failure
  cases reported?
- What evidence is D0/D1/D2, what is missing, and what counterevidence challenges the favorable read?

## Opening hypotheses, held at hypothesis grade

- Sequential reward-guided control may already be occupied in general agents, while strict
  black-box omni/speech instantiations remain differently constrained; mapping must identify the exact
  boundary rather than infer an empty intersection.
- Apparent controller gains may often be supply, tool, or evaluator gains. Stage-1B can map whether
  papers report the needed controls but cannot run the attribution experiment.
- Speech/omni specificity may arise from timing, acoustic evidence and persistent state rather than
  from the word “omni.” H5 cannot carry this claim until independent calibration closes.
- Budget-aware stopping and verification may be less developed than state/action orchestration, but
  this is only a queue priority until D2 evidence and citation closure establish the inspected set.

## Opening research progress

- The 65-row frozen arXiv batch is complete: 535 REC-1 rows contain 489 successful result pages,
  39 split-count probes, and 7 retained failed attempts with zero active failures after bounded retry.
  Hash-verified raw responses yielded a 20,727-ID D0 BFS union. Full counts and snapshot hashes are in
  `2026-07-21-arxiv-execution-snapshot.md`; this is not yet canonical REC-0 screening.
- `2026-07-21-opening-abstract-screening.md` records the first 27 D1
  `SELECT_FULLTEXT` decisions (15 P0, 10 P1, 2 P2), with a reason and paper-specific D2 questions.
  Selection occurs before download; no new paper receives a D2 label from an online abstract.
- The 27 selected papers have all been downloaded as both PDF and e-print to the external data store;
  54/54 unique renditions pass independent ledger hash/byte checks. All 15 new P0 papers have been read
  at D2. `2026-07-21-opening-d2-method-path-notes.md` maps the 22 prior/P0 full texts and
  `2026-07-21-opening-p1-p2-d2-notes.md` maps all 10 P1 method comparators and 2 P2 measurement
  instruments. Across the two records, 34 local full texts are represented.
- The current strongest structural proximity is not a single undifferentiated “agent” class:
  Omni-Decision exposes critic-mediated state updates plus readiness/exhaustion, AOP-Agent exposes a
  sufficiency-triggered reobserve loop, and AudioToolAgent exposes conflict-triggered prompt
  orchestration with a hard call cap. None of these facts is a novelty verdict.
- Native Active Perception, Light-Omni, and LatentOmni demonstrate that active or latent omni control
  is also occupied by trained paths. They remain visible boundary comparators rather than being
  removed from the map.
- REC-1 abstract screening has already produced adverse evidence against any broad “training-free
  multimodal reward-guided control is empty” formulation: T^2Agent describes MCTS with dual rewards,
  and VReST describes training-free MCTS with multimodal self-reward. These are D1 author claims until
  local D2 reading resolves reward access, information leakage, search budgets, and actual decision
  rights, but they must be treated as direct proximity threats now rather than deferred analogies.
- The same REC-1 batch surfaced negative/control evidence: the small-VLM test-time-scaling study
  reports prompt/token-budget effects dominating elaborate search or critics, while PInVerify reports
  no reliable gain from its tested active-view policies. Both were promoted to P0 D2 because favorable
  controller-only synthesis would be biased without them.
- Reflexion and LATS confirm broad no-weight-update feedback/memory and reward/value-guided tree-search
  lineage. DeepVerifier adds targeted evidence retrieval and rubric feedback but also documents
  verifier-induced regressions. PInVerify finds no reliable separation among its tested active-view
  policies. Together these findings force path-level information and attribution coding; they do not
  authorize an empty-intersection or novelty conclusion.
