## Provisional method-path map

The twenty-two local full texts separate into twelve paths that should not be collapsed:

1. **controlled feedback-and-revision** — What Drives Interactive Improvement supplies a matched
   self-refinement baseline and shows why feedback gain must be separated from repeated attempts;
2. **pre-execution review** — Reinforced Agent lets a reviewer revise or select provisional tool calls,
   while also exposing reviewer helpfulness/harmfulness and an optimized-prompt path;
3. **evidence-state closure** — Omni-Decision makes validation verdicts and unresolved needs explicit
   controller state, including readiness, exhaustion, and insufficient termination;
4. **reflect-and-reobserve** — AOP-Agent, AudioGenie-Reasoner, and OmniAgent use sufficiency or
   consistency judgments to acquire more audio/visual evidence without additional training; AGR
   additionally exposes over-iteration and OmniAgent exposes cross-modal discrepancies;
5. **prompt-guided tool orchestration** — AudioToolAgent reacts to conflicts and ambiguity through
   follow-up tool calls, with a hard call cap but no separately specified evaluator;
6. **sample then aggregate** — Thinking While Listening increases inference samples and aggregates
   traces, without an adaptive observation/action loop in the inspected path; and
7. **reward/value-guided tree search** — T²Agent backpropagates a dual trajectory/confidence reward
   through MCTS over a multimodal tool subset, VReST self-rewards multimodal reasoning steps, and
   LATS adds environment reward, an LM/self-consistency value function, and failed-path reflection;
8. **verifier-driven repair/selection** — EChO-Agent and Kestrel check grounded consistency and
   regenerate, correct, select, or stop under explicit verdicts;
9. **evidence-aligned consensus** — EAGLE filters multi-agent answer agreement using shared visual
   grounding evidence without becoming a sequential observation controller;
10. **cross-trial verbal reinforcement** — Reflexion turns sparse task feedback into persistent verbal
   memory that changes a later reset trial rather than the current environment trajectory;
11. **active-view verification with a null NBV result** — PInVerify supplies an explicit belief/action/
   stop process, but its tested random, geometric, and LLM view policies are statistically unresolved;
12. **learned active/latent/reflex policies** — Native Active Perception, Speech-Hands, Light-Omni,
   and LatentOmni
   obtain their controller or representation behavior through SFT/RL, adapters, soft prompts, or
   latent-objective training.

This map weakens any formulation that treats “agentic”, “reflection”, “training-free”, “frozen at
inference”, and “reward-guided inference” as interchangeable. It does not establish an unoccupied
intersection: the new P0/P1 downloads and citation closure must test whether other papers instantiate
the same signal-to-action rights under stricter black-box and no-update boundaries.

## Counterevidence and unresolved checks

- Omni-Decision already covers explicit evidence state, critic-mediated updates, readiness, repair,
  and insufficient stopping; later synthesis must compare exact decision rights rather than use a
  coarse “stateful controller” label.
- AOP-Agent and AudioToolAgent show that strong training-free omni/audio systems can obtain sequential
  behavior without an explicit scalar reward. Stage-1B must therefore code signal instances, not infer
  them from the words “reflect” or “agent”.
- Native Active Perception demonstrates reward-guided active perception under a trained policy. Any
  strict training-free claim must preserve this as a nearby but distinct path rather than exclude it
  from the map.
- Thinking While Listening contains both frozen zero-shot and retrained-embedding variants. Paper-level
  labels would create a false boundary; coding must be method-path specific.
- What Drives Interactive Improvement supplies a direct falsifier for attributing any multi-turn gain
  to feedback without an unguided retry baseline, while Reinforced Agent shows that a reviewer can
  both repair and break actions. RQ-CTRL and RQ-SAFE must retain both controls.
- AudioGenie-Reasoner already implements a training-free audio sufficiency-triggered acquisition loop;
  proximity synthesis must compare its explicit state, signal source, tool visibility, and iteration
  budget rather than describe the target only as “iterative audio reasoning”.
- T²Agent supplies a D2-confirmed training-free multimodal reward→tree-policy path, while OmniAgent
  supplies a D2-confirmed no-training omni reflection→reobserve path. Stage-1B must now map exact
  single-core/API topology and speech/omni causal boundaries; it cannot rely on a broad method-family
  absence.
- The small-VLM control study is adverse evidence against assuming a sophisticated verifier/search is

  settings. This negative result stays adjacent to favorable controller papers in synthesis.

- PInVerify finds no reliable advantage among its tested next-best-view policies and locates detection,
