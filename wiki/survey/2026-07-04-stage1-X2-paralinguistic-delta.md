## Open problems (P0-compliant: task-level, metric-named, literature-anchored)

### P-X2-1 — ladder: mixed

Frozen generative audio/omni LLMs remain near chance at speech-emotion recognition under zero-shot prompting — Qwen2-Audio 0.2913 accuracy / 0.2061 F1 on IEMOCAP dominant emotion; scaffold-free Qwen3-Omni-thinking 14.6% SAR on VoxSafeBench — and models fail entire emotion categories (no evaluated ALM reliably recognizes disgust on CREMA-D; only Qwen2-Audio shows partial success). Every robust recovery demonstrated through mid-2026 changes weights (LoRA, DPO, self-distillation); training-free strategies move points on a near-chance base without controls certifying acoustic grounding.

**Metric:** SER accuracy / F1 (IEMOCAP, MSP-Podcast, CREMA-D); JS divergence to annotator label distributions; VoxSafeBench SAR

**Named by:** [Decoding Ambiguous Emotions with Test-Time Scaling in Audio-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03873) (2026-02-01) · [ParaBridge: Bridging Paralinguistic Perception and Dialogue Behavior in Speech Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10581) (2026-06-09)

### P-X2-2 — ladder: b2

Frozen LALMs cannot reliably read speaker identity from acoustics via prompting: basic-prompt non-lexical judgments sit near chance (GPT-4o-Audio pronunciation 46.0%, speaking rate 46.9% vs 50% chance — AudioJudge), and as speaker-consistency judges 12 frozen LALMs fail to detect acoustic inconsistencies, with textual context actively degrading acoustic judgment — models prioritize textual coherence over acoustic cues, missing even obvious gender switches (SpeakerSleuth). Fine-grained k-way speaker-ID resists prompting/ICL entirely; all demonstrated recoveries use dedicated speaker encoders and/or training.

**Metric:** same/different-speaker judgment accuracy (chance 50%); multi-turn speaker-consistency detection accuracy/F1 (SpeakerSleuth, 1,818 instances); k-way speaker-ID accuracy vs ECAPA/ERes2Net EER

**Named by:** [SpeakerSleuth: Can Large Audio-Language Models Judge Speaker Consistency across Multi-turn Dialogues?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04029) (2026-01-07) · [AudioJudge: Understanding What Works in Large Audio Model Based Speech Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12705) (2025-07-17)


## Approach genealogy & evidence claims (cross-domain mandatory)

### X2-C1 — [new] origin: **llm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: c · anchor: P-X2-1

Self-described FIRST benchmark of test-time scaling for ambiguous speech-emotion recognition on frozen generative ALMs (Feb 2026): 'Decoding Ambiguous Emotions with Test-Time Scaling' benchmarks 5 inference-only strategies (CoT, BoN selecting the highest log-likelihood beam, Weighted-BoN via normalized log-likelihood self-weighting, frozen GPT-4o-as-verifier ALM-v/W-ALM-v) on 8 frozen models (Audio-Flamingo-2, Qwen2-Audio, Qwen2.5-Omni, Ultravox v0.3/0.4, GPT-4o, Gemini 2.0 Flash / 2.5 Pro) across IEMOCAP/MSP-Podcast/CREMA-D. Weighted aggregation realizes gains LABEL-FREE (W-BoN on Qwen2-Audio IEMOCAP: acc 0.2913->0.3669, F1 0.2061->0.3688; W-ALM-v JS divergence -31.1% relative on MSP-Podcast), while unweighted BoN and CoT sometimes DEGRADE (BoN JS +11.4% worse on MSP-Podcast). CRITICALLY: zero label-sensitivity or acoustic-grounding controls; the baseline is near-chance and post-TTS absolute accuracy (~0.37 on IEMOCAP) remains far below fine-tuned SOTA — this is condition-(c) selection-realizability evidence on the emotion surface (external positive for rho>0 with a self-likelihood selector), NOT a premise-overturning recovery.

**Sources:** [Decoding Ambiguous Emotions with Test-Time Scaling in Audio-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03873) (2026-02-01) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* BoN / self-consistency / verifier-reranking genealogy is text-LLM test-time-compute literature (Snell et al. 2024 scaling test-time compute; s1/Muennighoff 2025), which the paper builds on
*Speech-domain evidence:* this paper: 8 frozen ALMs, 3 SER datasets; verifier confirmed against arXiv HTML: model list, 5 strategies (W-BoN = 'weighted sum of all outputs generated by beam search, where each output is weighted by its normalized log-likelihood'), acc 0.2913->0.3669 / F1 0.2061->0.3688, W-ALM-v -31.1% and BoN +11.4% JS on MSP-Podcast

### X2-C2 — [new] origin: **llm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-X2-2

Strongest training-free movement found on any speaker-adjacent axis: AudioJudge shows frozen GPT-4o-Audio with BASIC prompting sits near chance on non-lexical judgments (pronunciation 46.0%, speaking rate 46.9% vs 50% chance; Gemini-2.5-Flash also evaluated), and audio-concatenation in-context examples (4-shot, demos and test concatenated into one audio stream) lift coarse same/different-speaker judgment on LibriTTS-R pairs 61.5%->70.0% (p<0.05) and pronunciation 46.0%->66.5% (p<0.001); speaking rate reaches only 55.3%. Prompting-only, no fine-tuning. Limits: binary/coarse speaker comparison on 200 pairs (not k-way fine ID), no label-sensitivity or acoustic-grounding controls, and 70% on a 50%-chance pair task remains ~30pp below dedicated speaker encoders — sharpens (does not overturn) the scoping: coarse speaker comparison is partially prompt-reachable, fine-grained ID remains undemonstrated.

**Sources:** [AudioJudge: Understanding What Works in Large Audio Model Based Speech Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12705) (2025-07-17) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* few-shot ICL-with-demonstrations genealogy is text-LLM (GPT-3-class ICL); the audio-concatenation packaging is the speech-native adaptation
*Speech-domain evidence:* this paper, Table 1 (GPT-4o-Audio): same-speaker judgment 61.5->70.0 with 4-shot Examples&Test audio concatenation; verifier confirmed all numbers and p-values against arXiv HTML, task confirmed binary 'Are these audios from the same speaker?'

### X2-C3 — [new] origin: **speech** · transfer: native · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-X2-2

SpeakerSleuth (ACL 2026 main) supplies new behavior-level confirmation of the generative-class premise for speaker: 12 frozen LALMs evaluated as speaker-consistency judges on 1,818 human-verified multi-turn instances (four datasets, synthetic and real speech) 'struggle to reliably detect acoustic inconsistencies', and — the sharpening finding — performance DEGRADES when textual context is provided because LALMs 'prioritize textual coherence over acoustic cues, failing to detect even obvious gender switches' (fundamental modality imbalance). Some acoustic discrimination exists in pure comparative settings (presence), but the text-dominant readout suppresses it — the presence-without-readout pattern our premise asserts, now shown at the prompted-behavior level rather than the probe level.

**Sources:** [SpeakerSleuth: Can Large Audio-Language Models Judge Speaker Consistency across Multi-turn Dialogues?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04029) (2026-01-07) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* native speech benchmark (ACL 2026 main); verifier corrected the title from the lane's paraphrase to the actual arXiv title
*Speech-domain evidence:* same paper; 12 frozen LALMs, 1,818 instances, text context actively harms acoustic speaker judgment — all confirmed against the arXiv abstract

### X2-C4 — [new] origin: **speech** · transfer: native · fence: gradient-trained · ladder: b1 · anchor: P-X2-1

ParaBridge (June 2026) names the paralinguistic perception-behavior gap on the HOUSE model class — scaffold-free Qwen3-Omni-thinking scores 14.6% SAR on VoxSafeBench — and shows the now-familiar pattern: a training-free paralinguistic instruction scaffold at inference DOES narrow the gap but is 'brittle under multi-turn context and competing instructions'; the durable recovery is on-policy self-distillation (weight change) lifting scaffold-free SAR 14.6%->40.3% and EchoMind average rating 3.27->3.92, with reported generalization to unseen paralinguistic cues and transfer across SLM backbones. This is the fourth consecutive paper (after VoxParadox-DPO 2605.27772, forced-choice-LoRA 2602.23136, FSA-GRPO 2606.02615 — all archived) in which prompt-level movement exists but robust paralinguistic recovery on a generative omni is achieved only by training — reinforcing the premise for Qwen3-Omni specifically.

**Sources:** [ParaBridge: Bridging Paralinguistic Perception and Dialogue Behavior in Speech Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10581) (2026-06-09) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* speech-native paper; the self-distillation recipe itself descends from text-LLM distillation literature
*Speech-domain evidence:* same paper; Qwen3-Omni-thinking scaffold-free 14.6% SAR baseline, trained recovery to 40.3%, EchoMind 3.27->3.92 — all confirmed against the arXiv abstract

### X2-C5 — [new] origin: **speech** · transfer: native · fence: gradient-trained · ladder: background · anchor: P-X2-2

2026 speaker-recovery frontier remains entirely out-of-fence, two new confirmations: (i) SpeakerLLM (May 2026) achieves speaker understanding/verification reasoning on an audio-LLM only via a TRAINED hierarchical speaker tokenizer (utterance-level embeddings + frame-level features for acoustic descriptors) with supervised verification-reasoning targets; (ii) 'Reasoning LLM Improves Speaker Recognition in Long-form TV Dramas' (July 2026, DramaSR-LRM on the 532K-line DramaSR benchmark) gains +2.30% absolute (85.49->87.79%, a 16% relative error reduction) but is SFT+GRPO-trained (Qwen3-8B, 3 epochs SFT + 2 epochs GRPO) AND sources all acoustic discrimination from an external frozen ERes2Net speaker encoder queried through a voice_sim cosine-similarity tool — the LLM consumes only text and tool outputs, never raw audio. Every 2026 speaker recovery found routes through dedicated speaker encoders and/or weight changes; zero exceptions.

**Sources:** [SpeakerLLM: A Speaker-Specialized Audio-LLM for Speaker Understanding and Verification Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15044) (2026-05-14) · [Reasoning LLM Improves Speaker Recognition in Long-form TV Dramas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02504) (2026-07-02) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* speech-native systems; the tool-use reasoning scaffold in the TV-drama paper descends from text-LLM agentic tool-use
*Speech-domain evidence:* same papers; verifier confirmed from full text: Qwen3-8B + 3-epoch SFT + 2-epoch GRPO, ERes2Net voice_sim cosine tool, LLM never reads audio, 85.49->87.79; SpeakerLLM hierarchical speaker tokenizer confirmed trained

### X2-C6 — [new] origin: **vlm** · transfer: partial · fence: trained-head-on-frozen · ladder: background · anchor: P-X2-1

EMO-TTA (ICASSP 2026) illustrates how the 'training-free' SER literature routes AROUND prompting: it is gradient-free but incrementally updates class-conditional statistics via an Expectation-Maximization procedure at test time, using model predictions as priors — i.e., an adaptively FITTED readout head on frozen audio-language-model features (trained-head-on-frozen in our fence taxonomy, VLM test-time-adaptation genealogy transferred to CLAP-style SER), improving over zero-shot on six out-of-domain SER benchmarks without modifying weights, gradients, or prompts. It is positioning evidence, not a counter-example: the gain comes from statistical readout fitting, not from prompt/ICL reachability, and no label-sensitivity or acoustic-grounding controls are run.

**Sources:** [EMO-TTA: Improving Test-Time Adaptation of Audio-Language Models for Speech Emotion Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25495) (2025-09-29 (rev 2026-02-03, ICASSP 2026)) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* training-free test-time adaptation with class-statistics caches originates in CLIP TTA literature (TDA/TPT class)
*Speech-domain evidence:* EMO-TTA transfers it to SER on audio-language models, six OOD benchmarks — confirmed against the arXiv abstract (verifier dropped the unconfirmed 'Gaussian' qualifier; abstract states class-conditional statistics via incremental EM)

### X2-C7 — [new] origin: **vlm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-X2-1

Contrastive-class scoping sharpener: 'Prompt Amplification and Zero-Shot Late Fusion' (March 2026) reports that on DUAL-ENCODER (CLAP-style) ALMs, repeating audio and text queries ('prompt amplification') discovers stronger zero-shot SER, and ZS-Fuse — zero-shot late fusion of the dual-encoder ALM's emotion estimates with specialist foundation models (3 ALMs x 2 FMs) — improves over SOTA baselines incl. WavLM-Large on three SER datasets. This is prompt-space movement on the VECTOR class readout — the class where our settled evidence places a single-vector ceiling (~0.40-0.51) — with no label-sensitivity/acoustic-grounding controls and numbers not independently extracted; b1/b2-unsplit. It suggests the vector-class emotion ceiling may be prompt-sensitive at the margin, worth noting against our 'nothing to read' phrasing, but does not touch the generative-class premise.

**Sources:** [Prompt Amplification and Zero-Shot Late Fusion in Audio-Language Models for Speech Emotion Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23057) (2026-03-24) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* zero-shot class-prompt engineering / prompt ensembling for dual encoders originates in CLIP zero-shot classification
*Speech-domain evidence:* this paper: prompt amplification + ZS-Fuse on three SER datasets over three dual-encoder ALMs and two specialist FMs — confirmed against the arXiv abstract


## Training-free vs fine-tuned SOTA positioning

## Positioning: training-free vs fine-tuned SOTA — paralinguistic axes on frozen audio/omni models (X2 delta, 2026-07-04; adversarially verified)

**Emotion (generative class).** Training-free frontier (new, Feb 2026): test-time scaling with self-likelihood-weighted best-of-N and a frozen GPT-4o verifier — Qwen2-Audio IEMOCAP acc 0.29→0.37, F1 0.21→0.37, W-ALM-v JS −31.1% on MSP-Podcast (arXiv:2602.03873; no label-sensitivity/grounding controls; unweighted BoN can DEGRADE, JS +11.4%). Gradient-trained frontier: forced-choice LoRA +44.5pp CREMA-D (2602.23136, archived); VoxParadox DPO+layer-mixer 17.40→65.20 (2605.27772, archived); ParaBridge self-distillation on Qwen3-Omni-thinking 14.6→40.3 SAR (2606.10581, new). **The fence gap remains decisive: training-free moves points on a near-chance base; training moves tens of points.** Middle lane: EMO-TTA's EM-fitted class-conditional statistics (trained-head-on-frozen, VLM-TTA transfer, 2509.25495, ICASSP 2026) — gains come from readout fitting, not prompt reachability.

**Speaker (generative class).** Training-free frontier: audio-concatenation ICL lifts coarse same/different-speaker judgment 61.5→70.0% on frozen GPT-4o-Audio (AudioJudge 2507.12705, LibriTTS-R pairs) — binary task, ~30pp below dedicated encoders; fine-grained ID remains near-chance under any prompting (2603.10827, unchanged). SpeakerSleuth (2601.04029, ACL 2026 main) adds behavior-level confirmation: 12 frozen LALMs are unreliable speaker-consistency judges and textual context actively SUPPRESSES acoustic judgment (models miss even obvious gender switches when text is coherent). Trained frontier: SpeakerLLM's trained hierarchical speaker tokenizer (2605.15044); SFT+GRPO reasoning over an external frozen ERes2Net encoder, LLM never reads audio (2607.02504). **Every demonstrated speaker recovery still routes through a dedicated encoder and/or weight change.**

**Vector/contrastive class.** Prompt amplification + zero-shot late fusion moves CLAP-style zero-shot SER over SOTA baselines incl. WavLM-Large (2603.23057, uncontrolled, b1/b2-unsplit) — a marginal caveat to the 'nothing to read / readout-ceiling' phrasing on the emotion axis, none on speaker.

**Consequence for the semantic pivot:** unchanged and strengthened — the premise survives its first standing-watch delta scan; the only genuinely training-free positives on paralinguistics are condition-(c) selection effects on a near-chance base, exactly the pattern that justifies keeping paralinguistic axes closed and focusing the sufficiency yardstick (H_prompt − H_fix, ρ) on ASR/SLU/Spoken-QA.
