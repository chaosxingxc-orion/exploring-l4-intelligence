### D1 kNN 门控 文献空白

WHAT THE LITERATURE DOES NOT PROVIDE (our open niches): (1) NO convergence proof for any gating rule. TARG (arXiv:2511.09803) — the current SOTA training-free gate — ASSUMES a threshold tau* exists where retrieval helps iff U>tau* ('usefulness calibration'), and only proves a static order-equivalence lemma; nobody proves tau*'s existence/optimality or that a learned threshold CONVERGES to it. This is exactly our T-A/T-B/T-E niche. (2) NO 'never-worse-than-base' guarantee under a FROZEN datastore. Drozdov (2210.15859), He (2109.04212), and Zheng (2105.13022) all make lambda adaptive but empirically/with trained nets; none prove the gated interpolator dominates the base LM, and none tie the gate to a neighborhood RADIUS with a proof. (3) NO reward-guided / RL formulation of the gate anywhere in the kNN line — all gates are fixed heuristics, small supervised regressors, or dev-calibrated uncertainty thresholds; none optimize the gate by a VERIFIABLE REWARD with a convergence guarantee (our reward-guided training-free RL is genuinely unoccupied). (4) The one formal risk-bound (RAG-as-noisy-ICL, 2506.03100) is for in-context LINEAR REGRESSION, not for the interpolation-of-DISTRIBUTIONS kNN-LM object; there is no bridge of its bias-floor/plateau result to the discrete-vocabulary NLL setting we actually use — we would have to build that bridge. ### D4 校准/τ 文献空白
