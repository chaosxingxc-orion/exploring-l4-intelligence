## Open problems (P0-compliant: task-level, metric-named, literature-anchored)

### P-SLU-1 — ladder: mixed

End-to-end (frozen or instruction-tuned) audio-LLMs still lag cascaded ASR->text-LLM/NLU pipelines on semantic SLU in zero-shot settings: Speech-MASSIVE's zero-shot baselines show cascaded Whisper+mT5 beating end-to-end for all 12 languages (avg intent accuracy 69.10 vs 57.07, with the same pattern on slot micro-F1), text LLMs on oracle transcripts approach supervised intent accuracy, and frozen Qwen-Audio trails a trained speech-LLM by 33% relative SLU-F1 on zero-shot SLURP slot filling.

**Metric:** intent accuracy and SLU-F1 / slot micro-F1 (SLURP, Speech-MASSIVE), E2E-vs-cascaded delta under matched zero-shot conditions

**Named by:** [Speech-MASSIVE: A Multilingual Speech Dataset for SLU and Beyond (Interspeech 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03900) (2024-08) · [WHISMA: A Speech-LLM to Perform Zero-shot Spoken Language Understanding (SLT 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16423) (2024-08) · [Can ChatGPT Detect Intent? Evaluating Large Language Models for Spoken Language Understanding (Interspeech 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13512) (2023-05)

### P-SLU-2 — ladder: mixed

Frozen LALMs are severely instruction/schema-sensitive: semantically equivalent instruction paraphrases, output-format constraints (e.g. JSON), and task composition cause large accuracy swings (ISA-Bench: significant degradation across instruction description / output format / task composition; AHAMask: different instructions of the same intention yield drastically different outcomes). The gradient fix is costly: fine-tuning Qwen2-Audio on instruction variants improves compliance but 'induces nontrivial catastrophic forgetting' of previously mastered tasks (ISA-Bench, verbatim).

**Metric:** accuracy/consistency variance across semantically-equivalent instruction variants (ISA-Bench compliance + task metrics); prompt-induced task drift under reworded ASR prompts (SALMONN phoneme-recognition/unrequested-translation drift documented in AudioBench, which added 20+ prompt templates in response)

**Named by:** [ISA-Bench: Benchmarking Instruction Sensitivity for Large Audio Language Models (ICASSP 2026 submission)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23558) (2025-10) · [AHAMask: Reliable Task Specification for Large Audio Language Models Without Instructions (AAAI 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01787) (2025-09) · [AudioBench: A Universal Benchmark for Audio Large Language Models (NAACL 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16020) (2024-06)

### P-SLU-3 — ladder: b2

Zero-shot / unseen-schema intent generalization on frozen speech models is far below supervised and below trained speech-LLMs: WHISMA needed SLU-focused parameter-efficient fine-tuning to gain +26.6% relative zero-shot SLURP slot filling over prior SOTA and built SLU-GLUE precisely because unseen-domain generalization of speech-LLMs was unmeasured; instruction-tuned speech models struggle with unseen tasks while performing reasonably on seen ones (Dynamic-SUPERB).

**Metric:** zero-shot SLU-F1 / intent accuracy on unseen domains (SLU-GLUE, SLURP zero-shot); seen-vs-unseen task accuracy delta (Dynamic-SUPERB)

**Named by:** [WHISMA: A Speech-LLM to Perform Zero-shot Spoken Language Understanding (SLT 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16423) (2024-08) · [Dynamic-SUPERB: A Dynamic, Collaborative, Comprehensive Instruction-Tuning Benchmark for Speech (ICASSP 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09510) (2023-09) · [Zero-Shot Spoken Language Understanding via Large Language Models: A Preliminary Study (LREC-COLING 2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.1554/) (2024-05)

### P-SLU-4 — ladder: mixed

Slot filling / structured semantic extraction is the weak link of prompting-only SLU: even where zero/few-shot intent classification approaches supervised accuracy, ChatGPT-class models are 'worse at slot filling' and sensitive to ASR errors; frozen Qwen-Audio loses 33% relative SLU-F1 vs a trained speech-LLM on zero-shot SLURP slots; JSON output constraints degrade even strong proprietary LALMs (ISA-Bench: Gemini-2.5-Pro 'fails under JSON-style formatting constraints', verbatim).

**Metric:** SLU-F1 / slot micro-F1 (SLURP), slot F1 vs intent accuracy gap on the same corpus, structured-format compliance vs task accuracy

**Named by:** [Can ChatGPT Detect Intent? Evaluating Large Language Models for Spoken Language Understanding (Interspeech 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13512) (2023-05) · [Zero-Shot Spoken Language Understanding via Large Language Models: A Preliminary Study (LREC-COLING 2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.1554/) (2024-05) · [WHISMA: A Speech-LLM to Perform Zero-shot Spoken Language Understanding (SLT 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16423) (2024-08) · [ISA-Bench: Benchmarking Instruction Sensitivity for Large Audio Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23558) (2025-10)

### P-SLU-5 — ladder: c

Choice-surface bias and miscalibrated confidence block label-free evaluation and selection on frozen LALMs: shifting MCQ option order or paraphrasing distractors changes results substantially (accuracy std up to 13.7% across MMAU/MMAR/MMSU on four LALMs; distractor rephrasing causes the biggest joint drop in accuracy and consistency-of-response), and uncertainty estimation for audio-aware LLMs is 'largely unexplored', with method effectiveness highly model- and benchmark-dependent — so condition-(c) selection has no validated instrument for SLU on frozen audio models.

**Metric:** accuracy standard deviation / consistency-of-response under choice-order and paraphrase perturbation; ECE/AUROC of confidence vs correctness for LALM outputs

**Named by:** [Robustness assessment of large audio language models in multiple-choice evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04584) (2025-10) · [Walking Through Uncertainty: An Empirical Study of Uncertainty Estimation for Audio-Aware Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25591) (2026-04) · [Benchmarking and Confidence Evaluation of LALMs For Temporal Reasoning (Interspeech 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13115) (2025-05)


## Approach genealogy & evidence claims (cross-domain mandatory)

### L2-C1 — [new] origin: **llm** · transfer: untransferred · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-SLU-2

Training-free output calibration removes prompt-induced classification bias on frozen LLMs: contextual calibration (content-free 'N/A' input to estimate per-label bias) improves GPT-3/GPT-2 zero/few-shot classification by up to 30.0% absolute and reduces variance across prompt choices; Batch Calibration (ICLR 2024) marginalizes contextual bias over the unlabeled test batch, is inference-only, beats prior calibration baselines on PaLM-2 across 10+ NLU tasks, and extends to zero-shot CLIP. This directly targets the schema/verbalizer-sensitivity deficit (P-SLU-2) with no weight or prompt changes.

**Sources:** [Calibrate Before Use: Improving Few-Shot Performance of Language Models (ICML 2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09690) (2021-02) · [Batch Calibration: Rethinking Calibration for In-Context Learning and Prompt Engineering (ICLR 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17249) (2023-09) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Zhao et al. ICML 2021: up to +30.0% absolute accuracy and large variance reduction on GPT-3 across classification tasks, label-free (content-free probe); Zhou et al. ICLR 2024: SOTA calibration on PaLM-2 (S/M/L) and CLIP over >10 tasks, zero-shot inference-only.
*Speech-domain evidence:* none found — no application of contextual/batch calibration to audio-LLM or speech-intent zero-shot classification located (searched 2026-07-04); nearest audio analog is transductive CLAP batch correction (L2-C9), which is on environmental-sound datasets, not SLU.

### L2-C2 — [new] origin: **llm** · transfer: untransferred · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-SLU-5

A sizeable fraction of measured zero-shot classification deficit is scoring-surface artifact, not missing task knowledge: surface-form competition shows probability mass splits across paraphrase surface forms of the same answer, and PMI-based domain-conditional scoring (PMI_DC) achieves consistent zero-shot gains over calibrated and uncalibrated scoring on all GPT-2/GPT-3 models across a variety of multiple-choice datasets, without any training. For SLU this predicts that part of the frozen-model intent/choice deficit (P-SLU-5) is recoverable by rescoring alone.

**Sources:** [Surface Form Competition: Why the Highest Probability Answer Isn't Always Right (EMNLP 2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08315) (2021-04) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Holtzman et al. EMNLP 2021: PMI_DC scoring consistently improves zero-shot multiple-choice performance for GPT-2/GPT-3 model families across many datasets, training-free.
*Speech-domain evidence:* none found — no surface-form-competition or PMI-rescoring study on LALM MCQ/intent classification located; the LALM MCQ-robustness paper (arXiv:2510.04584) documents the symptom (distractor-phrasing swings) without the rescoring remedy.

### L2-C3 — [new] origin: **llm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: c · anchor: P-SLU-2

Label-free prompt/schema selection is achievable in text: choosing the template that maximizes input-output mutual information gets 90% of the way from average-prompt to best-prompt accuracy without any ground-truth labels (Sorensen et al., 8 datasets / 7 tasks), and Zero-Label Prompt Selection picks strong prompts via ensemble pseudo-labels with no labeled data or gradients. This is the origin-domain existence proof that the schema-sensitivity headroom (P-SLU-2) is realizable under condition (c), i.e. rho substantially > 0 for prompt selection on classification.

**Sources:** [An Information-theoretic Approach to Prompt Engineering Without Ground Truth Labels (ACL 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11364) (2022-03) · [Zero-Label Prompt Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.04668) (2022-11) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Sorensen et al. 2022: MI-based selection gets 90% of the way from average-prompt to best-prompt accuracy across 8 datasets, fully label-free; Liao et al. 2022 (ZPS): prompt selection via prompt-ensemble pseudo-labels on unlabeled data, no labels, no gradient updates, significant improvements over prior zero-label methods.
*Speech-domain evidence:* partial analog on the audio bi-encoder class only: entropy-guided automatic prompt weighting for zero-shot audio-language classification (arXiv:2601.05011, ICASSP 2026) is label-free; nothing found for generative audio-LLM SLU prompt selection.

### L2-C4 — [update] origin: **llm** · transfer: untransferred · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-SLU-2

Text-LLM automatic prompt optimization quantifies large prompt-space headroom (the H_prompt - H_fix object): OPRO-optimized instructions beat human prompts by up to 8% on GSM8K and up to 50% on Big-Bench Hard; APE reaches human-level instruction quality (better or comparable to human instructions on 19/24 tasks); GEPA's reflective prompt evolution outperforms GRPO weight training by 6% average / up to 20% with up to 35x fewer rollouts, and beats MIPROv2 by >10%. Caveat: these select with labeled dev scores, so they certify reachability (b2) but not label-free realizability (c).

**Sources:** [Large Language Models as Optimizers (OPRO, ICLR 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03409) (2023-09) · [Large Language Models Are Human-Level Prompt Engineers (APE, ICLR 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.01910) (2022-11) · [GEPA: Reflective Prompt Evolution Can Outperform Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19457) (2025-07) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* OPRO: +8% GSM8K / +50% BBH over human prompts on PaLM-2 and GPT models; GEPA (current abstract): beats GRPO by 6% avg (up to 20%) with up to 35x fewer rollouts, and beats MIPROv2 by >10% (e.g. +12% on AIME-2025) — the only literature where prompt-space headroom is quantified, as the yardstick memo notes.
*Speech-domain evidence:* none found — no APE/OPRO/GEPA-style automatic prompt search reported on a frozen generative audio-LLM for SLU or any speech-semantic task (searched 2026-07-04); audio prompt work is confined to CLAP-class bi-encoders (TSPE, entropy weighting).

### L2-C5 — [new] origin: **vlm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-SLU-2

Class-schema surface enrichment moves genuine zero-shot accuracy on frozen bi-encoders in the VLM origin domain: CuPL replaces bare label names with LLM-generated per-class descriptions and gains >1 point on ImageNet (plus a range of benchmarks) fully zero-shot; description-based classification (DCLIP) similarly improves accuracy while adding interpretability. This is the direct lineage of the in-house MInDS-14 +0.126 prompt/schema-surface selection anchor (bi-encoder class, stage-1 directional only).

**Sources:** [What does a platypus look like? Generating customized prompts for zero-shot image classification (CuPL, ICCV 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03320) (2022-09) · [Visual Classification via Description from Large Language Models (ICLR 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07183) (2022-10) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* CuPL: >1pp ImageNet gain and improvements across zero-shot benchmarks with no training; Menon & Vondrick ICLR 2023: description-based scoring improves zero-shot CLIP accuracy (incl. under distribution shift) and adds feature-level interpretability.
*Speech-domain evidence:* partial: descriptor enrichment transfers to audio bi-encoders for environmental/vocal sound classification (TSPE +1.23-16.36% absolute, arXiv:2501.00398; class-description prompting SOTA on ambient sound, arXiv:2409.13676) — but no published transfer to speech-semantic intent (MInDS-14/SLURP-class); in-house MInDS-14 +0.126 [0.077, 0.181] is stage-1 directional only.

### L2-C6 — [new] origin: **vlm** · transfer: untransferred · fence: training-free · ladder: b1 · anchor: P-SLU-2

WaffleCLIP control result: replacing LLM-generated class descriptors with RANDOM character/word sequences achieves similar zero-shot gains across many visual classification tasks — much of the descriptor-enrichment lift is a non-semantic ensemble/regularization effect, not added class knowledge. Consequence for our lane: the MInDS-14 +0.126 schema-surface gain must be treated b1/b2-unsplit until a random-descriptor ablation separates semantic reachability from surface artifact.

**Sources:** [Waffling around for Performance: Visual Classification with Random Words and Broad Concepts (ICCV 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07282) (2023-06) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Roth et al. ICCV 2023: random character/word descriptors match LLM-description gains across visual classification tasks; residual semantic value survives mainly via high-level concepts, challenging the semantic-content explanation of descriptor averaging.
*Speech-domain evidence:* none found — no random-descriptor / semantic-ablation control published for audio or speech prompt enrichment (searched 2026-07-04); the b1/b2 split for audio schema-surface gains is untested.

### L2-C7 — [new] origin: **vlm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-SLU-2

Prompt-ensembling/descriptor enrichment has landed in the audio bi-encoder class: TSPE builds task-specific prompt ensembles (sound attributes + sources) and improves zero-shot audio classification by 1.23-16.36% absolute over vanilla evaluation across 12 datasets, training-free and without any weight updates. Transfer remains confined to audio-event/vocal-sound surfaces — speech-intent SLU on this class is the unoccupied cell our anchor probes.

**Sources:** [TSPE: Task-Specific Prompt Ensemble for Improved Zero-Shot Audio Classification (ICASSP 2025 SALMA Workshop)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00398) (2025-01) · [A sound description: exploring prompt templates and class descriptions to enhance zero-shot audio classification (DCASE 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13676) (2024-09) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Method lineage is CLIP prompt ensembling / CuPL-class descriptor enrichment (80-template CLIP ensembles; CuPL >1pp ImageNet), transplanted onto CLAP-style audio-text encoders.
*Speech-domain evidence:* native for audio bi-encoders: TSPE +1.23-16.36% absolute across 12 audio classification datasets; DCASE 2024 study (arXiv:2409.13676) finds properly formatted bare labels competitive with optimized templates while LLM class descriptions reach SOTA zero-shot on ambient-sound datasets — but on sound events, not spoken intent; no SLURP/MInDS-class result found.

### L2-C8 — [new] origin: **vlm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: c · anchor: P-SLU-5

Label-free prompt weighting works in the audio domain: entropy-guided automatic prompt weighting (low prediction entropy as confidence proxy) yields consistent gains over classical prompt ensembling in zero-shot audio-language classification — reported accuracy improvements ~5x larger than ensembling across a 5-dataset benchmark, with negligible overhead and no annotations. This is speech-adjacent condition-(c) evidence that schema-surface headroom is partially realizable label-free on frozen audio encoders.

**Sources:** [Leveraging Prediction Entropy for Automatic Prompt Weighting in Zero-Shot Audio-Language Classification (ICASSP 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05011) (2026-01) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Entropy-as-confidence prompt adaptation originates in VLM test-time prompt work (TPT-class entropy minimization over frozen CLIP, NeurIPS 2022, arXiv:2209.07511) and CLIP prompt-ensemble weighting.
*Speech-domain evidence:* native for audio bi-encoders: consistent label-free gains over prompt ensembling on 5 audio classification datasets (environmental/urban/vocal), accuracy improvements 5x larger than ensembling across the whole benchmark, negligible overhead; PAT (NAACL 2025) similarly weights prompt ensembles by uncertainty — neither tested on spoken-intent SLU.

### L2-C9 — [new] origin: **vlm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: c · anchor: P-SLU-5

Transductive, label-free batch correction transfers from VLM to audio: a TransCLIP-style text-anchored spherical Gaussian-mixture EM over the unlabeled test batch refines CLAP zero-shot posteriors with no labels, no gradients, ~15 ms on one CPU core for 2,000 clips, gaining +4.6 to +9.2 points (ESC-50 89.1->94.8, UrbanSound8K 73.8->81.8; 96.2 with entropy-guided prompt weighting). Existence proof that condition-(c) machinery can realize headroom on frozen audio encoders — untested on speech-intent tasks.

**Sources:** [Transductive Zero-Shot Audio Classification with Audio-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17160) (2026-06) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Method is explicitly TransCLIP-style transductive inference developed for CLIP zero-shot classification, ported to CLAP.
*Speech-domain evidence:* native for audio bi-encoders: +4.6-9.2pp label-free on ESC-50/UrbanSound8K/VocalSound; needs ~2.5 test samples per class per batch (diminishing returns beyond ~5); gains attenuate but stay positive under 20:1 long-tailed batches; documented negative result on TUT Urban 2018 where the zero-shot baseline is near chance; no spoken-intent (SLURP/MInDS) evaluation.

### L2-C10 — [new] origin: **speech** · transfer: native · fence: trained-head-on-frozen · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-SLU-2

AHAMask shows the task mass exists in frozen LALMs but the instruction surface fails to activate it reliably: binary attention-head masks on the frozen decoder (trainable parameters = head count; weights unchanged) achieve comparable or better performance than instruction prompting on single and composite acoustic tasks, revealing 'functional pathways' that instructions may not reliably trigger. For P-SLU-2 this bounds the prompt surface from above: some reachable capability is NOT prompt-reachable at current robustness.

**Sources:** [AHAMask: Reliable Task Specification for Large Audio Language Models Without Instructions (AAAI 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01787) (2025-09) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* AAAI 2026: masking attention heads in the frozen LLM backbone of LALMs matches/beats instruction prompting on single and composite tasks; motivated by drastic prompt sensitivity of LALMs ('different instructions of the same intention can yield drastically different outcomes').
*Speech-domain evidence:* native (speech-domain paper). Text-LLM ancestry exists in function/task-vector and attention-head steering work (archived L10, Function Vectors arXiv:2310.15213).

### L2-C11 — [new] origin: **speech** · transfer: native · fence: training-free · ladder: b1 · anchor: P-SLU-5

LALM MCQ classification results are substantially choice-surface-driven: across MMAU, MMAR and MMSU with Audio Flamingo 2/3, Qwen2.5-Omni-7B and Kimi-Audio-7B, shifting option order or paraphrasing choices substantially changes results; altering distractor phrasing alone produces accuracy standard deviation up to 13.7% (e.g. Audio Flamingo 2 on MMAU: 70.6% default -> 42.7% +/-13.7% under distractor rephrasing), with distractor rephrasing causing the biggest joint drop in accuracy and consistency-of-response. This quantifies the b1 (format/surface) component that any SLU prompting gain must be split against.

**Sources:** [Robustness assessment of large audio language models in multiple-choice evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04584) (2025-10) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Speech-domain study; text-LLM ancestry is the MCQA position/option-bias literature (e.g. 'LLMs May Perform MCQA by Selecting the Least Incorrect Option', arXiv:2402.01349).
*Speech-domain evidence:* native: 3 audio benchmarks x 4 LALMs; accuracy std up to 13.7% under distractor rephrasing; 'distractor rephrasing resulted in the biggest drop in both accuracy and CoR'; proposes a variation-aware evaluation protocol and metric.

### L2-C12 — [new] origin: **speech** · transfer: native · fence: gradient-trained · ladder: background · anchor: P-SLU-3

Gradient-trained positioning reference for zero-shot SLU: WHISMA (Whisper encoder + Llama-3, parameter-efficient fine-tuning on SLU corpora) improves zero-shot SLURP slot filling by 26.6% relative over the prior SOTA and beats frozen Qwen-Audio by 33.0% relative SLU-F1, and leads on the unseen-domain SLU-GLUE benchmark it introduces. This is the fine-tuned ceiling our training-free fence is positioned against, and simultaneously documents how weak the frozen generative baseline is.

**Sources:** [WHISMA: A Speech-LLM to Perform Zero-shot Spoken Language Understanding (SLT 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16423) (2024-08) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* SLT 2024: +26.6% rel zero-shot SLURP slot filling over prior SOTA; +33.0% rel SLU-F1 over Qwen-Audio; introduces SLU-GLUE for unseen-domain generalization.
*Speech-domain evidence:* native (speech-domain paper).

### L2-C13 — [new] origin: **speech** · transfer: native · fence: gradient-trained · ladder: background · anchor: P-SLU-1

Speech-MASSIVE establishes the multilingual E2E-vs-cascaded reference: 12-language intent+slot SLU with both cascaded (Whisper + mT5) and end-to-end (Whisper fine-tuned) baselines across zero-shot/few-shot/full fine-tune; in the zero-shot (English-trained) condition the cascaded system outperforms E2E for all languages (avg intent accuracy 69.10 vs 57.07; same pattern on slot micro-F1), with cascaded and E2E showing similar average performance only in few-shot mode. Positioning evidence that the training-free E2E space starts behind a cascade on semantic SLU (P-SLU-1).

**Sources:** [Speech-MASSIVE: A Multilingual Speech Dataset for SLU and Beyond (Interspeech 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03900) (2024-08) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Interspeech 2024: multilingual (12 languages) intent/slot dataset with cascaded and E2E baselines in zero-shot, few-shot, and full fine-tune scenarios; 'In this condition (En), for zero-shot mode, cascaded SLU outperforms E2E SLU for all languages' (Table 2: 69.10 vs 57.07 avg intent accuracy).
*Speech-domain evidence:* native (speech-domain dataset/baseline paper).

### L2-C14 — [new] origin: **llm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 · anchor: P-SLU-1

The training-free cascaded frontier: ChatGPT-class text LLMs reach intent-classification accuracy close to supervised models with zero or few shots across languages GIVEN ORACLE TRANSCRIPTS, but are markedly worse at slot filling, degrade under real ASR errors, and single-GPU-scale open models fall far behind. Zero-shot prompt-based SLU 'preliminary study' (LREC-COLING 2024, GPT-SLU two-stage prompting with GPT-3.5/ChatGPT) reaches the same conclusion of significant-potential-with-room-to-improve. This is the origin-domain strongest training-free baseline for P-SLU-1/P-SLU-4.

**Sources:** [Can ChatGPT Detect Intent? Evaluating Large Language Models for Spoken Language Understanding (Interspeech 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13512) (2023-05) · [Zero-Shot Spoken Language Understanding via Large Language Models: A Preliminary Study (LREC-COLING 2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.1554/) (2024-05) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* He & Garner Interspeech 2023: ChatGPT near-supervised intent accuracy zero/few-shot on oracle transcripts, 'the model is worse at slot filling', 'performance is sensitive to ASR errors'; smaller single-GPU models substantially weaker.
*Speech-domain evidence:* partial: evaluated on spoken SLU corpora but only via the cascaded transcript path — the frozen E2E audio-LLM cell is not covered by this evidence.

### L2-C15 — [new] origin: **llm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: a · anchor: P-SLU-1

Support injection for cascaded SLU without any training: prompting a frozen LLM with word confusion networks from ASR lattices (instead of 1-best transcripts) improves ASR-robust SLU on spoken question answering and intent classification, reducing the gap between top-1-transcript and oracle performance — the lattice carries alternative hypotheses the 1-best surface lacks, an (a)-condition support-expansion move realized purely in-context. The E2E analog (the omni model expanding its own hypothesis support for SLU) remains unoccupied, consistent with the yardstick's routing table.

**Sources:** [Towards ASR Robust Spoken Language Understanding Through In-Context Learning With Word Confusion Networks (ICASSP 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02921) (2024-01) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* ICL prompting of a frozen LLM with confusion-network alternatives from ASR lattices; experiments on spoken question answering and intent classification show improved resilience to noisy transcripts vs 1-best prompting, narrowing the top-1-to-oracle gap.
*Speech-domain evidence:* partial: applied to speech tasks but through the cascaded path (external ASR lattice feeding a text LLM); no frozen omni/audio-LLM self-generated-support variant found.

### L2-C16 — [update] origin: **llm** · transfer: partial · fence: training-free · ladder: c · anchor: P-SLU-4

Self-consistency-style aggregation is beginning to transfer to SLU: SFL-MTSC (Interspeech 2026) applies zero-shot, prompt-based multi-path self-consistency at the semantic-frame level (domain-intent grouping + slot-level clustering with path-support scoring over sampled LLM outputs) and improves slot F1 and overall accuracy over single-path inference on the MAC-SLU multi-intent benchmark, with intent accuracy largely stable across most settings. Condition-(c) aggregation evidence for slots (P-SLU-4); the frozen E2E audio-LLM variant is still untested.

**Sources:** [SFL-MTSC: Leveraging Semantic Frame-Level Multi-Task Self-Consistency for Robust Multi-Intent Spoken Language Understanding (Interspeech 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25552) (2026-06) · [Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning in Language Models (ICLR 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171) (2022-03) · verified: True

*Origin-domain evidence:* Self-consistency (Wang et al., ICLR 2023) is the canonical text-LLM sample-and-aggregate result (archived); SFL-MTSC adapts it to intent-specific frames and slot clusters with path-support scoring.
*Speech-domain evidence:* partial: SFL-MTSC operates on prompt-based SLU with LLMs (MAC-SLU; abstract does not specify input modality — appears transcript-side rather than raw-audio E2E) — first SLU-specific self-consistency instance found; no frozen audio-LLM self-consistency SLU study located.

# L2-SLU positioning — training-free vs fine-tuned SOTA (intent + slot family)

**Fine-tuned ceiling (out-of-fence, positioning only).** The strongest zero-shot SLU numbers on SLURP-class tasks come from gradient training: WHISMA (Whisper+Llama-3, PEFT on SLU corpora) gains +26.6% rel zero-shot slot SLU-F1 over prior SOTA and +33.0% rel over frozen Qwen-Audio ([2408.16423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16423)); instruction-tuned text LLMs (ILLUMINER, [2403.17536](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17536), FLAN-T5 11B beating GPT-3.5 ICL on slots by 11.1–32.2pp) dominate few-shot intent+slot; FSA-GRPO-class RL post-training installs few-shot leverage on omni models (archived). ISA-Bench adds a decisive cost datum for the gradient route: fine-tuning Qwen2-Audio for instruction compliance 'induces nontrivial catastrophic forgetting' of mastered tasks ([2510.23558](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23558)) — robustness bought by weights is not free.

**Training-free frontier, by model class.**
- *Cascaded (text-LLM over transcripts)*: the strongest in-fence baseline. ChatGPT-class models reach near-supervised **intent** accuracy zero/few-shot on oracle transcripts but stay weak on **slots** and degrade under ASR noise ([2305.13512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13512); [LREC-COLING 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.1554/)); confusion-network prompting partially repairs the ASR-noise hole ([2401.02921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02921)).
- *Bi-encoder (CLAP/omni-embedding)*: schema-surface engineering is established and label-free machinery exists — TSPE prompt ensembles +1.23–16.36% abs ([2501.00398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00398)), entropy-weighted prompts ([2601.05011](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05011)), transductive batch EM +4.6–9.2pp ([2606.17160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17160)) — but **only on audio-event/vocal-sound surfaces** (and the transductive gains come with a documented TUT-2018 negative result where the zero-shot baseline is near chance). Speech-semantic intent on this class is an unoccupied cell; our MInDS-14 **+0.126 [0.077, 0.181]** (stage-1 directional only, in-house) is the only datum we know of, and WaffleCLIP ([2306.07282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07282)) mandates a random-descriptor ablation before it can be graded b2 rather than b1.
- *Frozen generative audio-LLM (the house cell)*: essentially empty. No calibration transfer (contextual/batch), no automatic prompt search (APE/OPRO/GEPA-class), no best-of-N/pass@k headroom measurement, and no validated label-free selector — while the text origin domain has quantified all four (calibration up to +30% abs [2102.09690](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09690); MI prompt selection recovering 90% of the average-to-best-prompt gap label-free [2203.11364](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11364); GEPA beating GRPO by 6% avg (up to 20%) with up to 35× fewer rollouts [2507.19457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19457)). AHAMask ([2509.01787](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01787), trained-head-on-frozen) shows the task mass is *in* the frozen model when the prompt surface fails — evidence the deficit is reachability/realizability, not support.

**Net position.** For SLU, the training-free fence is not competing with fine-tuned SOTA head-on; it is competing with (i) the cascaded text-LLM baseline (must be reported for every claim) and (ii) the *unmeasured* prompt-space headroom H_prompt−H_fix on frozen omni models — where the calibration + label-free-selection genealogy gives concrete, cheap, transferable instruments whose speech transfer is verified-absent.
