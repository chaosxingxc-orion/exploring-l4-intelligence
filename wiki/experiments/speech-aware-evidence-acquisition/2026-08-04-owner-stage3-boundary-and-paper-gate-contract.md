# Owner Stage-3 boundary and paper-gate contract

This contract fixes **speech-aware evidence acquisition** as a Stage-2 study carrier, and moves the
Stage-3 mission (large-scale empirical work and publication) out of this study's default
authorization. The existing speech-only boundary, the semantic identity, the E0→R0→R1→X sequence,
the budget, and every historical exposure/authorization fact are unchanged and are never written
back.

## Retained authorization (unchanged)

What the 2026-08-04 identity contract and the Stage-2A entry sequence
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-02-speech-aware-evidence-acquisition-stage2a-entry.md`) already
granted: E0 model-free closure, the runtime receipt, the R0 vertical slice, R1 closest/
strongest-prior reproduction, and bounded X directional exploration (discovery data, within the
budget ceiling, used for candidate selection).

## Study endpoint (newly defined)

This study's Stage-2 endpoint is to form one or more falsifiable, reproducible **qualified paper
candidates** (improvement claim and null hypothesis, mechanism, baseline receipts, experimental and
statistical design, unread-confirmatory declaration) — not to complete the final paper experiments.

## Forbidden by default (promotion through `OWNER_GO_AND_PAPER_EXECUTION_CONTRACT` into a standalone paper repository must come first)

- production-scale method implementations and large-scale confirmatory campaigns;
- final superiority/generalization conclusions; a bounded-probe result must never be written up as
  a paper-level claim;
- manuscript, submission, publication release.

## Integrity discipline

Discovery/confirmatory isolation and exposure accounting continue to run under the contracts in
force (study repo `docs/exposure-ledger.md`; a confirmatory consumption row carries the split
identity hash and the consumed flag — see the 2026-08-03 program-visibility discipline). A zero or
negative result is a legitimate completion form for the study; candidates must never share
unregistered test exposure. One candidate receiving a paper GO authorizes no other candidate of
this study.

## Owner acceptance

Accepted by owner on 2026-08-04 (session direction; recorded in Decision-Log-2026-08 continuation
entry 91).
