## Abstract

Frozen large audio and omni-modal models are documented to be severely instruction-sensitive: same-intent paraphrases swing task performance and can even flip which task is executed, while attention-head-masking evidence shows the task competence exists inside the frozen weights. In the text-LLM origin domain, prompt-space headroom is quantified (APE, OPRO, GEPA — the last outperforming GRPO weight training at a fraction of the rollouts); for audio-in models no published work quantifies that headroom's magnitude — a single two-point existence positive [60] aside — and the automatic-prompt-optimization field's own survey and its multimodal expansion both stop before audio. This Stage-1 survey asks, from the ICL perspective, whether the optimization space of instruct-prompt-driven rollout on a frozen omni speech model is sufficient for semantic tasks — ASR/ST, SLU, spoken-QA, and speech-agentic task completion — and, only if not, whether an agentic system should extend it. Contributions: an operational sufficiency yardstick separating support (a), prompt-reachability (b1/b2), and label-free realizability (c); an eleven-family cross-domain transfer map with origin-domain attribution; four task-family deep dives; a per-family ladder evidence table in which verified-empty cells are first-class negative results; and eight unranked candidate research problems. The central cell, H_prompt − H_fix, has zero published quantification of its *magnitude* — a single two-point existence comparison [60] (PromptingWhisper) aside — for any audio-in model, and existing ≥20-template evaluations [2] already contain H_prompt(K, N=1)-computable data by re-analysis; a budget-matched in-house probe (n=50, measuring the matched-budget diversity contrast Δ_BM, not the budget-unconstrained H_prompt − H_fix) [directional-only] read uninformative-to-weakly-fix-favoring for ASR at this budget, its two readouts (oracle −0.00137, MBR +0.0045) disagreeing in sign with both CIs spanning zero. The survey recommends; the owner decision document decides.

## S1 — Introduction and Motivation

### 1.1 A capability trapped behind a brittle surface

Frozen large audio-language and omni-modal models exhibit a well-documented and troubling dissociation: the task competence appears to be present in the weights, yet the instruction surface that is supposed to invoke it is unreliable. ISA-Bench, among the first benchmarks to systematically vary instruction wording for large audio-language models (AudioBench's twenty-plus prompt templates predate it), finds that same-intent paraphrases of a task instruction swing measured performance substantially [1]; and the multi-task evaluations of AudioBench document the sharper failure — SALMONN, given reworded ASR prompts, is observed to perform phoneme recognition or unrequested translation instead of transcription, and AudioBench added twenty-plus prompt templates in response — a prompt-induced task flip that AudioBench, not ISA-Bench, records [2]. Most strikingly, AHAMask shows that masking a small, selected set of attention heads in a frozen audio-language model elicits reliable task execution *without any instruction at all* — direct evidence that the task mass exists inside the frozen model while the natural-language instruction surface fails to activate it dependably [3]. If a frozen model can do the task when its heads are steered but not when its prompt is paraphrased, then the space of instructions is not a neutral interface: it is an optimization space, and its extent is an empirical question.

### 1.2 An asymmetry between domains

That question has been answered — quantitatively, repeatedly, and at scale — in exactly one origin domain: text-only LLMs. There, automatic prompt search established early that machine-generated instructions reach human-level quality on 19 of 24 instruction-induction tasks (APE) [4]; that treating the prompt as the optimization variable yields gains of up to 8% on GSM8K and up to 50% on Big-Bench Hard over human-written prompts (OPRO) [5]; and, most consequentially for the present survey, that reflective prompt evolution can *outperform GRPO weight training* by 6% on average and up to 20%, while using up to 35× fewer rollouts (GEPA) [6] — a direct, measured comparison between optimizing the prompt space and optimizing the weights of the same system. For audio-in models, no published work quantifies the *magnitude* of that headroom — a single two-point existence comparison ([60], §4.2, showing H_prompt − H_fix > 0 from prompt manipulation alone) aside — anywhere in the literature we surveyed; indeed the ≥20-template evaluations already inside AudioBench [2] contain H_prompt(K, N=1)-computable data that no one has re-analyzed as headroom. The automatic-prompt-optimization field's own systematic survey covers no audio domain [7], and the field's dedicated modality expansion, Multimodal Prompt Optimization, extends the machinery to images, videos, and molecules — and stops before audio [8]. This is the central empirical hole the survey maps: the one domain in which prompt-space headroom has been priced is not the domain we care about, and the domain we care about has never priced it.

### 1.3 The question, and what kind of document this is

The question this survey serves is posed verbatim: *from the ICL perspective, is the optimization space of instruct-prompt-driven rollout on a frozen omni speech model sufficient for semantic tasks — and only if not, should an agentic system extend it?* The framing matters as much as the content. This paper is a Stage-1 (problem-definition) document within a three-stage methodology: its argumentation must stand on published literature and theory; in-house small-n numbers appear only as directional signals, each carrying an explicit grade tag ([scoped], [hypothesis-grade], [directional], [settled (vector class)]) assigned by a dated evidence re-grade that audited every prior in-house claim against Stage-1 standards. One consequence of that audit is worth stating up front, because it motivates the survey's center of gravity: the strongest in-house best-of-N pipeline ran a *single fixed instruction* end-to-end, so the prompt-space dimension of the question had zero in-house measurement — the survey must argue it from literature or declare it open. Accordingly, this paper argues, bounds, and recommends candidate research problems; it decides nothing. Ranking and selection belong to the owner's problem-definition discussion that consumes this document.

### 1.4 Scope: why the semantic layer

We fix scope to the *semantic layer* — ASR and speech translation, spoken language understanding (intent and slots), spoken question answering and audio reasoning, and speech-agentic task completion. This is not an arbitrary restriction but the consequence of a consolidated premise: for training-free work on frozen omni models, the shallow (paralinguistic) speech signals are out of scope. On the contrastive bi-encoder (vector) class, the evidence is graded settled: speaker identity is never written to the pooled output vector, across all probed layers, pooling methods, and seeds, and emotion is present in the representation but unread at the single-vector readout [settled (vector class)]. On the generative (Thinker-Talker) class the premise is literature-only and negative-leaning [hypothesis-grade]: paralinguistic information demonstrably exists in the token stream, but no training-free activation of it has been located.

The 2026 delta scan sustains this standing. Frozen audio-language models remain unreliable judges of speaker consistency across dialogue turns, with textual content actively suppressing acoustic judgment [9]. Every robust paralinguistic recovery published through mid-2026 changes weights — bridging paralinguistic perception to dialogue behavior [10], speaker-specialized fine-tuning [14], and reasoning-model speaker recognition in long-form media [15] are all gradient-trained. The only training-free positives on record are selection effects operating on a near-chance base, reported without label-sensitivity or acoustic-grounding controls [11][12]; the remaining entries are a marginal bi-encoder prompt-ensemble caveat [13] and a trained-readout middle lane that adapts statistics at test time rather than prompting [16]. The premise is falsifiable — it is overturned the day a training-free, prompt-or-ICL-only recovery of speaker identity or emotion on a frozen omni model survives label-sensitivity and acoustic-grounding controls — and as of 2026-07-04 the watch has not been triggered. The semantic layer, where content fidelity on the vector class is near-ceiling and generation is pretraining-native, is where the sufficiency question is live.

## S2 — The Sufficiency Yardstick

### 2.1 Why a yardstick must precede the answer

"Sufficiency" has, at present, no measurable definition in the literature. Theory constrains the distribution *reachable by selection on a fixed base policy* q₀, but no support-expansion or ceiling-movement theorem exists; and — the deeper problem — "the task mass exists in the frozen model" has never been separated from "the task mass is reachable by prompting." AHAMask's head-masking result [3] makes the distinction vivid: existence and prompt-reachability can come apart. Answering the owner's question without first fixing definitions would collapse these back together. This section therefore fixes an operational yardstick before any evidence is weighed, so that S4–S8 can fill its cells and S9 can route its failures.

### 2.2 The chain of fractions

Fix a task family *T* with family metric *m*, an instruction (task-definition) set of size *K*, and a per-input sampling budget *N*. The yardstick is a chain of three quantities, each attached to one rung of what we will call the **sufficiency ladder** — condition (a) *support*, condition (b) *reachability*, condition (c) *realizability*:

- **H_fix(T, N)** — the oracle-over-sampling headroom under *one fixed instruction*: the gain of an oracle selector over the default decode when N rollouts are drawn from the frozen model with the instruction held constant. This measures condition (a) restricted to the current conditioning: does the frozen q₀ place non-trivial mass on higher-reward outputs at all? The in-house anchor is an ASR oracle headroom of +0.0418 [0.0289, 0.0564] at N=8 under +5 dB SNR (mild additive noise) [scoped — one instruction, one model, one condition].

- **H_prompt(T, K, N)** — the oracle headroom when sampling ranges over *K instructions × N rollouts*. The prompt-space contribution has **two non-equivalent operationalizations**, distinguished here because they behave differently in sign and only one is what a budget-capped probe can measure. (1) The **budget-unconstrained nested-pool contribution**, H_prompt(T, K, N) − H_fix(T, N): the oracle over K×N rollouts minus the oracle over N rollouts under one fixed instruction. Because the K-instruction pool *nests* the single-instruction pool, this quantity is **nonnegative by construction** — enlarging the candidate set can only raise the oracle. It is the direct operationalization of the owner's question and the intellectual center of gravity of this survey (a *motivational* label — the one empty cell that made the survey necessary — not a ranking of any candidate problem; see §9.1). (2) The **matched-budget diversity contrast**, Δ_BM(T, N) ≔ H_{K×(N/K)} − H_{1×N}: at a *fixed* total rollout budget, the oracle when that budget is spread across K instructions minus the oracle when it is spent on depth under one instruction. Δ_BM is **sign-free** — spreading a fixed budget across instructions can help (diversity) or hurt (lost depth) — and it is the quantity a budget-capped probe actually estimates (§8.3); a negative Δ_BM is fully compatible with a strictly positive nested-pool H_prompt − H_fix. The nested-pool quantity's measurement status at survey time was doubly zero: no in-house measurement existed (the pipeline that produced the anchor above ran one fixed instruction end-to-end), and no published quantification of its magnitude exists for omni speech models (§8.2–§8.3). The closest in-house evidence for condition (b) on any surface is the MInDS-14 intent gain of +0.126 [0.077, 0.181] from prompt/schema-surface variation on a frozen bi-encoder [scoped, b1/b2-unsplit — see §2.5].

- **ρ(T)** — the *realization fraction*: the best label-free selector's gain divided by the oracle headroom it realizes. Because there are two oracle headrooms, ρ splits accordingly: **ρ_fix** (selector gain ÷ H_fix — the fraction of fixed-instruction oracle mass a deployable selector harvests) and **ρ_prompt** (selector gain ÷ H_prompt — the fraction of the K-instruction oracle mass). Oracle headroom that no deployable selector can harvest is not usable headroom; ρ is condition (c) made quantitative. Where this survey states a bare ρ for ASR it means **ρ_fix**, since H_fix is the only ASR oracle so far measured in-house; the ledger and §4.1-P3 use the same ρ_fix convention. The house prior for ASR is ρ_fix ≈ 0 — MBR selection was null on two evaluation slices and a memory-based selector returned exactly zero [hypothesis-grade] — against one external in-fence positive, a frozen-LM utility swap inside MBR worth 9.0% relative WER [25].

**Sufficiency(T) at budget (K, N)** is then defined as: H_prompt(T, K, N) ≥ δ_T **and** H_prompt(T, K, N) − H_fix(T, N) ≥ δ′_T **and** ρ(T) ≥ ρ_min, for thresholds δ_T, δ′_T, ρ_min to be fixed at Stage-2 pre-registration. The middle conjunct is deliberate: without it a family could be scored "sufficient" on fixed-instruction support alone, with zero prompt-space contribution — which would make the owner's question vacuous — so sufficiency here requires that the *prompt space itself* carry non-trivial mass. The definition makes sufficiency a *budgeted, per-family* property rather than a global boolean. Two readouts of the prompt-space contribution must not be conflated: holding the total rollout budget fixed at B = K·N, the oracle can differ between a K-instruction spread and single-instruction depth — that matched-budget difference is Δ_BM — whereas the budget-unconstrained nested-pool contribution H_prompt − H_fix (which *adds* the K instructions on top of the fixed-instruction budget rather than redistributing it) is the estimand Stage-2 would pre-register; Δ_BM is only the budget-capped proxy a Stage-1 probe can afford. (The earlier draft's "(K=32, N=8) vs (K=1, N=8)" illustration conflated the prompt contribution with a 32× budget increase and is withdrawn.) What Stage-1 evidence would argue *against* proceeding — the falsifier this survey can state — is a measured near-zero nested-pool H_prompt − H_fix at adequate K on a family where support is already known real (ASR): a null there, unlike a null in Δ_BM at tiny budget, would be a genuine negative for the prompt-space thesis. Whether such a null suffices to halt Stage-2 is an owner decision this survey does not adjudicate.

### 2.3 What theory covers: conditions (a) and (c)

Conditions (a) and (c) rest on genuine theory, imported wholesale from the text-LLM origin domain. On the sampling side, repeated-sampling coverage grows log-linearly with N across four orders of magnitude, while common label-free selection methods plateau far below oracle — the coverage/selection gap is the empirical signature of (a) outrunning (c) [17], and compute-optimal allocation results extend the picture to when sampling beats scaling [18]. On the selection side, best-of-N over a frozen base policy obeys a KL trust-region bound, KL(π_BoN ‖ q₀) ≤ log N − (N−1)/N, so selection can only re-weight mass the base policy already carries [19]; soft best-of-N characterizes the trade-off continuum [20]. Reward-guided selection is further bounded above by over-optimization: pushing harder against an imperfect proxy reward eventually degrades true task quality [21], and there exists an interior optimum N* beyond which additional sampling hurts — inference-time reward hacking with a tunable hedge [22].

The ASR instantiation of this theory is unusually mature: the n-best oracle framing has a decades-long lineage and its modern form, the HyPoradise oracle tables, prices exactly how much (a)-headroom sits in ASR hypothesis lists [23]. On the (c) side, self-certainty — the strongest cheap label-free selector from the text domain — remains below the oracle at every N [24], and a systematic anatomy of the CTC oracle gap finds eleven CTC-internal selectors null, with a frozen-LM utility swap inside MBR as the lone training-free positive [25]. The theory and its speech instantiation agree: condition (a) is measurable and often generous; condition (c) is measurable and usually stingy.

### 2.4 Instruments that make H_prompt estimable

Condition (b) has no theorems (§2.6), but it does have instruments — all training-free, task-agnostic, and imported from text-LLM evaluation research. FormatSpread computes performance *spread intervals* across semantically equivalent prompt formats at modest cost, turning sensitivity into a measured interval rather than an anecdote [26]. Multi-prompt evaluation shows single-prompt benchmarking produces unstable model rankings, motivating distribution-over-prompts reporting as the default [27]. PromptEval estimates the full performance distribution across 100 prompts at roughly 2× the cost of a single-prompt evaluation — and a max-over-K quantile of that distribution *is* H_prompt(K) on the evaluation side [28]. The execution-accuracy metric of instruction induction supplies the founding measurement convention [29]. The significance of this inventory for the survey is a one-line verdict recorded in the cross-cutting lane: for audio-LLMs, *sensitivity* measurement exists (ISA-Bench-class [1]), but *headroom* measurement does not — no published work runs any of these instruments, or any prompt search, against a frozen speech or omni model (S8 tabulates this absence family by family).

### 2.5 The b1/b2 split inside condition (b)

A prompt-driven lift that merely normalizes output format does not count as reachability of task mass. Condition (b) therefore splits internally: **b1** — format/schema-compliance movement (the answer becomes parseable, the label set is respected); **b2** — genuine accuracy movement, certified by two controls: *label-sensitivity* (the gain vanishes when demonstration or descriptor labels are shuffled — the control introduced by Min et al. for text-domain ICL [164]) and *acoustic-grounding* (the gain vanishes when the audio is ablated or swapped). Both confounds are documented, one in each neighboring domain. ALICE, the audio instance, finds that in-context demonstrations fix format compliance while *degrading* accuracy for large audio-language models — a pure-b1 gain wearing a b2 costume [30]. WaffleCLIP, the canonical misattribution control from the VLM domain, shows that random character-string descriptors match LLM-generated semantic descriptors for zero-shot classification, so schema-surface gains attributed to semantics may be structural artifacts [31]. The consequence for our own evidence is binding: the in-house MInDS-14 +0.126 [scoped] is a prompt/schema-surface gain of exactly the kind WaffleCLIP warns about, and it remains **b1/b2-unsplit** — citable as (b)-evidence in the aggregate, but not as b2 — until a random-descriptor ablation at equal K is run.

### 2.6 The theory asymmetry, stated plainly

The asymmetry that shapes this survey's entire argumentative strategy is this: **conditions (a) and (c) have theorems; condition (b) has none.** The KL trust-region, the coverage laws, and the over-optimization ceiling all constrain sampling and selection on a *fixed* conditional distribution. No published result bounds how far changing the instruction can move a frozen model's output distribution — prompt-reachability is unbounded above and below by current theory, and must be treated as an empirical parameter per task family. The nearest formal object is the analysis of in-context learning as implicit Bayesian inference: demonstrations serve to *locate* latent tasks (concepts) acquired during pretraining rather than to teach new ones [32] — a result proven in a synthetic / meta-learning setting (the GINC dataset), so its transfer to frozen omni speech models is an analogy, not a theorem. (This nearest-object role substitutes for the yardstick memo's original pointer to a different formal analysis; the substitution is recorded here for audit.) Read through our yardstick, this gives a soft upper bound: (b)-headroom is capped by pretrained support — prompting can sharpen the posterior over tasks q₀ already contains, but cannot move mass q₀ lacks. That is a direction, not a magnitude. Two consequences follow. First, (b) carries the heaviest argumentation burden in this paper: where (a) and (c) can be bounded, (b) can only be triangulated. Second, the triangulation source is forced: the text-LLM prompt-optimization literature [4][5][6] is the *only* place prompt-space headroom has been quantified at all, so a cross-domain survey with explicit origin-domain attribution is not a stylistic choice but the only available method.

### 2.7 Failure routing: the yardstick as a problem generator

The yardstick would be sterile if it only issued verdicts. Its purpose is generative: each condition's *failure* opens a different, well-posed research-problem family, and this routing is how the agentic question legitimately re-enters the program — as a conditional consequence of a measured failure, never as the starting point. Table 2.1 fixes the routing.

**Table 2.1 — Failure routing from yardstick conditions to research-problem families.**

| Failing condition | Diagnosis | Problem family opened | Anchor evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Support** — H_fix ≈ 0 even at large N | the frozen q₀ lacks high-reward mass for T | **external support injection** — retrieval, hypothesis generation, lexicon/context injection; the legitimate agentic re-entry point | ProGRes: prompted generation of *new* hypotheses beyond the n-best list, 5–25% relative WER [33]; RECOVER: agentic orchestration of hypothesis variants for entity correction [34]; "the omni model expands its *own* support" is the named unoccupied cell |
| **(b) Reachability** — H_prompt − H_fix ≈ 0 despite mass existing | task mass exists but instruction/task-definition/ICL surfaces cannot move q₀ onto it | **conditioning research** — task-activating prompting, demonstration selection, iterative re-prompting; agentic re-prompting loops as escalation | TAP: task-activating prompting [35]; ALICE format-vs-accuracy split [30]; APE/OPRO/GEPA-class headroom distributions, text domain only [4][5][6] |

### 2.9 What Stage 1 does, and does not do, with this yardstick

Stage 1 does not attempt to measure H_prompt, H_fix, or ρ to decision-grade precision. It does three things. First, it *bounds plausible ranges* from others' published numbers: the text-domain prompt-optimization gains [4][5][6] bound what (b)-headroom has looked like where it was measured; the oracle-gap literature [17][23][24][25] bounds (a) and (c). Second, it *fixes definitions* — the chain of fractions above — so that Stage 2 has a pre-registrable target rather than a vibe. Third, it reserves **at most one directional probe**, pre-authorized and tightly scoped: K instructions × small N on MInDS-14 or LibriSpeech, at most half a GPU-day, at most 200 items, single-touch, graded `directional-only` on arrival. That probe occupies the [PROBE-PENDING] slot of the S8 sufficiency analysis (§8.3, filled at assembly time with a directional-only result); its design constraints are specified there. No sufficiency verdict is issued anywhere in this paper: on the evidence assembled below, the question is answerable but unanswered, and the yardstick exists so that answering it becomes a measurement problem rather than a debate.

## S3 — Cross-Domain Method Taxonomy & Transfer Map

### 3.1 How to read the map

Every training-free lever available to a frozen omni speech model today descends from a method family developed first for text-only LLMs or, in a smaller number of cases, for vision–language models; none of the eleven families' *modern LLM-era instantiations* originated in speech, though two have classical speech-native ancestors noted in place — F1's n-best oracle analysis, and F2's minimum-Bayes-risk decoding, formalized for ASR by Goel & Byrne in 2000 [165]. This section makes that genealogy explicit, because the sufficiency question of S2 cannot be answered from the speech literature alone: condition (b) — prompt-reachability — has been quantified only in the text-LLM origin domain, and the failure modes that decide whether a family survives modality transfer are documented mainly in the VLM literature, which crossed the perception boundary first. Each family therefore carries five attributes throughout: its text-LLM origin with the key quantified result, its VLM transfer reference including the documented failure mode, its speech status (native / partial / untransferred, with citation), its ladder condition (a / b1 / b2 / c), and its fence (training-free / trained-head-on-frozen / gradient-trained). The fence convention is applied uniformly throughout: **training-free** forbids *new* training but permits an off-the-shelf trained utility or judge used as-is — so a pretrained pseudo-log-likelihood LM used as an MBR utility [25], a neural COMET-class metric used as-is [38][39], and even an audio-blind trained QE head such as BLASER 2.0-QE [124] all count in-fence *as utilities*, even where (as with BLASER) the head is inadequate as a reward for a given task. Table 3.1 gives the map; the prose that follows walks the families in order. Four of them — verifier-guided best-of-N (F1–F3), automatic prompt search (F4), self-correction (F8), and single-session agentic scaffolding (F11) — were subjected to full three-way WHY/HOW/WHAT comparisons, and each of those subsections closes with the corresponding transfer verdict: what to import first, and the origin- or VLM-documented risk that travels with it.

### 3.2 F1 — Repeated sampling and coverage

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: native · fence: training-free · ladder: (a))*

The foundation of every test-time family is the observation that a frozen sampler's *support* exceeds its greedy output: repeated sampling makes coverage (pass@N) grow log-linearly over four orders of magnitude of N [17], and compute-optimally allocated test-time compute can outperform a roughly fourteen-times-larger model [18]. This establishes (a)-type support headroom as a generic property of frozen samplers, not a task-specific accident. The VLM literature contributes the boundary condition: coverage converts to realized accuracy only where answers are automatically checkable, and test-time-scaling gains on perception-focused benchmarks are limited [77]. For speech the family is native rather than imported — the thirty-year n-best oracle-gap literature is exactly a pass@N coverage measurement, and HyPoradise institutionalizes it with oracle tables over 334K+ hypothesis–transcription pairs [23]. The frozen-*omni* analog is the in-house anchor: on frozen Qwen3-Omni-30B at +5 dB SNR (mild additive noise), greedy WER 0.1183 against an oracle-over-8-samples of 0.0765, an oracle reduction of +0.0418 [0.0289, 0.0564] [scoped — one instruction, one model, one condition]. Because that pool was generated under a single fixed instruction, it measures H_fix, not H_prompt — the distinction the whole survey turns on.

### 3.3 F2 — Consensus and MBR selection

*(origin: text-LLM/MT · speech transfer: native (ASR/ST), K×N cell untransferred · fence: training-free · ladder: (c))*

Consensus is the cheapest label-free realization instrument: self-consistency lifts GSM8K by +17.9% by majority-voting sampled reasoning paths [36], and Universal Self-Consistency extends consensus selection to free-form generation by letting the model pick the most mutually consistent candidate [37]. The generative-decoding form of the same idea, minimum-Bayes-risk selection, is in fact speech-native in origin — Goel & Byrne formalized minimum-Bayes-risk ASR over word lattices in 2000 [165] — and its modern neural-utility form matured in machine translation [38] before transferring back to speech: MBR over Whisper sample pools outperforms beam search for ASR and ST in most settings [39]. One text-domain extension deserves emphasis because it is the exact operationalization of the survey's central quantity: multi-prompt MBR builds the candidate pool under K prompts rather than one, jointly measuring the prompt-space contribution (H_prompt − H_fix) and supplying a deployable selector [40] — and it has no speech instance; the K-instruction × N-rollout cell is unoccupied. The VLM failure mode to import is that consensus can be a label-prior artifact rather than perceptual evidence: retrieval-augmented demonstration selection in VLMs collapses to majority voting over context labels [73] (cross-referenced at F6). The in-house counter-datum is that overlap-utility MBR over the F1 pools was null at N=8 (+0.0037 [−0.0082, 0.0170]) [hypothesis-grade] — in tension with the Whisper-positive result [39], which is itself a datum: pool geometry from an instruct-prompted omni chat model may differ from an encoder–decoder's.

### 3.4 F3 — Verifier-guided selection

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: partial · fence: mostly trained-head-on-frozen · ladder: (c))*

In the origin domain, the realized fraction of best-of-N coverage rides overwhelmingly on *trained* verifiers: outcome verifiers let a 6B model with verification beat a 175B model without it [41], and process supervision reaches ~78% on a MATH-subset best-of-N reranking, beating outcome reward models and majority voting [42]. The same source that established coverage scaling also established the selection plateau — coverage 79.8%→95.3% from 100 to 10k samples while reward-model selection moved 38.7%→39.8% [17]. The VLM column doubled down on trained verifier heads: VisualPRM adds +5.9 points average across seven reasoning benchmarks at matched pools [43], and VisVM's TD-trained value model anticipates hallucination downstream of locally plausible steps [45]. But VL-RewardBench shows the verifier itself is the failure point: multimodal judges fail predominantly at basic *perception* rather than reasoning, with GPT-4o at only 65.4% pairwise accuracy — and downstream best-of-N gain tracks judge strength [44]. This is the warning speech must import verbatim. On the speech side the family is only partially occupied. WavReward prices what any prompted judge must chase — a trained generalist evaluator lifts spoken-dialogue evaluation from a 53.4% baseline to 91.5% [46] — while the first systematic audio test-time-compute study supplies the lone frozen-judge positive: five LLM-origin TTC methods on five frozen audio LLMs yield 9–150% relative gains, but on synthetic auditory-cognition tasks, not deployment ASR/SLU/SQA benchmarks, and with the best method model-dependent [47].

**Transfer verdict (best-of-N + verifier).** Import first the *utility-function swap* over identical stored pools: self-certainty, the policy-internal confidence selector that keeps scaling where majority voting saturates [24], and the frozen-LM pseudo-log-likelihood MBR utility that produced the only in-fence label-free ASR selection positive (~9% relative WER on LibriSpeech test-other, after eleven CTC-internal selectors came up null) [25]. Both attack condition (c) directly, at zero gradient cost, on pools that already exist. The biggest imported risk is the perception-blind verifier [44]: every candidate selector scores *text*, so a fluent-but-unfaithful transcription is systematically preferred — the audio analog of VLM judges missing basic visual facts — which mandates acoustic-grounding controls before any gain is credited as (c)-realization, and a cap on N near the reward-hacking optimum N* [22].

### 3.5 F4 — Automatic prompt search and optimization (the H_prompt family)

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: untransferred (verified-empty) · fence: training-free · ladder: (b2), with b1 confound)*

This family is the survey's center of gravity, because it is the only literature anywhere that *quantifies* prompt-space headroom. The lineage runs APE — LLM-proposed instructions scored by execution accuracy, matching or beating human instructions on 19/24 tasks [4] — through OPRO's meta-prompt optimization, whose discovered prompts beat human prompts by up to 8% on GSM8K and up to 50% on Big-Bench Hard [5], APO's textual "gradient descent" with beam search and bandit selection (up to 31% over initial prompts) [50], the evolutionary branch EvoPrompt (up to 25% on BBH across 31 datasets) [48] and PromptBreeder's self-referential mutation [49], to GEPA, the literature's only head-to-head prompt-space-versus-weight-space measurement: reflective prompt evolution outperforms GRPO fine-tuning by 6% on average and up to 20%, at up to 35× fewer rollouts, and beats MIPROv2 [166] by more than 10% [6]. Two origin-domain caveats travel with the family: optimizer-LLMs collapse below a capability threshold, so a frozen omni model should not be assumed able to optimize itself [51], and optimizer reflections are not faithful explanations — part of any measured gain may be exploitation of unpredictable target-model behavior [52].

The VLM column is the one modality-transfer precedent, and it favored training-free: an LLM used as a black-box optimizer over CLIP prompts beats the gradient-trained soft-prompt method CoOp by +1.5% average across eleven datasets in one-shot classification [53], IPO repeats the result with human-readable prompts and better generalization [54], and evolutionary search over VLM system prompts reaches ~50% relative gains on selected reasoning benchmarks at workshop grade [55]. The imported risks are equally well documented: CoOp-style optimized prompts overfit base classes and can lose to hand prompts off-distribution [56], and the sibling schema-surface lineage — CuPL and DCLIP, which generate class descriptions and ensemble them [57][58] — is qualified by WaffleCLIP, where *random* descriptors match semantic ones: the canonical b1/b2 misattribution control [31]. Notably, absolute headroom on contrastive perception models (~1–3%) is an order of magnitude below headroom on generative reasoning tasks (8–50%).

The speech status is the sharpest negative result in the map: no closed-loop scored instruction search has ever been run on any audio-in model — verified-empty across every lane of this campaign, including adversarial re-sweeps. The nearest artifacts do not fill the cell: evolutionary prompt design exists inside an ASR pipeline, but it optimizes a *text* corrector consuming N-best lists, leaving the audio-in prompt space untouched [59]; TAP's task-activating dialogues are hand-crafted, and likewise condition a text post-processor [35]; and hand-crafted prompting of frozen Whisper improves zero-shot AVSR, code-switched ASR, and unseen-pair ST by 10–45% relative — a two-point comparison showing H_prompt − H_fix > 0 on a frozen speech model without bounding it [60].

**Transfer verdict (prompt search).** Import first a GEPA/OPRO-shaped scored instruction search run *directly on the frozen omni model*, scoring K candidates × N rollouts with verifiable speech rewards on a small labeled dev slice, labels used offline at search time only — the cheapest direct measurement of condition (b), noting that offline labeled prompt selection measures reachability, not the per-utterance label-free selector of condition (c). The biggest imported risk is WaffleCLIP-style misattribution compounded by ALICE's format finding: mandatory controls are a random/shuffled-instruction baseline at equal K [31], label-sensitivity and acoustic-grounding checks, held-out-task generalization against CoOp-style dev-slice overfitting [56], subtraction of the FormatSpread b1 floor [26], and scoring by task metric rather than model self-optimization [51]. Closure fence: a per-task offline search is in-fence; a continuously accumulating cross-session prompt-evolver collides with the closed question and is flagged, not proposed.

### 3.6 F5 — Output calibration and rescoring

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: partial (bi-encoder surfaces only) · fence: training-free · ladder: b2 for bias-correction mechanisms, (c) for selection instruments)*

Few-shot classification scores on frozen LLMs are contaminated by majority-label, recency, and common-token biases; contextual calibration corrects them content-free for up to +30% absolute [61], Batch Calibration recovers the correction from the test batch alone [62], and PMI-based domain-conditional scoring neutralizes surface-form competition among answer strings [63]. On the selection side, information-theoretic prompt selection recovers roughly 90% of the average-to-best-prompt gap without ground truth [64], and zero-label prompt selection reaches the same end via ensemble pseudo-labels [65] — an existence proof that (c)-type instruments can work on the choice surface. The convention adopted here, harmonized from the lanes, is that bias-correction mechanisms count as b2 evidence (the scoring surface, not missing task knowledge, was the obstacle to reachability) while label-free prompt selection counts toward (c) (realizability). The argument for filing calibration under b2 rather than (c): contextual and batch calibration do not *select* among candidate outputs — they re-score the choice surface so that mass the model already placed on the correct answer stops being masked by majority-label, recency, or common-token bias. What looked like missing headroom is thereby re-read as a measurement artifact of the scoring surface, which is a reachability (b) fact — the answer becomes reachable once the surface is de-biased, with no new selector introduced — not a realizability (c) one. Label-free prompt *selection* [64][65], by contrast, chooses among prompts or outputs and is therefore genuine (c) machinery; the same split sharpens CP-8, which applies calibration to the LALM choice surface as a b2 probe rather than as a selector. Audio transfer exists but only on bi-encoder (CLAP-class) sound-event surfaces: task-specific prompt ensembles [66], class-description templates [67], entropy-weighted prompt weighting [68], and transductive batch EM [69]. The cells that matter for this survey — the generative audio-LLM choice surface and speech intent classification — are empty, which is what makes this family one of the cheapest transfers named in S9.

### 3.7 F6 — In-context learning and demonstration selection

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: native, with b1 confound · fence: training-free · ladder: (b2))*

kNN retrieval of demonstrations (KATE) established that demo choice, like instruction choice, is a high-variance free variable [70]. Speech is the rare family member with *native* transfer and genuine accuracy movement: SICL applies retrieval-based demo selection to frozen Whisper, explicitly without gradient descent, cutting Chinese-dialect isolated-word WER by 32.3% relative on average and 36.4% with kNN selection [71]; TICL extends the recipe to modern multimodal LLMs via text-embedding kNN over ASR pseudo-labels, reaching up to 84.7% relative WER reduction on accented, multilingual, and children's speech [72]. The VLM failure mode is the family's standing caution: multimodal ICL is text-driven — images in demonstrations contribute little, and retrieval-based selection collapses to majority voting over context labels [73]. The audio replication already exists: in ALICE, demonstrations improve format compliance while degrading task accuracy across six LALMs [30]. The resulting screening rule is mechanical: any demo-driven lift must pass label-sensitivity and acoustic-grounding controls before being credited as b2. SICL and TICL's WER movement is content-sensitive and therefore qualifies ALICE's format-only null, but still awaits screening for lexical-overlap and acoustic-mimicry confounds.

### 3.8 F7 — Chain-of-thought, test-time compute, and modality-interleaved reasoning

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: partial · fence: training-free at the boundary; fixes gradient-trained · ladder: (b2), task-boundary-limited)*

Chain-of-thought prompting is the emblematic (b2) lever in text [74], but its boundary is now measured: across a meta-analysis of more than one hundred papers and twenty datasets, CoT helps mainly math and symbolic reasoning [75]. The VLM column sharpens the boundary into a warning triad: MME-CoT documents harmful overthinking on perception-heavy tasks [76]; the test-time-scaling limits study finds gains concentrate on multi-step reasoning while iterative refinement often degrades open-source VLMs [77]; and the "grounding paradox" names the circular dependency of long chains over perceptual input — the model must decide where to attend before it has the evidence that decision requires [80], a direct caution for long reasoning chains over audio. The audio replication is faithful: Audio-CoT helps easy and medium tasks and hurts hard ones [78]. The stronger levers in this family are out-of-fence: budget forcing presupposes reasoning-SFT [79], and the fix for textual-surrogate reasoning — chains that reason about text stand-ins rather than the signal — is gradient-trained modality-grounded distillation in Step-Audio-R1, with no training-free counterpart [81]. The strongest training-free audio positive instead *interleaves the chain with the signal*: Thinking-with-Sound alternates text reasoning with audio tool calls and gains +24.7–36.6pp absolute on perturbed emotion QA [82] — the chain earns its keep exactly when it returns to the audio, foreshadowing F10. Two clarifications keep this positive on the semantic side of the §1.4 paralinguistic fence, since its benchmark (MELD-Hard1k) is nominally an emotion task. First, the gain is *robustness-recovery of already-prompt-reachable competence* — frozen models lose over half their emotion-QA accuracy under acoustic perturbation and the tool calls restore it — not the training-free *activation* of latent paralinguistic information that §1.4's watch is defined to detect; nothing here reads speaker identity or emotion off a representation that a fixed prompt could not already reach. Second, the mechanism is *tool-augmented* (external denoising, source-separation, and spectral-analysis calls), not prompt-or-ICL-only, so it does not meet the watch's prompt/ICL-only trigger condition either; the §1.4 falsifiability clause therefore remains untriggered.

### 3.9 F8 — Self-correction and refinement

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: mislabeled-partial · fence: training-free · ladder: (c), mostly negative)*

The optimistic origin results are Self-Refine, roughly +20% average via self-feedback, concentrated on open-ended preference-shaped tasks [83]; Reflexion, which converts sparse *verifiable* reward into verbal memory across retries (HumanEval pass@1 80→91 with no weight updates) [84]; and CRITIC, where tool-grounded critique reliably improves while critique-only stagnates [85]. The corrective literature is equally clear: intrinsic self-correction without external feedback degrades reasoning accuracy (GPT-4 GSM8K 95.5→89.0 after two rounds), earlier positives were oracle-stopping artifacts, and at matched budget plain self-consistency beats multi-agent debate [86]; the TACL survey isolates the working conditions as reliable external feedback and separable detection/revision [87]; self-verification gains reappear only with external *sound* verifiers [88]; and MLLM verifiers over-validate ("agreement bias"), partially fixed by generating expectations before seeing the output — +25pp failure detection [89]. The VLM reference is VISCO: across 24 LVLMs, human critiques lift post-correction accuracy while self-critiques are less helpful and sometimes detrimental — critique, not correction, is the bottleneck — with LookBack, forced re-attention to the image before critique, the one training-free positive at up to +13.5% [90]. The field otherwise routed the failure into weights (SCL's DPO on self-correction traces) [91] or into external expert models (Woodpecker) [92].

The speech column must be read carefully: generative error correction is *not* self-correction. In the training-free / frozen GER setting a different, text-only LLM corrects the speech model's output and never hears the audio (audio-fed correction exists, but only out-of-fence as gradient-trained cross-modal GER — Whispering-LLaMA fuses Whisper-encoder features into a LLaMA corrector [167], and "Listening and Seeing Again" extends the recipe to audio-visual GER [168]); the working training-free speech fixes are verification-first pipelines (RLLM-CF's three-stage pre-detection/correction/verification framework, 9–21% relative CER/WER with GPT-4o) [93]. The named empty cell is therefore exact: no published instance exists of a frozen speech or omni model correcting its own output by re-listening to the audio.

**Transfer verdict (self-correction).** Import in order: (i) the Huang control discipline — no oracle stopping, and a matched-budget self-consistency baseline, which beat the fancier loop by 5.2pp at equal call count in the only controlled test [86]; (ii) Reflexion's regime restriction — run iterative loops only where a verifiable reward exists [84]; (iii) LookBack as the one mechanically new transferable idea: "re-listen before you rewrite," forcing the omni model to re-attend the audio to verify each claim of its own transcript before critiquing [90]. The biggest imported risk is the modality-blind critic: VISCO's failure modes predict that an omni model asked to self-correct ASR will keep its acoustic errors and fluently rewrite the text around them — precisely the over-correction documented as WER-increasing in the low-error regime — so any loop needs a do-no-harm gate under an external verifiable score, never unconstrained self-refinement. Closure flag: within-episode reflection is in-fence; Reflexion-style cross-session accumulation collides with the closed question.

### 3.10 F9 — Decoding-time interventions

*(origin: text-LLM/VLM · speech transfer: partial-to-untransferred · fence: mixed · ladder: c / b2)*

Frozen LLMs can be aligned during decoding rather than after it: ARGS scores tokens with a reward model during search (+19.56% average reward, 64.33% GPT-4 preference-or-tie) [94]; RAIN self-evaluates and rewinds with no reward model and no training [95]; DeAL frames decoding as heuristic-guided search over programmable objectives [96]. These generalize sequence-level best-of-N to token- and segment-level tilts of the frozen distribution — and none has reached speech: token/segment-level reward-guided decoding on an omni model with verifiable speech rewards (entity match, WER proxies) is an unoccupied cell, existing speech machinery being sequence-level best-of-N only. The VLM contribution is contrastive decoding against hallucination (VCD) [97], whose audio transfer exists but stops short of the perception-critical case: temporal contrastive decoding for LALMs does not yet reach ASR-under-noise [98]. The VLM lesson doubles as the bottleneck diagnosis: value-guided decoding needs a calibrated per-token guidance signal, and that signal — not the search — is what speech lacks.

### 3.11 F10 — Modality-native input scaffolds

*(origin: VLM · speech transfer: untransferred (empty cell) · fence: training-free · ladder: (b2))*

Set-of-Mark prompting is the existence proof that reachability failures can be fixed in the *input* space rather than the instruction space: overlaying segmentation marks on the image unlocks zero-shot GPT-4V grounding that beats the fully fine-tuned RefCOCOg state of the art, training-free [99]. It works because mature off-the-shelf segmenters exist to generate the marks. The audio cell is empty: no training-free acoustic-anchor scaffold — diarization or VAD segment marks, spelled-entity spans, timestamp scaffolds surfaced into the prompt — has been published for frozen audio-LLMs; the nearest neighbor is gradient-trained soft prompting through an instruction-aware audio aligner [100]. The bounding caveat imports with the idea: audio's universal segmenters (VAD, diarization) are weaker than SAM-class visual ones, so the analog is bounded by its mark generator.

### 3.12 F11 — Agentic scaffolds, single-session

*(origin: text-LLM · speech transfer: partial · fence: training-free; the VLM fix is out-of-fence · ladder: b2 / c)*

ReAct established the interleaved reason–act–observe loop as external support injection against hallucination [101]. The origin domain's mature lesson, however, is *interface over autonomy*: SWE-agent's agent–computer interface moved resolve rates from 3.8% to 12.5% by interface design alone on the same frozen model [102]; Agentless, a fixed three-phase pipeline with no autonomy, beat autonomous open-source agents at $0.70 per issue [104]; the cost-controlled Pareto critique showed simple retry baselines matching Reflexion/LDB/LATS-class scaffolds with no significant accuracy advantage, at up to ~50× lower cost (LATS; Reflexion/LDB ≈1.5×) [103]; and component stacking can interfere destructively, with lean subsets beating all-components scaffolds [105]. The VLM/GUI reference supplies the family's binding-constraint failure: OSWorld agents reach 12.24% against a human 72.36%, with the deficiency located in grounding rather than planning [106] — and the fix that worked is a gradient-trained grounding module, out-of-fence [107]. What speech inherits directly is the reward design: tau-bench's database-state checks make task success programmatically verifiable [108], tau2-bench's dual-control decomposition isolates an ~11% pure communication cost (GPT-4.1 74/56% single-control → 34% dual-control telecom) [109], and best-of-N over whole agent rollouts adds ~+8pp on GAIA in the origin domain [110]. The speech-native scaffold positive is Speech-Copilot: task decomposition and program generation over modular speech tools reaches state of the art on Dynamic-SUPERB without any training [111].

**Transfer verdict (agentic scaffolding).** Import first the cost-controlled scaffold-ablation protocol: before building any voice scaffold, run plain prompting plus best-of-N retry under the verifiable environment reward as the mandatory baseline and report the accuracy-versus-cost Pareto frontier [103] — this is simultaneously the field's verified-empty cell in speech and the cleanest instantiation of condition (c), since speech-agentic is the one family where reward equals verifiable task success. Import second the Agentless/ACI lesson — fixed dialogue decomposition plus a speech-ACI with guarded feedback and explicit confirmation subroutines [102][104] — and do not import self-reflection loops, which are matched-budget-negative at origin [86][103]. The biggest imported risk is the binding-constraint trap: if voice-agent degradation is in fact dominated by acoustic perception and turn-taking rather than agent behavior, training-free scaffolds hit the OSWorld wall, whose only known fix is gradient-trained [106][107]; the first measurement of any transfer effort must therefore re-derive the failure split between audio front-end and agent behavior under matched budget.

### 3.13 The regime rule

Read as a whole, the map supports a two-condition regime rule. Training-free methods match or beat fine-tuning when (i) an external verifiable signal or a modality-native grounding intervention exists, and (ii) the task is reasoning-shaped: GEPA against GRPO [6], Set-of-Mark against fine-tuned grounding [99], Reflexion under unit tests [84], SICL on frozen Whisper against gradient routes to the same capability [71]. They lag when intrinsic self-assessment is the bottleneck — realized best-of-N gains in text-math ride on trained verifiers [41][42], and critique fails first at perception in both VLM [90][44] and audio — or when the task itself is perception-shaped, where CoT and test-time scaling are limited in VLMs [76][77] and audio alike [78]. The semantic speech families of S4–S7 split along exactly this line: spoken-QA and speech-agentic tasks sit in the favorable regime (verifiable or near-verifiable rewards, reasoning-shaped structure, condition (c) plausibly cheapest in the agentic family), while ASR sits in the unfavorable, perception-shaped regime — a placement consistent with the in-house selector nulls [hypothesis-grade] and with the fact that the published audio test-time-compute positives appear on cognition-style rather than transcription tasks [47]. SICL and Set-of-Mark are the rule's instructive boundary cases: neither is reasoning-shaped, yet both win training-free — because in each the grounding intervention (retrieved audio–text demonstrations; segmentation marks) *substitutes* for reasoning-shape by supplying the external-signal condition (i) names, rather than satisfying the reasoning-shaped condition (ii); read the conjunction loosely on that account, or mark these as explicit boundary cases. Either way the rule is offered as **descriptive synthesis of what the cited evidence shows, not a predictive law** — Stage-2 must not treat it as one. The next four sections examine each family against this rule.

## S4 — ASR & Speech Translation (Lane L1)

The ASR/ST family is the most heavily instrumented of the four semantic task families, and for that reason it is where the survey's ladder vocabulary can be applied with the greatest precision. Thirty years of n-best and lattice research mean that condition (a) — the existence of better hypotheses inside the frozen model's sampled support — is quantified here as nowhere else; yet the two cells this survey most needs, the K-instruction prompt space and a deployable label-free reward for translation, turn out to be exactly the cells the literature has never measured. This section states the family's open problems in P0-compliant form, traces the method genealogy in the family frame of S3, positions the training-free fence against the fine-tuned ceiling, and closes with the verified-empty cells as first-class negative results.

### 4.1 Open problems

**P1 — Long-tail entity recognition** *(ladder: mixed; metric: entity-WER / NE-WER and NE-FNR, rare-word recall)*. Web-scale frozen ASR and audio-LLMs fail disproportionately on rare words and named entities: Whisper-class models have difficulty recognizing rare, domain-specific terms despite 680K hours of training audio [113], and ContextASR-Bench formalizes the deficiency for large audio-language models with entity-focused metrics (NE-WER, NE-FNR) across contextless, coarse-grained, and fine-grained context settings [112]. The strongest published fixes are gradient-trained — biasing-instruction fine-tuning reports +45.6% rare-word and +60.8% unseen-word recognition [113] — while agentic entity correction over hypothesis variants (RECOVER, 8–46% relative entity-WER) shows what orchestration can recover post hoc [34]. A fence note applies to this problem alone in the family: the solution sub-family that *accumulates a personalized bias lexicon across sessions* reduces to the closed cross-session accumulating-agent question and would require owner amendment under re-open conditions r1–r3; per-session context injection carries no such collision and remains in scope.

**P2 — Noise hallucination** *(ladder: mixed; metrics: hallucination rate, harmful-content share, label-free detection F1, insertion-dominant WER under SNR sweeps)*. Frozen ASR fabricates fluent content absent from the audio, concentrated on silence, disfluency, and noise: roughly 1% of Whisper transcriptions contain entire hallucinated phrases, 38% of which carry explicit harms, with disparate rates for aphasia speakers [114]. Label-free detection remains unsolved within the training-free fence — text-metric heuristics reach only F1 23.6%, and the best detectors are gradient-trained probes of decoder internals [115].

**P3 — The oracle-versus-deployable selection gap** *(ladder: c; metric: ρ, the realized fraction of n-best oracle WER reduction under label-free selection)*. Large oracle headroom exists in sampled and beam pools, but label-free selectors realize only a small fraction of it. Model-internal confidence is essentially exhausted: eleven CTC-internal scoring strategies yield no gain over greedy decoding distinguishable from noise at pool size G=16, with score–WER correlation degrading as pools grow [25]; self-certainty, the strongest generic text-domain selector, sits below the oracle at every N in its origin domain [24]; and HyPoradise frames the reranking upper bound that only trained correction has surpassed [23]. The in-house anchor is consistent with this picture: oracle headroom of +0.0418 WER reduction [0.0289, 0.0564] at N=8 under +5 dB SNR (mild additive noise) [scoped — one model, one condition, one fixed instruction, i.e. a measurement of H_fix, not H_prompt], against MBR realizing roughly 0–10% of it [hypothesis-grade].

**P4 — Limits of generative error correction without acoustic grounding** *(ladder: mixed; metric: signed ΔWER pre-to-post correction, especially out-of-domain and noisy; entity recall after correction)*. LLM-based generative error correction (the GER/HyPoradise line) degrades without external evidence: unconstrained zero-shot correction can *increase* WER by introducing truncation and deletion errors, and becomes safe only under N-best or lattice-constrained decoding [116][117]. This mirrors the origin-domain boundary result that intrinsic self-correction without external feedback degrades performance [86], and under acoustic noise GER quality collapses unless rescued by gradient-trained noise-aware tuning [119].

**P5 — Low-resource speech translation** *(ladder: mixed; metric: BLEU/COMET on CoVoST2 X→En low-resource pairs and FLEURS low-resource subsets, versus cascaded SOTA)*. A dedicated 2025 benchmark study concludes that SpeechLLMs do not yet redefine the state of the art against cascaded ASR+MT for low-resource pairs, where two-stage recipes remain ahead [120]; zero-resource ST is explicitly posed as open [121]; and the strongest N-best-integration fix, GenTranslate, is fine-tuned rather than training-free [122].

**P6 — ST selection blocked at the reward** *(ladder: c; metrics: correlation of reference-free QE with human judgment on speech phenomena; realized fraction of best-of-N oracle COMET/BLEU gain)*. Label-free selection for speech translation is blocked one level below the selector: reference-free QE metrics for ST systematically ignore the audio signal and fail on speech-specific phenomena [123], and the standard deployable proxy, BLASER 2.0-QE [124], though **in-fence** as an off-the-shelf utility (no *new* training, per the fence convention of §3.1), is **audio-blind** as a reward. Consequently ρ(ST) — the realized fraction of best-of-N oracle gain over ST candidate pools — is **unmeasured** (not unmeasurable): any ρ(ST) one measures would inherit that audio-blind reward's failure modes.

### 4.2 Approach genealogy

Read through the family frame of S3, the ASR/ST lane populates four method families, each carrying its origin-domain tag.

**Support expansion (family F1/F2 territory; ladder a).** HyPoradise is the canonical quantification of ASR n-best headroom — 334K+ hypothesis–transcription pairs with oracle tables framing the reranking upper bound — and its efficiently fine-tuned GER surpasses that bound by generating tokens missing from the N-best list (origin: speech; fence: gradient-trained) [23]. The in-fence counterpart is ProGRes, where a frozen instruction-tuned LLM both generates new candidate hypotheses beyond the ASR sampler's n-best — moving the pool oracle itself — and rescores them, for 5–25% relative WER improvement (origin: speech pipeline over a text-LLM mechanism; fence: training-free; ladder: a) [33]. On the ST side, GenTranslate is the (a)-evidence realized out-of-fence: an LLM fine-tuned over N-best pools outperforms direct-ST systems on FLEURS/CoVoST-2, proving the pool contains the information while leaving the prompted, training-free analog unoccupied [122]. "The omni model expands its own support" remains an empty cell in every fence.

**Conditioning (family F4/F6; ladder b2).** Task-activating prompting ports the text-LLM prompt-chaining playbook to N-best correction, reaching results competitive with domain-tuned rescoring LMs on ATIS/WSJ with a fully frozen LLM (origin: text-LLM; transfer: native; fence: training-free) [35]. SICL places k labelled audio–text pairs in frozen Whisper's context for 32.3% average relative WER reduction (36.4% with kNN example selection) on dialect ASR, explicitly without gradient descent — genuine accuracy movement from in-context conditioning (origin: text-LLM ICL; transfer: native; ladder: b2) [71]. PromptingWhisper manipulates frozen Whisper's prompt surface directly for 10–45% relative gains on zero-shot tasks including ST on unseen pairs, occasionally beating supervised systems [60] — a two-point comparison showing H_prompt − H_fix > 0 on a frozen speech model without bounding it. ContextASR-Bench makes prompt-injected entity context the live training-free lever on the entity surface, with frozen LALMs outperforming conventional ASR by a large margin when given domain- or entity-list context (origin: speech; fence: training-free; ladder: b2-leaning — label-sensitivity and acoustic-grounding controls in the yardstick's sense are absent, so the b1/b2 split is not formally certified) [112].

**Correction (family F8; ladder mixed).** The safe training-free variants are all evidence-grounded: constrained decoding over N-best or lattice evidence repairs the failure mode of free-form rewriting [116], and Ma et al.'s frozen-ChatGPT arm shows unconstrained correction can perform ASR error correction only when tethered to the N-best list [117]. The counterpoint from the trained side is instructive: compact specialized correctors outperform prompted LLM correction in the low-error regime, naming precisely where prompt-only correction fails condition (b2) [118]. RobustGER marks the gradient ceiling under noise — up to 53.9% correction improvement via language-space noise embeddings plus efficient fine-tuning — while documenting that naively injecting audio-encoder noise embeddings *harms* tuning across the modality gap [119]. No training-free method currently recovers GER quality under noise. Note that this entire column is not self-correction in the F8 sense: in the training-free / frozen setting a different, text-only LLM corrects, and the speech model never re-listens to the audio (audio-fed correction exists only out-of-fence, as gradient-trained cross-modal GER [167][168]).

**Selection (family F2/F5/F9; ladder c).** MBR with neural utility transfers natively from MT to Whisper-class ASR and ST, beating beam search in most evaluated settings, though with candidate-consensus utility and no oracle-realization measurement [39]. The frozen-LM utility swap in the CTC oracle-gap anatomy is the lone in-fence selector positive (9% relative), against eleven null internal-confidence baselines [25]. Multi-prompt MBR — the exact text-domain operationalization of H_prompt − H_fix plus a deployable selector — remains untransferred to speech [40]. Contrastive decoding, the VLM-origin hallucination lever [97], has reached frozen audio-LLM understanding benchmarks via temporal contrastive decoding [98] but stops short of ASR transcription hallucination under noise, leaving P2's training-free mitigation cell open.

### 4.3 Training-free versus fine-tuned positioning

In every sub-family the fine-tuned ceiling is documented and higher; the question the fence asks is how much of that gap prompting and selection can close without weights.

**Table 4.1 — ASR/ST positioning: fine-tuned ceiling, training-free levers, unquantified cells.**

| Sub-family | Fine-tuned ceiling | Training-free live levers | Unquantified cell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entity/long-tail (P1) | biasing-instruction fine-tuning, +45.6%/+60.8% [113] | context injection [112], SICL demos [71], agentic correction [34] | K-instruction prompt space |
| GER/correction (P4) | tuned GER above the reranking bound [23]; RobustGER under noise [119] | TAP [35], ProGRes [33], constrained decoding [116] | prompt-searched correction instructions |
| Selection (P3) | trained verifiers/QE, decoder-state probes [115] | MBR [39], frozen-LM utility [25] | ρ under multi-prompt pools [40] |
| Speech translation (P5/P6) | GenTranslate [122]; cascades on low-resource [120] | PromptingWhisper ST gains [60], Whisper ST MBR [39] | ST-selection reward [123][124]; ρ(ST) |

The training-free biasing line is thinning — recent biasing work is predominantly gradient-trained — so the lane's live levers reduce to prompt-context injection, ICL demonstrations, prompted hypothesis generation and rescoring, kNN-datastore decoding (kNN-CTC / kNN-Whisper retrieval over a training-free datastore), and MBR. Two cells receive zero quantification anywhere in the literature: the K-instruction prompt space (H_prompt − H_fix) and the ST-selection reward. The in-house numbers, cited once and only as directional anchors, sit inside this frame: oracle +0.0418 [scoped] and MBR realizing ~0–10% of it [hypothesis-grade] — small-n signals consistent with the published selector nulls, not evidence beyond them.

### 4.4 Negative findings as first-class results

Five verified-empty searches (2026-07-04) define this family's contribution to the sufficiency table in S8. First, no published application of APE/OPRO/GEPA-class automatic prompt search to WER or BLEU objectives on any frozen speech or omni model exists — H_prompt(ASR/ST) has zero published quantification. Second, multi-prompt candidate generation with MBR selection [40] has no speech instance: no study samples ASR/ST candidates across K instructions and selects label-free, so the K×N cell is unoccupied. Third, contrastive-decoding hallucination mitigation transferred from VLMs [97] to audio-LLM understanding benchmarks [98] but has no instance targeting ASR transcription hallucination under noise. Fourth, no oracle-realization measurement exists for ST — Whisper ST MBR beats beam search [39], but with consensus utility and no oracle comparison, so ρ(ST) is unmeasured rather than closed, and remains reward-blocked besides [123][124]. Fifth, re-affirmed from the archive: no gradient-free reference-free WER predictor exists anywhere in the searched literature; training-free label-free selection signals for ASR remain internal confidence (statistically null as a selector at G=16 [25]) and consensus/MBR. Each of these absences is itself a datum: for the family with the deepest oracle literature, the prompt-reachability condition (b) has never been given a number.

## S5 — SLU: Intent + Slot (Lane L2)

Spoken language understanding — mapping an utterance to an intent label and a set of typed slots — is the semantic-layer family where the gap between "the knowledge exists" and "the prompt reaches it" is most cleanly documented, and where the cheapest cross-domain instruments for closing that gap remain conspicuously untried. This section surveys the family's open problems, traces the genealogy of applicable training-free methods with their origin-domain tags, positions the training-free fence against the fine-tuned ceiling, and records the verified-empty cells as first-class negative results.

### 5.1 Open problems

**P-SLU-1 (E2E lags cascades zero-shot; ladder: mixed).** End-to-end audio-LLMs, frozen or instruction-tuned, still trail cascaded ASR→text-LLM pipelines on zero-shot semantic SLU. Speech-MASSIVE's baselines show cascaded Whisper+mT5 beating end-to-end for all twelve languages in the zero-shot condition (average intent accuracy 69.10 vs 57.07, with the same pattern on slot micro-F1) [125]; text LLMs given oracle transcripts approach supervised intent accuracy [127]; and frozen Qwen-Audio trails a gradient-trained speech-LLM by 33% relative SLU-F1 on zero-shot SLURP slot filling [126]. The training-free E2E space starts behind a cascade on this family, and every claim in it must be reported against that cascaded baseline.

**P-SLU-2 (instruction/schema sensitivity; ladder: mixed).** Frozen LALMs are severely instruction- and schema-sensitive: semantically equivalent paraphrases, output-format constraints, and task composition produce large accuracy swings on ISA-Bench [1], and AHAMask's motivating observation is that "different instructions of the same intention can yield drastically different outcomes" [3]. The sensitivity extends to outright task drift — AudioBench documents SALMONN triggering phoneme recognition or unrequested speech translation under reworded ASR prompts, and added twenty-plus prompt templates in response [2]. Crucially, the gradient fix is not free: fine-tuning Qwen2-Audio for instruction compliance "induces nontrivial catastrophic forgetting" of previously mastered tasks [1] (speech-native evidence; gradient-trained fence). This problem is the family's direct instantiation of the survey's central object, the unmeasured prompt-reachability term of condition (b).

**P-SLU-3 (unseen-schema generalization; ladder: b2).** Zero-shot generalization to unseen domains and schemas on frozen speech models sits far below both supervised systems and trained speech-LLMs: WHISMA required SLU-focused parameter-efficient fine-tuning to gain +26.6% relative zero-shot SLURP slot filling over prior SOTA, and built the SLU-GLUE benchmark precisely because unseen-domain generalization of speech-LLMs was unmeasured [126]; Dynamic-SUPERB likewise finds instruction-tuned speech models performing reasonably on seen tasks while struggling on unseen ones [129]; prompt-based zero-shot SLU studies reach the same significant-potential-with-room-to-improve verdict [128].

**P-SLU-4 (slot filling as the weak link; ladder: mixed).** Wherever prompting-only SLU approaches supervised intent accuracy, structured extraction lags: ChatGPT-class models are "worse at slot filling" and sensitive to ASR errors [127][128], frozen Qwen-Audio loses the 33% relative SLU-F1 noted above [126], and JSON output constraints degrade even strong proprietary models — ISA-Bench reports Gemini-2.5-Pro "fails under JSON-style formatting constraints" [1]. Slot filling thus couples the b1 (format-compliance) and b2 (accuracy) strands of condition (b) more tightly than any other semantic task.

**P-SLU-5 (choice-surface bias blocks label-free selection; ladder: c).** Condition-(c) selection has no validated instrument for SLU on frozen audio models. Shifting MCQ option order or rephrasing distractors changes results substantially — accuracy standard deviation up to 13.7% across MMAU/MMAR/MMSU on four LALMs, with Audio Flamingo 2 dropping from 70.6% to 42.7% ± 13.7% under distractor rephrasing alone [131]. Uncertainty estimation for audio-aware LLMs is "largely unexplored," with method effectiveness highly model- and benchmark-dependent [132], and confidence evaluation on temporal-reasoning MCQ reaches similarly cautionary conclusions [133]. Miscalibrated confidence and a bias-dominated choice surface jointly block the label-free selectors the yardstick's ρ term requires.

### 5.2 Approach genealogy

The applicable training-free machinery for this family descends almost entirely from the text-LLM and VLM origin domains (S3, families F4–F6), and its speech-transfer status is the story.

**Calibration and rescoring (text-LLM origin; untransferred; training-free).** Contextual calibration removes prompt-induced classification bias on frozen LLMs at up to +30.0% absolute while reducing variance across prompt choices [61]; Batch Calibration marginalizes contextual bias over the unlabeled test batch, inference-only, and extends to zero-shot CLIP [62]; PMI-based domain-conditional scoring shows that part of any measured zero-shot deficit is surface-form competition rather than missing task knowledge [63]. These are precisely the instruments P-SLU-2 and P-SLU-5 call for, and none has been applied to any audio-LLM or speech-intent classification. The nearest audio analog is transductive, label-free batch correction on CLAP-class bi-encoders — a TransCLIP-style EM over the test batch gaining +4.6 to +9.2 points (ESC-50 89.1→94.8; UrbanSound8K 73.8→81.8) at roughly 15 ms of CPU per 2,000 clips, though with a documented negative result where the zero-shot baseline is near chance (TUT Urban 2018) [69]. One tagging convention is worth fixing here, following the lane: bias-correction *mechanisms* (calibration, PMI rescoring) certify b2 reachability — they change which answer surfaces — while label-free *selection instruments* certify c realizability; the claims straddling this line are read under that split throughout.

**Label-free prompt selection (text-LLM origin; partial transfer; ladder c).** Choosing the template that maximizes input-output mutual information recovers 90% of the distance from average-prompt to best-prompt accuracy without ground-truth labels [64], and Zero-Label Prompt Selection achieves the same end via ensemble pseudo-labels [65]. This is the origin-domain existence proof that schema-sensitivity headroom is realizable under condition (c) — that ρ can be substantially above zero for prompt selection on classification — yet its only speech-adjacent analog is entropy-guided prompt weighting on audio bi-encoders [68].

**Schema-surface enrichment (VLM origin; partial transfer; ladder b2 with a b1 confound).** The CuPL lineage — replacing bare label names with LLM-generated class descriptions on frozen bi-encoders [57] — has landed in audio: TSPE's task-specific prompt ensembles improve zero-shot audio classification by 1.23–16.36% absolute across twelve datasets [66]; a DCASE 2024 study finds properly formatted bare labels competitive with optimized templates while LLM class descriptions reach zero-shot SOTA on ambient-sound datasets [67]; and entropy-weighted prompt combination yields label-free gains roughly five times larger than classical ensembling across a five-dataset benchmark [68]. Every one of these results, however, lives on audio-event and vocal-sound surfaces — speech-semantic intent on this class is an unoccupied cell. It is exactly the cell probed by the in-house MInDS-14 anchor, +0.126 [0.077, 0.181] [scoped], which sits in this lineage directly. And the lineage carries a mandatory control: WaffleCLIP shows that random character/word descriptors match LLM-description gains across visual classification tasks, attributing much of the lift to a non-semantic ensemble effect [31]. Until a random-descriptor ablation at equal ensemble size is run, the MInDS-14 gain remains b1/b2-unsplit [scoped] — a surface-artifact reading cannot be excluded.

**Bounding the prompt surface from above (speech-native; trained-head-on-frozen).** AHAMask trains only binary attention-head masks on a frozen LALM decoder — parameter count equal to head count, weights unchanged — and matches or beats instruction prompting on single and composite acoustic tasks, revealing functional pathways that instructions fail to trigger reliably [3]. For P-SLU-2 this is the pivotal interpretive datum: the task mass is *in* the frozen model, so the measured deficit is one of reachability and realizability, not of missing support. What AHAMask does not tell us is how much of that gap prompting alone could close — that is the H_prompt question, unmeasured here as everywhere.

**The cascaded frontier and in-context support injection (text-LLM origin; partial transfer).** The strongest in-fence baseline remains the cascade: ChatGPT-class models reach near-supervised intent accuracy zero/few-shot on oracle transcripts but stay weak on slots and degrade under real ASR errors [127][128]. Prompting a frozen LLM with word confusion networks from ASR lattices — alternative hypotheses the 1-best transcript lacks — improves ASR-robust intent classification and spoken QA, narrowing the top-1-to-oracle gap purely in-context [134]: a condition-(a) support-expansion move whose E2E analog (a frozen omni model expanding its own hypothesis support) remains unoccupied. Finally, self-consistency [36] has produced its first SLU-specific instance: SFL-MTSC applies frame-level multi-path self-consistency (domain-intent grouping plus slot-level clustering with path-support scoring) and improves slot F1 and overall accuracy over single-path inference on a multi-intent benchmark [135] — condition-(c) aggregation evidence for slots, though on the transcript side rather than a frozen E2E audio-LLM.

### 5.3 Training-free versus fine-tuned positioning

The fine-tuned ceiling is visible and higher: WHISMA's PEFT gains on zero-shot slots [126], ILLUMINER's instruction-tuned FLAN-T5 11B beating GPT-3.5 in-context learning on slot filling by 11.1–32.2 percentage points [130], and Speech-MASSIVE's trained E2E baselines [125] — with ISA-Bench's catastrophic-forgetting datum pricing the gradient route's hidden cost [1]. But the training-free fence is not competing with fine-tuned SOTA head-on. It competes with (i) the cascaded text-LLM baseline, which must be reported alongside every in-fence claim, and (ii) the unmeasured prompt-space headroom H_prompt − H_fix on frozen omni models — where the calibration, rescoring, and label-free-selection instruments of the text origin domain are cheap, task-agnostic, transferable, and verified-absent in speech. For SLU specifically, the interesting question is not whether prompting beats WHISMA; it is whether the frozen generative cell's documented deficit is largely scoring-surface artifact and schema-surface under-conditioning, both of which the imported instruments address at near-zero cost.

### 5.4 Negative findings

Five verified-empty cells, each a result in its own right (all searches 2026-07-04). First, no published pass@k or best-of-N oracle-headroom measurement exists for intent classification or slot filling on frozen generative audio-LLMs — H_fix for this family is unmeasured in the literature, matching the yardstick's prediction that condition-(a) evidence would be sparse here. Second, no application of contextual or batch calibration [61][62] to any audio-LLM or speech-intent task was located; the transductive CLAP result [69] is the nearest analog and stops at environmental sound. Third, no APE/OPRO/GEPA-class automatic prompt search (S3, family F4) has been reported on a frozen generative audio-LLM for SLU or any speech-semantic task; all located audio prompt-space work is confined to CLAP-class bi-encoders [66][67][68]. Fourth, MInDS-14 appears in no located audio-LLM benchmark suite (AudioBench, Dynamic-SUPERB, MMAU, AIR-Bench class) — the in-house anchor has no published frozen-audio-LLM baseline to compare against. Fifth, no WaffleCLIP-style random-descriptor control has been published anywhere in the audio literature — the b1/b2 split of every audio schema-surface gain, including our own, is untested. The net consequence for the ladder: condition (c) for this family currently rests entirely on cross-domain and bi-encoder evidence [64][65][68][69], and condition (b) rests on sensitivity measurements [1][2][3][131] with no headroom measurement at all. The lane's closure-fence audit is clean: none of P-SLU-1 through P-SLU-5 reduces to the closed cross-session accumulating-agent question.

## S6 — Spoken-QA & Audio Reasoning (Lane L3)

The spoken-QA and audio-reasoning family is where the survey's central question becomes sharpest, because it is the one semantic family in which the reasoning capacity of the frozen backbone is demonstrably intact while the audio interface demonstrably fails to feed it. It is also, as we show below, the family whose measurement infrastructure is most mature — shortcut-controlled benchmarks, multi-hop probes, perception-stratified test suites — and whose sufficiency ladder is nonetheless almost entirely unpopulated: no oracle headroom number, no prompt-search result, and a single deployed label-free-selector datum exist for the entire family.

### 6.1 Open problems

Six problems structure the family, and the first fixes its geometry. On Big Bench Audio — 1,000 audio questions derived from Big-Bench-Hard, so that the reasoning content is held identical across modalities — GPT-4o scores 92% on the text rendition but 66% operating natively speech-to-speech, a 26-point drop, while a training-free ASR→LLM→TTS pipeline over the same backbone shows minimal degradation [136][137] (SQA-P1). These figures are time-stamped non-archival leaderboard/blog observations (2024-12 / 2025), read here as **illustrative** of the interface deficit rather than as a verdict-carrying measurement; the same-direction finding is corroborated in the peer-reviewed record by VoiceBench's >20-point end-to-end-vs-cascade gap on spoken instructions [145] and S2SBench's intelligence-degradation quantification [153]. The deficit therefore sits at the audio interface, not in the reasoning engine; the pipeline is the training-free reference ceiling, and closing the gap natively has so far required gradient-trained audio reasoning models.

The second problem is epistemic: many published "audio reasoning" gains may not be about audio at all. Omni-R1's ablations show that much of its GRPO gain on MMAU is attributable to improved text-side reasoning — fine-tuning *without audio*, on a text-only dataset, improves audio-benchmark scores [139] — and MMAU-Pro was constructed precisely because text-only models score non-trivially on prior audio benchmarks; under its shortcut controls, text-only performance drops to 16–30% [140] (SQA-P2). The same text-over-audio bias is documented directly — LALMs prioritize contradictory text over audio evidence across audio-QA, SER, and vocal-sound tasks [171] — and per-sample audio-contribution analysis finds large silent-audio zero-contribution rates (49.8/36.6/37.8% on MMAU/MMAR/MMSU) [163]. This is the audio instance of the VLM MMStar problem, and it renders any claimed conditioning lift uninterpretable without audio-blind controls — the b2-certification burden defined in S2 lands with full weight here.

Third, reasoning fails over audio the model has already perceived correctly. SAKURA (4,000 human-verified MCQs across gender, language, emotion, and animal-sound tracks) finds that frozen LALMs answer single-hop attribute questions yet fail multi-hop questions built on the *same* attribute even when extraction succeeded — the sharpest published localization of the break at integration rather than perception, with reasoning remaining text-driven and latent audio representations left out of the chain [142]. MMAR extends the finding: every item demands multi-step reasoning, and models are weakest at the Signal layer despite its having the highest random-guess baseline [143] (SQA-P3).

Fourth, the field's default reasoning lever inverts. Chain-of-thought prompting on audio LLMs helps easy and medium items but degrades hard ones [78]; R1-AQA reports that "the explicit reasoning process has not shown significant benefits for AQA tasks" [144]; and Step-Audio-R1 names the mechanism — *textual surrogate reasoning*, chains grounded in transcripts and captions rather than acoustics, so that performance degrades as chains lengthen [81] (SQA-P4). Fifth, robustness collapses under speaker and environment variation: VoiceBench shows novel accents, environmental noise, and mispronunciations degrade voice assistants sharply, with end-to-end models trailing ASR+LLM pipelines by a margin exceeding 20 points on speech instructions [145], while HeySQuAD quantifies a 12.51% improvement in answering human-spoken questions when training includes transcribed human-spoken questions — a margin simply forfeited without it [146] (SQA-P5). Sixth, and most fundamentally for condition (a), the perceptual support itself is in question: on MMSU (5,000 triplets, 47 tasks), the best of fourteen SpeechLLMs reaches 60.68% against a best-human 89.72%, with phonology worst at 53.60%, and models *invert* the human profile — humans find perception easier than reasoning (91.24% vs 86.77%), models fail perception harder than semantic reasoning [147] (SQA-P6). Whether the fine-grained perceptual mass exists in frozen models at all is, for the weakest cells, an open question that prompting cannot answer by assumption.

### 6.2 Approach genealogy

Every training-free lever in this family descends from the text-LLM or VLM origin domains, and the transfer record is one of partial arrivals. Chain-of-thought prompting (origin: text-LLM; training-free; ladder b2) produced its origin-domain gains through pure prompting at scale [74]; its audio transfer, Audio-CoT, lifts Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct on MMAU test-mini from 55.60% to 57.80% but confines the gains to easy and medium items, with degradation on hard ones — the transfer is real and capped [78]. Self-consistency, the canonical text-LLM label-free selector (+17.9% absolute on GSM8K at origin [36]; ladder c), arrives thinner still: SC@5 adds +0.30pp over ZS-CoT (57.80 → 58.10) on the same setup [78] — a published external datum implying the realization fraction ρ > 0 for this family (a nonzero label-free selector gain exists), though its *magnitude* is unknowable until condition (a), the oracle headroom denominator, is itself measured: the +0.30pp cannot be read as "ρ small" when that denominator is UNMEASURED (§6.4-N1). It does, however, contrast with the in-house ASR prior of ρ_fix ≈ 0 [hypothesis-grade]. The first systematic audio test-time-compute study evaluates five training-free methods — CoT, temperature-based majority voting, weighted beam search, and two frozen-LLM-verifier best-of-N selectors — across five audio LLMs, reporting 9–150% relative gains with the strongest lifts from external-verifier selection on the weakest models [47]; the caveat, carried forward to S8, is that these numbers come from a small self-collected ten-participant dataset of synthetic auditory-cognition tasks, not from MMAU-class standard benchmarks.

The VLM column supplies both the warnings and the templates. MME-CoT finds CoT prompting often *degrades* multimodal performance on perception-heavy tasks — "harmful overthinking" — the cross-modal signature that Audio-CoT's hard-task degradation replicates [76]. MMStar named and metricized the modality-shortcut problem (benchmark answers derivable without the image; leakage letting a VLM beat its own text backbone image-free) and prescribed per-sample vision-indispensability curation with explicit leakage and multi-modal-gain metrics [141] — the direct template for audio-blind certification that MMAU-Pro only partially adopts at benchmark level [140]. On the selection side, LLaVA-CoT's stage-level beam search — generate candidates per reasoning stage, self-select, continue — outperforms both best-of-N and sentence-level beam search at comparable compute, with accuracy monotone in candidate count [148]; this process-level, label-free selector family (ladder c) has no published speech instance whatsoever.

The strongest published training-free accuracy movement in the family interleaves the chain with the modality itself. Thinking-with-Sound (origin: VLM "thinking-with-images"; transfer: native; training-free; ladder b2) alternates linguistic CoT with on-the-fly audio tool calls — denoising, source separation, spectral analysis — and recovers +24.73 to +36.61pp *absolute* on MELD-Hard1k, where baseline frozen models lose over half their accuracy under acoustic perturbation (Qwen2.5-Omni-7B falls 47.65% → 12.36%), with the paper reporting superlinear scaling of improvements with model size [82]. Finally, the gradient-trained fence posts: R1-AQA's GRPO on 38k samples reaches 64.5% on MMAU test-mini on the same backbone where training-free conditioning tops out at 58.10% [144]; Step-Audio-R1 fixes textual surrogate reasoning with Modality-Grounded Reasoning Distillation plus RL, surpassing Gemini 2.5 Pro across speech, sound, and music reasoning — with no training-free counterpart of acoustically grounded chains in existence [81]; and Omni-R1 deflates the apparent size of the trained advantage by showing part of it is text-shortcut movement [139].

### 6.3 Training-free versus fine-tuned positioning

The cleanest same-backbone comparison in the whole survey sits here: Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct on MMAU test-mini moves 55.60 → 58.10 under the best published training-free conditioning (ZS-CoT + SC@5) versus 64.5 under GRPO — gradient RL currently captures roughly three to four times more of the gap [78][144]. But the comparison is contaminated in a direction that favors the trained number: since text-only fine-tuning inflates "audio" scores [139] and the field compresses under MMAU-Pro's shortcut controls (best model 59.2% vs humans 77.9%) [140], the training-free-versus-trained gap *under shortcut controls* is effectively unmeasured — a live positioning opening rather than a settled defeat. The uncertainty cuts both ways, and this survey states it symmetrically: the training-free conditioning gains on the same shortcut-vulnerable MMAU test-mini (Audio-CoT's 55.60→58.10, §6.2) are *equally* uncertified under audio-blind controls (§6.4-N1), so "live positioning opening" describes the trained side's discount and the training-free side's un-discounted number alike — neither is credited here as acoustically grounded. Where training-free methods are strong, the pattern matches the S3 regime rule: system composition (the pipeline nearly erases the 26pp Big Bench Audio gap and is the more perturbation-robust architecture [136][145]), tool-augmented CoT that re-grounds the chain in the modality [82], and test-time compute on cognition-style tasks where an external frozen judge can verify [47]. Native parity on pure spoken reasoning arrived only with gradient-trained reasoning models: as of a 2026-07-04 fetch, a commercial leaderboard (time-stamped non-archival) shows Gemini 2.5 Flash Native Audio Thinking at 91% and realtime reasoning models at 97–99% on Big Bench Audio [137][138] — illustrative, not verdict-carrying, and uncorroborated in the archival record.

### 6.4 Negative findings as first-class results

Four verified-empty cells define this family's contribution to the S8 ladder table. First, no oracle pass@k / best-of-N headroom quantification exists for frozen audio LLMs on any standard audio-reasoning benchmark (MMAU, MMAR, MMSU, Big Bench Audio, MMAU-Pro) — condition (a) is absent despite the MCQ format making the measurement near-trivial; the closest published numbers are deployable-selector results, so the oracle-versus-selector decomposition is entirely missing. Second, no APE/OPRO/GEPA-class prompt or instruction search has been run on any audio LLM for this family: targeted searches returned only text-domain hits, leaving H_prompt − H_fix unquantified, consistent with the cross-family zero of S8. Third, per-sample audio-contribution filtering does exist — but for **training-data curation**: AudioMCQ measures each sample's audio contribution and reports silent-audio zero-contribution rates of 49.8/36.6/37.8% on MMAU/MMAR/MMSU [163]. What remains verified-absent is the **eval-side** per-sample certification *harness* — audio-indispensability with explicit leakage and multi-modal-gain metrics in the MMStar sense [141], applied to certify a conditioning gain — since MMAU-Pro's controls operate at benchmark level only [140] and AudioMCQ's filtering targets curation, not per-result certification (cell re-swept 2026-07-04 with "audio contribution" / "silent audio" queries; the DCASE-2026 audio-dependent-QA task further erodes the emptiness). Fourth, the VLM process-level selection family (stage-level beam search, step-wise verifier selection over chains [148]) is untransferred to audio. The compensating asset is equally concrete: MMAU-Pro's shortcut-controlled construction is the ready-made b1/b2 splitter that this family uniquely offers — any future training-free conditioning gain here can be certified acoustically grounded at lower instrumentation cost than in any other family in this survey.

## S7 — Speech-Agentic Tasks: Voice Tool Use, Dialog State, and Verifiable Task Completion (Lane L4)

The speech-agentic family — voice tool use, dialog-state instruction following, and end-to-end task completion over audio — is the youngest of the four semantic families surveyed here, and for this survey's purposes the most diagnostically sharp. Two properties set it apart. First, every headline number in its literature is already a measurement of a *frozen* model under a prompt scaffold: the evaluated agents are frozen commercial speech-to-speech systems driven by system prompts and tool schemas, so the entire published record sits inside the training-free fence by construction. Second, it is the only family in which a label-free verifiable reward — database-state assertion inherited from the text-agent lineage — existed *before* any selection research began, making the yardstick's condition (c) the cheapest to instrument of all four families, even as condition (a) has never been measured at all. This section surveys the family's five documented open problems, traces its method genealogy (almost entirely text-LLM in origin, with the VLM/GUI-agent lineage as the nearest cross-domain precedent), positions the training-free frontier against the gradient-trained one, and closes with the lane's negative findings and closure-fence audit.

### 7.1 Open problems

**P1 — Voice task-completion collapse** (ladder: mixed). Full-duplex voice agents complete far fewer verifiable-reward customer-service tasks than same-generation text agents. On tau-Voice, the text state of the art (GPT-5 with reasoning) reaches 85% pass@1, while voice agents score 31–51% under clean audio and 26–38% under realistic audio — a retention of only 30–45% of text capability — with success deterministically evaluated by comparing end-of-episode database state against a gold standard [149]. The decisive diagnostic is *where* the failures sit: 79–90% are agent-driven rather than transcription-driven — logical errors despite accurate transcription, hallucinated tool completions ("I've updated your shipping address" without any tool call issued), and unresponsive silence — and accents alone cost up to 18 points for some providers while leaving others nearly unaffected [149]. Full-Duplex-Bench-v3 reports the same collapse under real-world disfluency (best Pass@1 of 0.600) [150], and the tau2 voice extension packages the environment class for reproduction [151]. The deficit, in other words, lives in the agent loop, not merely at the ASR front-end.

**P2 — Spoken instruction-following and reasoning gap** (ladder: b2). End-to-end speech models lose instruction-following and reasoning capability relative to their *own* text backbones: GPT-4o drops from 92% (text) to 66% (speech-to-speech) on Big Bench Audio [136]; URO-Bench finds open-source spoken dialogue models lag their backbone LLMs in instruction-following and suffer catastrophic forgetting [152]; S2SBench quantifies the same intelligence degradation systematically [153]. VoiceBench adds the robustness dimension — speaker, environment, and content variation degrade all voice assistants, while a naive ASR cascade outperforms all open-source end-to-end models by more than 20 points on spoken instructions [145].

**P3 — Dialog state under interruption** (ladder: b2; strictly within-session — see the fence audit in §7.4). Voice agents cannot maintain and update dialog state under full-duplex phenomena. On EchoChain's 200 interrupted conversations no evaluated model exceeds a 50% pass rate (best 47.5%), and a paired half-duplex control removes 40.2% of failures — causally isolating state-update-under-interruption, rather than task difficulty, as the deficit, with named failure patterns of contextual inertia, interruption amnesia, and objective displacement [154]. Full-Duplex-Bench-v3 identifies self-correction handling and multi-step reasoning under disfluency as the most consistent failure modes [150], and Audio MultiChallenge's best system passes only 54.65% of its instance-specific rubrics, with Self-Coherence degrading as audio context grows [155]. The spoken dialog-state-tracking lineage this problem extends traces to SpokenWOZ, whose best dialog-state tracker reached only 25.65% joint goal accuracy on real human spoken conversations [170]. All of this state is within-conversation; no cross-session accumulation is involved.

**P4 — Spoken argument-value fidelity** (ladder: mixed). Tool-call argument values are corrupted by the speech channel itself. Annotation-preserving text-to-voice conversion of tool benchmarks costs 1.8–4.8 points on Confetti, with "misunderstandings of argument values in the speech" the dominant failure mode [156]; the best ASR–LLM pipelines reach only 60.6% average parameter-filling accuracy in English, with sharper degradation in Indic languages [157]; Audio2Tool shows tool use degrading markedly under combined compositional and acoustic challenge [158]. tau-Voice localizes the failure at name-spelling and authentication turns — agents fail to transcribe names and emails even when spelled letter-by-letter, blocking all downstream actions [149].

**P5 — Speech-robust verifiable reward** (ladder: c). The family's reward is only *half*-verifiable. The environment half — database-state assertion — transfers natively from tau-bench [108][149]. The communicate-to-user half must be judged from speech, and prompted (training-free) audio judges measure weak: they lag human judgment by 32 percentage points on average, with severe calibration failures precisely in tie cases where the correct decision is to abstain [159]; pointwise numeric scoring shows high MSE (roughly 1.8–3.5 across datasets), leading AudioJudge to recommend pairwise-only protocols [12]; and a prompted Qwen2.5-Omni reaches only 53.4% objective accuracy as a dialogue evaluator [46]. Any selection or reranking method aimed at the spoken half would therefore be optimizing an unreliable reward — a condition-(c) obstruction distinct from those in S4–S6.

### 7.2 Approach genealogy

The family's method stack descends almost entirely from the text-LLM origin domain. The reward design comes from tau-bench (origin: text-LLM; transfer: native; fence: training-free): task success verified by comparing database state to an annotated goal state, plus the pass^k reliability metric under which frontier function-calling agents succeed on fewer than half of tasks and drop below 25% pass^8 on retail [108]. tau-Voice transplants this environment to full-duplex audio unchanged — the verifiable reward is the one component that crossed the modality boundary intact [149].

The scaffold that created the agent class under test is ReAct (origin: text-LLM; transfer: partial; ladder: b2): interleaved reasoning and acting via prompting alone, worth +34 points on ALFWorld and +10 on WebShop from one or two in-context examples and no gradient updates [101]. Voice agents inherit ReAct-style loops only through the cascade — the loop runs over ASR transcripts; no audio-native, audio-token ReAct evaluation has surfaced. Indeed, the largest measured training-free lift in the family is precisely **cascade re-grounding** (origin: speech; ladder: b2), which this survey reads as the family's functional analog of Set-of-Mark: surfacing the modality as text tokens makes the task prompt-reachable. A Whisper→GPT-4o→TTS pipeline holds near text parity (~92%) where native S2S GPT-4o scores 66% on Big Bench Audio [136]; the naive cascade beats all open-source end-to-end assistants by over 20 points on spoken instructions [145] and leads agentic parameter filling at 60.6% [157] — at a latency price (10.12 s versus roughly 4–5 s native) [150].

The nearest cross-domain precedent for the deficit itself is the VLM/GUI-agent lineage. Prompt-scaffolded multimodal agents opened at 12.24% on OSWorld against a 72.36% human baseline [106] and reached the low 70s by late 2025 via grounding scaffolds and multi-model rollout ensembles; the emblematic training-free move was Set-of-Mark prompting, which let zero-shot GPT-4V outperform the fully fine-tuned referring-segmentation state of the art [99]. But the lineage also carries the imported risk: the binding constraint (visual grounding) was ultimately resolved by a gradient-trained grounding module [107], a caution that a modality-specific bottleneck may not be prompt-reachable. No audio-native analog of mark-injection exists (negative finding N4 below); voice agents sit at the "12% stage" with only the full cascade available as a re-grounding scaffold.

For prompt-space headroom, the origin-domain evidence is GEPA-class reflective prompt evolution, which outperforms GRPO weight training by 6% on average (up to 20%) at up to 35× fewer rollouts [6] — yet the speech-side transfer consists of exactly one non-peer-reviewed engineering demo, an APE loop over a commercial voice assistant's function-calling prompt scored against TTS audio test suites [161]. Two further genealogy lines are native positives: Speech-Copilot carries the task-decomposition/program-generation scaffold (HuggingGPT/code-as-policy class, text-LLM origin) natively into speech, reaching state-of-the-art results on Dynamic-SUPERB without any training [111]; and the From-Text-to-Voice framework converts any annotated text tool benchmark to audio while preserving the original annotations, with open-source judges of at least 8B parameters exceeding 80% agreement with proprietary judges [156] — the instrument that makes condition (c) cheapest to stand up in this family. The trained frontier frames the ceiling: leaderboards as of 2026-07-04 (time-stamped non-archival) show native S2S reasoning largely catching up to the pipeline on single-turn spoken reasoning [138], Step-Audio 2 trains reasoning-centric RL with native tool calling end-to-end [160], and WavReward's gradient-trained evaluator lifts spoken-dialogue judging from 53.4% to 91.5% objective accuracy [46] — pricing what any prompted judge must chase, while remaining out-of-fence evidence.

### 7.3 Training-free versus fine-tuned positioning

The positioning peculiar to this family is that the training-free fence is not a methodological choice but the published condition of measurement: every leaderboard number already reflects a frozen model under a prompt scaffold. Gradient training has visibly closed the *single-turn* spoken-reasoning gap — the newest realtime models score in the 80s and 90s on Big Bench Audio (time-stamped non-archival leaderboard [138]) where their predecessors scored 66% — but the 2026 *agentic* deficits (tau-Voice's 26–51% against 85% text with 79–90% agent-driven failures; EchoChain's sub-50% pass under interruption; FDB-v3's 0.600 [149][154][150]) persist in exactly those newest models. And those residual deficits are unmeasured for sampling and prompt-space headroom: the yardstick's (a) and (b) fractions have never been computed on any voice-agent benchmark. Any future scaffold work here should import the text domain's cost-controlled evaluation discipline — jointly optimizing accuracy and cost, against the finding that simple baselines can dominate elaborate scaffolds at far lower cost [103][104] — as the mandatory baseline protocol. The corresponding measurement proposal appears as CP-4 in S9, and the family's UNMEASURED cells are entered as such in the S8 ladder table.

### 7.4 Negative findings and the closure-fence audit

The lane's verified-empty searches are first-class results. **N1:** no published pass@k, best-of-N, or oracle-over-sampling measurement exists on any voice-agent benchmark — tau-Voice explicitly reports only pass@1 [149] — so condition (a) is an unoccupied measurement cell, exactly as the yardstick predicted for this family; the best-of-N-over-agent-rollouts recipe surveyed in S3 (F11) remains text-only. **N2:** no peer-reviewed APE/OPRO/GEPA-class prompt optimization on any voice-agent or spoken-instruction benchmark; the closest artifact is the engineering demo above [161]. H_prompt − H_fix for this family has zero published quantification. **N3:** no self-consistency or majority-vote selection over ReAct-style voice-agent trajectories — and even the text origin domain describes consensus over open-ended agent trajectories as underexplored (lane provenance: arXiv 2310.16340, listed in the pending-verification annex), so the speech transfer lacks an origin-complete recipe to copy. **N4:** no audio-native Set-of-Mark analog — no training-free scaffold surfacing acoustic anchors (time-aligned events, speaker marks, spelled-entity spans) as in-context tokens (absence-of-evidence grade, two-search depth); CP-7 in S9 targets this cell.

**N5 — closure-fence audit (kept where the lane flagged it).** The cross-session accumulating-agent question is closed, not merely deferred: "The agent-level program this paper deferred is now closed under the frozen contract — by frozen procedure plus one genuine kill — rather than by scoping... the question is therefore closed, not merely deferred, unless re-open conditions r1–r3 obtain" (2026-07-03 NO-GO decision §10; owner-ratified 2026-07-04). The re-open conditions are: **r1** — a public cross-session, same-speaker speech corpus appears; **r2** — a peer-reviewed non-separable decomposition bound appears; **r3** — a mechanism-lane kill is overturned by new literature. The lane's audit is clean: all five problems above are single-episode, single-session task deficiencies; P3's dialog state is within-conversation by construction; no candidate drawn from this family requires skill or memory accumulation across episodes; and tau2-class environments do not satisfy r1 (synthetic personas, no cross-session same-speaker audio corpus) [151]. Nothing in this section re-enters the closed question.

Read column-wise, the table says something no single lane could. Condition (a) is deeply measured exactly once — ASR, where three decades of n-best analysis plus HyPoradise [23] settle that sampled pools carry real oracle mass — and unmeasured everywhere else, including the two families (SQA with its MCQ format, speech-agentic with its programmatic reward) where measuring it would be almost mechanical. Condition (b2) has genuine speech-native positives (SICL, TICL, PromptingWhisper, Thinking-with-Sound, the cascade), but almost none of them passes, or was ever subjected to, the certification controls the yardstick requires: the random-descriptor control has never been run in audio [31], per-sample audio-indispensability has never been curated [141], and ALICE stands as the only audio study that split format from accuracy at all [30]. Condition (c) shows the sharpest cross-family asymmetry: for ASR the selectors are measured and mostly null (with one 9% relative in-fence exception [25]), for SLU and SQA the evidence is thin-to-absent, and for speech-agentic the selection instrument — a verifiable environment reward — already exists while nobody has yet sampled more than once against it.

### 8.2 The H_prompt − H_fix cell: doubly zero at survey time

The survey's center of gravity — the prompt-space contribution H_prompt − H_fix, the direct operationalization of the Stage-1 question — was empty from both directions when this survey was drafted. In-house it was zero by construction: the C1 best-of-N pipeline ran one fixed instruction end-to-end, so the +0.0418 anchor [scoped] measures H_fix and nothing more; the instruct-prompt space had never been explored in our own repository until the single pre-authorized probe of §8.3, whose n=50, single-condition reading is graded directional-only and cannot fill a published-quantification hole (re-grade doc §1.3). In the literature its *magnitude* is zero by verified absence — though its existence is not: PromptingWhisper [60] already shows H_prompt − H_fix > 0 on a frozen audio-in model (10–45% relative from prompt manipulation alone, §4.2), a two-point existence positive that bounds nothing. Five lanes (L1–L4, X1) independently searched for any APE/OPRO/GEPA-class instruction search, any max-over-K oracle, or any FormatSpread-style spread interval on an audio-in model, and all returned empty, with the adversarial re-sweep in X1 returning empty again. The hole is corroborated from the optimization field's own side: the systematic survey of automatic prompt optimization covers no audio domain [7], and the field's modality expansion (MPO) reaches images, videos, and molecules while leaving audio untouched in both experiments and framing [8]. The one indirect positive is instructive rather than conclusive: a gradient-trained prompt projector improves LLM-based ASR by learning to move prompt embeddings into "more effective regions of the LLM input space" [162] — better prompt regions therefore exist on frozen speech backbones, but they have only ever been reached by a trained projection, never by searched discrete prompts. Between the sensitivity literature (which proves the prompt surface moves outcomes, sometimes violently [1][2]) and the projection result (which implies better points exist), the exploitable maximum sits unmeasured — precisely the gap between ISA-Bench and the APE/OPRO line that X1 names as the field's unoccupied cell.

### 8.3 The in-house directional probe — the [PROBE-PENDING] slot, filled

The yardstick memo pre-authorizes exactly one Stage-1 measurement against this cell, and this subsection is its reserved slot. The probe as scoped: K instructions × small N on MInDS-14 or LibriSpeech; at most half a GPU-day; at most 200 items; single-touch (no iteration on the result); its output graded `directional-only` on arrival, whatever it shows. The survey adds five design constraints, each imported from a documented failure mode. (i) Prompts must be scored by the task metric (WER / intent accuracy), not by the model optimizing itself — optimizer-LLMs collapse below a capability threshold [51]. (ii) The b1 floor must be subtracted: a FormatSpread-style spread over semantically equivalent formats is the baseline against which any max-over-K gain is read [26]. (iii) A WaffleCLIP-style random-instruction control at equal K is mandatory before any gain is credited past b1 [31]. (iv) A matched-budget self-consistency baseline is mandatory before any gain is credited to prompt search rather than to sampling, following the Huang control discipline [86]. (v) Estimation under the 200-item budget should reuse PromptEval-class machinery, which is published, consistent, and task-agnostic [28].

**Probe result (slot filled at assembly, 2026-07-04).** The budget-matched probe (8 task-definition instructions × 4 samples vs 1 fixed instruction × 32 samples — one shared 32-sample budget; n=50 fresh LibriSpeech test-other utterances, +5 dB SNR (mild additive noise), frozen Qwen3-Omni-30B; artifact `_repro/probe_hprompt_vs_hfix.json`, mini-prereg pre-committed at bae2184) measured a fixed-arm oracle headroom 0.0530 (1×32) vs a prompt-arm oracle headroom 0.0516 (8×4) — a matched-budget diversity contrast **Δ_BM = −0.00137** (descriptive CI [−0.0126, +0.0095], spans zero, slightly favoring the FIXED arm). This is Δ_BM, *not* the budget-unconstrained H_prompt − H_fix of §2.2, which the probe's equal-budget design cannot estimate. The fixed instruction produced the best candidate on 33/50 utterances; and the MBR readout weakly reversed (prompt-arm MBR 0.0884 vs fix-arm 0.0929, delta +0.0045, CI spans zero). **[directional-only | n=50 | single-touch | not significance-bearing]**

Directional reading: for ASR at this matched budget the result is uninformative-to-weakly-fix-favoring — the two readouts disagree in sign (oracle Δ_BM −0.00137; MBR +0.0045) and both descriptive CIs span zero, and the eight instructions are an unvalidated, undescribed sample of the prompt space (constraint (v) below, unrun). It measures Δ_BM at a tiny budget, not the nested-pool H_prompt − H_fix, and it cannot rank problems or kill hypotheses. Any program-steering reading — e.g. that effort might shift toward schema-rich tasks (SLU/SQA) and the (c) realization gap — is offered only as a recommendation for the owner discussion (the §9.3 "one input among the rest" register), not as a finding of this probe. Two design notes bound the reading further. The probe instantiates constraint (iv) by construction — the two arms share one 32-sample budget, so any prompt-arm gain would have been read against a matched sampling baseline — while constraints (ii), (iii), and (v) (the b1-floor subtraction, the random-instruction control at equal K, and PromptEval-class estimation) remain unrun and are owed by any Stage-2 measurement; this is part of why the result carries the grade it does. The table cell this probe fills is the ASR b2 house line of Table 8.1; nothing in S9 depends on its direction, and per the Stage-1 rule it decides nothing.

### 8.4 What remains unmeasured after this survey

Enumerated as named negative results, each with its certifying lane: H_prompt for every family in the published literature (X1; L1–L4) — the §8.3 probe adds a single directional-only in-house ASR reading, not a measurement; H_fix for SLU and speech-agentic (L2, L4/N1); ρ(ST), blocked upstream because reference-free ST rewards ignore the audio and the deployable proxy is a trained head [123][124] (L1); ρ(SQA) beyond the single SC@5 datum [78] (L3); the b1/b2 split of every audio schema-surface gain, ours included, pending the never-run random-descriptor control [31] (L2); per-sample audio-indispensability and leakage metrics in the MMStar sense [141][140] (L3); any audio critique/correction benchmark in the VISCO sense [90] (X3); and the cost-controlled voice-scaffold ablation, the Kapoor-style Pareto analysis with zero speech instantiation [103] (3W-agentic).

### 8.5 What this survey can and cannot conclude

The literature bounds two of the three fractions usefully. For (a), where measured, support is real and substantial, and the origin-domain theory (coverage growing log-linearly while label-free selection plateaus [17]) gives the expected shape of the unmeasured cells. For (c), the evidence is coherent across domains: realization rides on external, verifiable, or at least modality-grounded signals, and collapses when the model must assess itself — the house ASR nulls [hypothesis-grade] are consistent with that pattern, not independent proof of it. For (b), the survey can conclude nothing quantitative, because condition (b) has no theorem (S2) and, for audio-in models, no published measurement: the only honest statement is that prompt-space headroom on frozen omni speech models is an empirical parameter that no published work has estimated — the §8.3 probe contributes one directional-only, single-condition ASR reading, and nothing more. The sufficiency question is therefore answerable but unanswered. Every instrument required to answer it exists, is training-free, and is published in an adjacent domain; the path is the yardstick's definitions, the directional probe above, and a pre-registered Stage-2 measurement. No sufficiency verdict is issued here, and per the Stage-1 rule none could be: the section that follows recommends candidate problems; it decides nothing.

## S9 — Open Problems & Candidate Research Problems

### 9.1 Preamble (binding)

The candidates below are drafted from the lanes' P0-compliant open problems and the transfer verdicts of the four cross-domain comparisons. They are presented **unranked**: ranking, selection, and any commitment of Stage-2 resources belong to the owner's problem-definition discussion, not to this survey. Each candidate carries a statement, its ladder placement, the closest published prior and the distance to it, the lane problems it feeds from, and a fence/closure note where relevant. One flagging rule applies throughout (S2): any variant that reduces to a cross-session accumulating agent collides with the closed NO-GO question and is labeled as such rather than silently included. One usage note prevents a misreading: where this survey calls the H_prompt − H_fix measurement its "center of gravity" (§1.3, §2.2, §3.5, §8.2), that phrase marks the *motivational* gap the whole survey is organized around — the single empty cell that made the survey necessary — and is explicitly **not** a ranking of CP-1 above the other candidates, which remain unordered.

### 9.2 The candidates

**CP-1 — Quantify H_prompt − H_fix on a frozen omni model (ASR + SLU + SQA).** Run a K-instruction scored search — APE/OPRO/GEPA-shaped [4][5][6] — against WER, intent accuracy, and MCQ exact-match on a frozen omni backbone, reporting the max-over-K oracle alongside the fixed-instruction baseline, with PromptEval/FormatSpread estimation under budget [28][26] and the mandatory b1 controls (random-instruction arm at equal K; format-spread floor; ALICE-style format-vs-accuracy split) [31][30]. Ladder: (b), split b1/b2 by construction. Closest prior and distance: the quantification exists only in text [5][6]; MPO stops one modality short of audio [8]; the speech cell is verified-empty across four lanes — so the distance is not methodological but simply that nobody has run it. Feeds from: P-X1-3, PR-X3-4, P-SLU-2. Fence note: a per-task offline search is in-fence; a continuously accumulating cross-session prompt-evolver collides with the closure — flagged, not proposed.

**CP-2 — Multi-prompt candidate pools for ASR/ST with label-free selection.** Transfer multi-prompt MBR [40] to frozen omni ASR/ST: sample candidates across K instructions × N rollouts, select label-free, and report both the pool-oracle movement (does the K-dimension enrich support?) and the realized fraction. This measures the K×N cell and condition (c) jointly, in the one family where MBR machinery is already speech-native [39]. Ladder: (b) × (c) joint. Closest prior: the text-domain operationalization itself [40], untransferred; Whisper MBR [39] as the single-prompt baseline. Feeds from: L1:P3, L1:P6, L1-C08.

**CP-3 — Selector/utility anatomy on frozen omni pools: measure ρ(ASR) properly.** Over identical stored candidate pools, compare self-certainty [24], the frozen-LM MBR utility swap (the lone in-fence speech positive, 9% rel.) [25], and overlap-MBR, against the pool oracle. The house prior ρ_fix ≈ 0 [hypothesis-grade] is the datum to test, not to assume; the tension between the Whisper-MBR positive [39] and the omni-pool null is itself unexplained and may be pool-geometry, not selector, driven. Ladder: (c). Closest prior: [25] (CTC pools, not omni-instruct pools); self-certainty has no speech instance. Feeds from: L1:P3, PR-X3-6, the 3W-BoN transfer verdict.

**CP-4 — Condition-(a) + (c) instrumentation for voice agents: pass@k and verifiable-reward best-of-N on τ-Voice-class environments.** Sample N full agent rollouts per task, report pass@k (the family's first H_fix number), then select by the environment's DB-state reward — label-free inside the env by construction [108][149][156]. The origin recipe is concrete (BoN over agent rollouts, ~+8pp GAIA, list-wise verification best [110]); the mandatory baseline is the cost-controlled scaffold-ablation protocol, i.e. plain prompting plus retries on the accuracy-vs-cost Pareto frontier [103]. Ladder: (a) measurement plus (c) realization. Closest prior: pass@1-only reporting [149]; N1/N2 verified-empty. Feeds from: L4:P1, L4:P5, L4-C06/C07/C14, the 3W-agentic verdict.

**CP-5 — An audio-blind b1/b2 certification harness for spoken-QA conditioning gains.** Build per-sample audio-indispensability and leakage metrics in the MMStar sense [141] on top of MMAU-Pro's benchmark-level shortcut controls [140], and apply the harness to any training-free conditioning result (CoT variants, demo selection, instruction search) before its gain is credited b2. Ladder: (b)-split instrumentation rather than a method. Closest prior: [140][141][163]; per-sample audio-contribution filtering exists for **training-data curation** [163], but the **eval-side** per-sample certification harness (leakage + multi-modal-gain metrics, MMStar-style) remains verified-absent. Feeds from: SQA-P2, L3-C06/C14. This family uniquely offers the ready-made splitter; the harness would also certify CP-1's SQA arm.

**CP-6 — "Re-listen before you rewrite": acoustically grounded self-verification on a frozen omni model.** Transfer LookBack [90]: before critiquing or correcting its own transcript/answer, the model re-attends the audio and verifies each claim against it, under a do-no-harm gate (accept a correction only when an external verifiable score does not worsen) and a matched-budget consensus baseline per the Huang discipline [86]. This is the one VLM-column self-correction mechanism with a positive training-free delta (+13.5%) against the perception-critique failure mode, and it directly operationalizes b2 acoustic-grounding certification. Ladder: (b2)/(c). Closest prior: [90] (VLM); the entire speech correction column delegates to text LLMs that never hear the signal (3W negative), with verification-first ASR pipelines the nearest working shape [93]. Feeds from: L1:P4, PR-X3-1/2.

**CP-7 — An audio-native Set-of-Mark: acoustic anchors as in-context tokens.** Surface diarization/VAD segment marks, spelled-entity spans, and timestamp scaffolds into the prompt of a frozen omni model, for long-audio SQA and voice-agent argument-value fidelity — the audio analog of the input-space intervention that let zero-shot GPT-4V beat fine-tuned grounding SOTA [99]. Ladder: (b2) input-space conditioning. Closest prior: [99] (VLM); N4 verified-empty in audio; the only re-grounding scaffold in measured use is the full cascade [136][145], which pays ~10s latency for its gains. Caveat carried from X3: audio's weaker universal segmenters bound how far the analog can go. Feeds from: L4:P4, PR-X3-3, X3-14.

**CP-8 — Calibration and PMI rescoring on the frozen audio-LLM choice surface.** Apply contextual calibration, Batch Calibration, and domain-conditional PMI scoring [61][62][63] to LALM MCQ/intent surfaces, against the documented distractor-rephrasing sensitivity (accuracy std up to 13.7%) [131]. The measurable question: how much of the SLU/MCQ deficit is scoring-surface artifact rather than missing task knowledge? These are the cheapest instruments in the paper — inference-only, label-free, no prompt changes. Ladder: (b2)/(c) on the choice surface. Closest prior: text-only [61][62][63], with PromptBoosting's K-prompt classification ensembling [169] as the nearest origin-domain shape to this SLU choice-surface setting; transfer verified-empty. Feeds from: P-SLU-2, P-SLU-5, L2-C1/C2/C11.

### 9.3 How the candidates route back through the yardstick

The failure-routing table of S2 makes each candidate's possible outcomes productive rather than terminal, and it is worth closing on how. If CP-1 or CP-4 finds large prompt-space headroom that the controls certify as b2, the sufficiency question tilts toward "the space is richer than one instruction reveals," and selector candidates (CP-2, CP-3, the (c) half of CP-4) would be *one natural next focus* — a (c)-failure world, opening selector research — though which candidate the owner prioritizes is not fixed by this routing. If instead the probes find headroom that is real at the oracle but format-dominated under the CP-5/CP-1 controls, the field's b1/b2 confound is the finding, and instrumentation (CP-5, CP-8) leads. If support itself measures thin — CP-4's pass@k flat in N, or SLU H_fix near zero once measured — that is an (a)-failure, and only then does the agentic question legitimately re-enter, as the yardstick's routing prescribes: external support injection, per-session context and hypothesis generation first [33][34][112], with "the omni expands its own support" the unoccupied cell any such work would aim at. That re-entry is conditional and fence-respecting — every solution family cited here is single-session, and the one variant that is not (the accumulating prompt-evolver under CP-1) is flagged for owner amendment rather than proposed. Nothing above is ranked; the ordering of effort across CP-1–CP-8 is a decision this survey hands to the owner discussion with the definitions fixed, the empty cells named, and the §8.3 directional probe — spent on ASR at matched budget, graded directional-only — on the table as one input among the rest.
