dimension vs control principle vs integration layer); (2) survey section from the dossier, with
occupied-surface map and open steps; (3) candidate instantiations (plural, unlocked — no

watchlist + the DFS four-question procedure for absorbing new work during Stage-2; (8) modality
wording rule: instantiate as speech/audio-omni under H5 `WITHHOLD` — no cross-modality load-bearing
claims.
